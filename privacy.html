Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. NexusAI by Kulbeer ("we", "us") operates https://kulbeer191.github.io/kulbeer-nexusai/ (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect and what we do with it — in plain language.

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you use the sponsorship inquiry form on our Sponsor page, you provide your name, work email, company name, tool URL, package choice, budget, and an optional message. That submission is sent to us by email (via Web3Forms, our form-delivery service) so we can reply to your inquiry. We use it only to respond to you and to manage the sponsorship relationship.

Information stored on your device. If you upvote a tool or save tools on the homepage, your choices are stored in your own browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

Information we do not collect. We do not run user accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics that identify you. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

How the Site is hosted

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages requested) as part of operating its hosting service, under GitHub's own privacy policy. We do not receive or use those logs to identify visitors.

Third-party services

  • Web3Forms delivers sponsorship form submissions to our email inbox. It processes the form fields you submit and nothing else.
  • Google Search Console helps us see how the Site appears in Google Search. It provides aggregate search data only and does not identify individual visitors.
  • Outbound links. Review pages link to the official websites of the tools we cover. Those sites have their own privacy policies; we are not responsible for them.

Cookies

The Site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage is used only for the upvote and saved-tools features described above, and only on your device.

Children

The Site is intended for adults evaluating AI tools for work. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove your saved upvotes and saved tools. To ask us to delete an inquiry email you sent, or for any privacy question, email ukusa299@gmail.com and we will handle it.
