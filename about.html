About NexusAI by Kulbeer

An independent AI research and side-by-side comparison platform, built for US founders, operators, and builders who need to pick the right AI tool — with real pricing, real trade-offs, and no hype.

What NexusAI is

NexusAI by Kulbeer is a curated directory of AI tools and autonomous agents. Every listing is a full review: what the tool does, who it is best for, verified pricing, honest pros and cons, and a side-by-side comparison with the closest alternatives. There are no paid rankings and no scraped listings — every page is written and checked by hand.

Who builds it

NexusAI was founded by Kulbeer, an independent builder who got tired of AI tool lists that read like press releases. The site is a one-person operation: research, testing, writing, and publishing are all done by the founder, which is exactly why the reviews can say no when a tool does not deserve a yes.

How we test and review

Our methodology is simple and public:

  • Official sources first. Pricing, plans, and features are checked on the tool's own website. If a price cannot be verified on the official site, the page says so and tells you to confirm it there — we never invent a number.
  • Side-by-side, not in isolation. Tools are compared against their real competitors on pricing, limits, and best-fit use cases, so you can see the trade-off instead of a sales pitch.
  • Pros and cons, always. Every review lists what is genuinely good and what to watch out for, including pricing cliffs, missing free plans, and features that changed in 2026.
  • Updated in batches. New tools are added in verified daily batches, and pricing is re-checked when a page is touched. Prices change — always confirm on the official site before buying.

Editorial independence

Sponsorship never buys a positive review. Companies can pay for placement — a Featured Tool Listing ($299/month), a Dedicated Deep-Dive Review ($499 one-time), or a Site-Wide Header Banner ($799/month) — and paid placement is always clearly labeled "Sponsored". Featured buys placement for a review page; Dedicated buys the review itself, written for you. Neither buys a rating, a ranking, or a kind word we do not mean. See our Sponsor page for the full packages.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, run ads, or publish paid rankings. We do not list tools we have not researched. And we do not pretend a tool is free when it only offers a trial.
