Notion AI Review 2026

Write, summarise and organise inside Notion. Great for teams already living in docs, wikis and project tracking.

Overview

Teams in Notion already keep their docs, wikis and projects there — but finding answers and writing updates still takes hours. Notion AI adds writing assistance, workspace search, AI Meeting Notes and an agent that works across your pages, so the knowledge you already have turns into drafts, summaries and answers without leaving Notion.

Official site: https://www.notion.so/product/ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Lives inside the workspace your team already uses
  • Full AI suite on Business: writing, search, meeting notes, agent
  • Strong for wikis, docs and project tracking
  • Enterprise search across connected apps

Frequently asked questions

Is Notion AI free?

Yes — Notion AI has a free plan (Free, $0 / month). The entry paid plan is Plus at $10 / user / month billed annually ($12 monthly).

What is Notion AI best for?

Best for: teams already running their work in Notion who want AI where their docs live. If you are not on Notion yet, do not buy it for the AI alone — compare it with ChatGPT or Claude first.

What should I watch out for with Notion AI?
