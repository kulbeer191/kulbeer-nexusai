ChatGPT Review 2026

The general-purpose AI assistant from OpenAI — for writing, research, coding help and everyday work. The reference point most US teams compare other assistants against.

What it is

Most teams lose hours to drafting, summarising, researching and debugging. ChatGPT takes a plain-language instruction and returns a usable draft, explanation or plan in seconds — across writing, code, data and research — so one assistant covers the everyday work that used to need five different tools.

Official site: https://openai.com/chatgpt

✅ Pros

  • Free plan with no card needed to start
  • Broadest range of any general assistant: GPT-5.6 reasoning, deep research, Codex coding, custom GPTs
  • Huge ecosystem of apps, connectors and integrations
  • Business plans with admin controls and no training on business data by default

❌ Cons

  • Can be confidently wrong — always verify facts and code
  • Free and Go plans have usage limits, and Go may show ads
  • Plus has no annual discount
  • Pro tiers get expensive fast ($100–$500/mo)

Pricing

Prices change — always check the official site before buying. Prices below are the widely published plans at time of writing. Prices exclude taxes.

Verdict

Best for: most individuals and small teams who want one general assistant that handles writing, research and coding help. Start free, move to Plus at $20/mo when you hit limits, and switch to Business when you need a shared workspace.
