Anyword Review 2026

AI copywriting with a predictive performance score on every variation — pick the copy most likely to convert before spending a dollar on ads, with brand voice and audience personas built in.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

AI made writing cheap, but knowing which copy will actually convert is still expensive. Anyword adds real A/B-test data to AI copywriting: it generates marketing copy and scores each variation with a predictive performance score, so teams can pick the version most likely to win before spending a dollar on ads. It also centralizes brand voice, audience personas and messaging guidelines so every asset stays on-brand.

Official site: https://www.anyword.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Predictive performance scoring is genuinely differentiated — Anyword claims 82% accuracy at picking the better of two copy variations vs 52% for a generic model like GPT-5, based on its A/B-test dataset
  • Unlimited copy generation on every paid plan — you pay for prediction allowances and seats, not per word
  • Strong brand controls — centralized brand voice, tone, audience personas, messaging bank, and custom-built AI models trained on your own past campaigns (Business+)
  • Enterprise-grade trust — SOC 2, ISO, GDPR and HIPAA compliance, plus SSO, API access and a private LLM option on Enterprise

❌ Cons

  • No free plan — only a 7-day trial, so there is no way to test it properly before paying; the entry price ($49/mo) is steep for solo writers
  • A prediction score is not an A/B test result — the 82% accuracy claim is the vendor’s own figure and does not guarantee real-world ad performance
  • The meter is confusing — monthly and annual billing give different prediction allowances (Starter 50/mo monthly vs 100/mo annual; Data-Driven 100 vs 175), so the real cost depends on which billing view you are reading

Frequently asked questions

Is Anyword free?

No — Anyword has no free plan, only a 7-day trial. The entry paid plan is Starter at $49 / month ($39 billed annually).

Best for: paid social and email teams that want data-backed copy choices before launch — a prediction score on every variant, not just more words. No free plan: Starter $49/mo is the entry tier, and confirm the price on the official site first.
