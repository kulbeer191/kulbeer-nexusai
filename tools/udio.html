Udio Review 2026

AI music generation with deep editing — write, blend and restyle songs from a prompt, with voice control and style references.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Songwriting from scratch is slow, and stock music all sounds the same. Udio turns a prompt into a song and then lets you edit it — change the style, rewrite lyrics, blend influences, control the voice — so creators can shape a track instead of accepting the first generation.

Official site: https://udio.com

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Strong audio quality with deep editing: styles, voice control, lyric edits
  • Cheap entry — Standard is $10/mo with 2,400 credits
  • Style references and audio uploads for shaping a sound
  • Up to 5 concurrent generations on Pro

Frequently asked questions

Is Udio free?

Yes — Udio has a free plan (Free, $0 / month). The entry paid plan is Standard at $10 / month ($8 annual).

What is Udio best for?

Best for: in 2026, Udio is a free idea generator, not a production tool — you cannot download anything you make on any plan. Try it free to sketch songs and test styles, but do not pay for it to release music until downloads and commercial use return. Check udio.com terms before subscribing.
