Otter.ai Review 2026

AI meeting agent that transcribes, summarizes and turns calls into action items.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Teams lose hours taking notes and chasing follow-ups after meetings, and decisions made on calls are forgotten. Otter.ai automatically joins Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet calls (or records bot-free from desktop), transcribes in real time, and turns every meeting into a searchable summary with action items — plus an AI chat that answers questions across all past meetings.

Official site: https://otter.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Automatic meeting capture with live transcription, speaker identification, and summaries with action items
  • Otter AI Chat searches across meetings and connected apps to draft follow-ups and reports
  • Free Basic plan is genuinely usable for light use, with Zoom/Teams/Meet integrations included
  • Business features include CRM sync (Salesforce/HubSpot), unlimited imports, and admin controls

  • Promo pricing on the page makes the real list price easy to misread
