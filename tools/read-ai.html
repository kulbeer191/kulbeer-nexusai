Overview

US teams lose hours to meeting notes and follow-ups — decisions, action items and promises made in Zoom, Teams and Meet calls vanish once the call ends, and nobody has time to rewatch recordings. Read AI joins meetings as an assistant, auto-generates summaries, transcripts, action items and topic readouts, and makes everything searchable across meetings, email and messages — plus a personal meeting coach, and in 2026 Ada, an email-based digital twin that schedules follow-ups and resolves calendar conflicts.

Official site: https://www.read.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Auto summaries, transcripts, action items and topic readouts for Zoom, Teams and Meet
  • Ask Read AI search across meetings, email and messages with citations
  • Personalized meeting coach (talking speed, filler words, bias, charisma)
  • Ada digital twin schedules follow-ups and resolves calendar conflicts by email
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA compliant; no AI training on customer data by default

✅ Pros

  • Platform-agnostic — works across Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet, plus in-person capture via mobile/desktop apps; no install required to start
  • Genuine free plan — $0, 5 meeting transcripts a month, no credit card required, with unlimited enterprise search and the personal meeting coach included
  • Beyond notes: Ask Read AI search across meetings, email and messages with citations; action items routed to the tools the team already uses; personalized meeting coach (talking speed, filler words, bias, charisma)
  • Strong privacy posture — SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA compliant; no AI training on customer data by default; host approval required before the assistant joins; 20+ languages supported

❌ Cons

  • Audio/video playback is paywalled — only on Enterprise ($22.50/user a month annual); Fathom records video on its free plan, so Read AI is the expensive way to rewatch a meeting
  • Free plan is thin — 5 transcripts a month with a 1-hour meeting cap; any team that meets daily needs Pro
  • Enterprise+ needs a 5-license minimum ($1,785 a year annual commitment floor)
  • A bot joins every meeting — some clients/participants find a visible notetaker intrusive, and host approval is required (can block ad-hoc calls)

Frequently asked questions

Is Read AI free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with 5 meeting transcripts a month, a 1-hour meeting cap, unlimited enterprise search and the personal meeting coach — no credit card required. Any team that meets daily needs Pro at $15 a user a month billed annually.

What is Read AI best for?

US teams that live in meetings across Zoom, Teams and Meet and want summaries, transcripts and searchable meeting memory in one place — sales teams with auto-summarized client calls and CRM integrations, remote teams catching up async, and founders using the meeting coach and Ada for follow-ups.

What should I watch out for with Read AI?

Rewatching recordings costs Enterprise ($22.50 a user a month annual) — Fathom records video on its free plan. The free plan caps at 5 meetings a month, and a visible bot joins every meeting (host approval required).

Verdict

Best for: US teams that live in meetings across Zoom, Teams and Meet and want summaries, transcripts and searchable meeting memory in one place — with the warning that rewatching recordings costs Enterprise, and the free plan caps at 5 meetings a month.
