Perplexity Teammates Review 2026

Always-on AI agents that take persistent roles, keep working in the background and check in over Slack.

Overview

Knowledge work stalls between meetings — research, follow-ups, and recurring tasks pile up with no owner. Perplexity Teammates are always-on AI agents that take persistent roles (for example on-call engineer, legal researcher, or ads manager), inherit your Perplexity Computer projects and memory, and keep working in the background. They check in over Slack and ask you only when a decision is needed.

Official site: https://www.perplexity.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Inherits Perplexity Computer projects and memory, and works inside Slack
  • Built on Perplexity's cited research engine with frontier-model access on Max
