Outwrite Review 2026

A budget AI writing assistant that catches spelling and grammar errors and rewrites sentences for clarity, style and structure — in English, French and Spanish — wherever you write, with plagiarism checks included in Pro.

Overview

Non-native and busy professional writers produce emails, docs and marketing copy with grammar slips and wordy, passive sentences — and hiring an editor for every draft is too slow and expensive. Outwrite is a budget AI writing assistant that catches spelling/grammar errors and rewrites sentences for clarity, style and structure, in English, French and Spanish, wherever you write (web app, Chrome extension, Google Docs, MS Word).

Official site: https://www.outwrite.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Spelling and grammar checking in English, French and Spanish
  • AI style improvements and structural suggestions
  • Sentence rewriting (shorten/lengthen/formalize) and passive-to-active voice
  • Plagiarism checks (50/month on Pro)
  • Web app, browser extensions, Google Docs, Microsoft Word

✅ Pros

  • Cheapest full-featured grammar checker among the majors — Pro $9.95/mo billed yearly, under Grammarly's $12
  • Plagiarism checks (50/month) included in Pro at no extra cost
  • Goes beyond grammar: AI style improvements, structural suggestions, sentence rewriting (shorten/lengthen/formalize), passive-to-active voice
  • Works in three languages — English, French, Spanish
  • Works everywhere: web app, browser extensions, Google Docs, Microsoft Word
  • Free Essential plan covers core spelling/grammar + thesaurus with no card required

❌ Cons

  • Free plan is bare-bones — no style, structural, rewriting or plagiarism features
  • Only 3 languages (vs LanguageTool's 30+); no AI content detector
  • Plagiarism checks capped at 50/month even on Pro
  • No free trial of Pro is stated on the pricing page
  • Pricing page localizes currency — this read showed EUR, so US buyers should confirm USD at checkout

Frequently asked questions

Is Outwrite free?

Yes. The Essential plan costs $0 and covers core spelling and grammar plus a thesaurus with no card required — but it has no style, structural, rewriting or plagiarism features. Paid plans start at Pro, $9.95 a month billed yearly.

What is Outwrite best for?

Marketing and content teams polishing blog posts, landing pages and social copy before publishing, or small-business teams standardizing client emails and proposals on the Teams plan.

What should I watch out for with Outwrite?

The free plan is bare-bones, there is no free trial of Pro, and the pricing page localizes currency — US buyers should confirm USD at checkout.

Verdict

Best for: Budget-conscious writers and small teams that want a full-featured grammar checker under Grammarly's price — with the warning that the free plan is bare-bones and there is no free trial of Pro.
