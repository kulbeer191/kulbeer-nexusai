Raycast Review 2026

A keyboard-first launcher for Mac (and Windows beta) that replaces Spotlight with one command bar for apps, files, clipboard, snippets and AI — everything a power user does, without touching the mouse.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Power users lose hours a day to context switching — hunting through menus, retyping the same text, digging up copied items and juggling windows. Raycast puts it all behind one keyboard shortcut: launch apps, search files, paste from clipboard history, run extensions and ask AI, all without leaving the keyboard.

Official site: https://www.raycast.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Keyboard-first launcher replacing Spotlight
  • Raycast AI with chat, agents and screen awareness
  • Thousands of extensions in the Raycast Store
  • Clipboard history, snippets and window management
  • Cloud Sync, dictation and custom themes on Pro

✅ Pros

  • Blazing-fast launcher that replaces Spotlight — apps, files, calculations and commands in one keystroke
  • Raycast AI built in on Pro: Quick AI, AI Chat, agents, screen awareness and memory, with frontier models
  • Huge extension store — thousands of community extensions for GitHub, Linear, Slack, Jira and more
  • Clipboard history, snippets, window management and quicklinks cover the daily productivity basics
  • Cloud Sync, dictation, translator and custom themes on Pro keep your setup identical everywhere

❌ Cons

  • AI is paywalled — the Free plan has no Raycast AI at all, and AI runs on monthly credits (500 on Pro)
  • Mac-first: the Windows version only arrived as a public beta in November 2025 and still lacks Cloud Sync
  • Credit-based AI pricing climbs fast — heavy AI users need Plus ($20/mo) or Max ($50/mo) for more credits and top-tier models
  • Learning curve: getting real value means learning shortcuts and configuring extensions, not just installing it

Frequently asked questions

Is Raycast free?

Yes — Raycast Free costs $0 and covers the core launcher, clipboard history (3 months), snippets, window management and thousands of extensions. But it includes no Raycast AI and no Cloud Sync; AI starts with Pro at $10/mo.

What is Raycast best for?

Mac power users who live on the keyboard — developers, writers and ops folks who want apps, files, clipboard, snippets and AI behind one shortcut, plus teams that share commands and snippets through Teams Pro.

What should I watch out for with Raycast?

AI is credit-based, so 500 credits a month on Pro can run out fast if you lean on AI all day — Plus and Max get expensive quickly; and Windows users should know the Windows build is still a public beta without Cloud Sync.

Verdict

Best for: Mac power users who live on the keyboard — developers, writers and ops folks who want apps, files, clipboard, snippets and AI behind one shortcut.
