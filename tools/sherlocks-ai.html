Overview

On-call engineers lose hours triaging alerts across fragmented tools while incidents burn. Sherlocks.ai deploys 16+ specialized AI SRE agents that investigate incidents in parallel the moment an alert fires, correlating logs, metrics, traces and runbooks, and hand the on-call engineer a root cause with its evidence in minutes — inside Slack, without replacing the existing observability stack.

Official site: https://www.sherlocks.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • 16+ specialized AI SRE agents investigating incidents in parallel
  • Root-cause analysis with evidence, delivered in Slack and Microsoft Teams
  • In-VPC or air-gapped deployment with read-only data access
  • Alert correlation and noise reduction across the existing observability stack
  • SOC 2 Type 2 certified with SSO/SAML, audit logs and RBAC on Enterprise

✅ Pros

  • 16+ domain-specialized agents investigate in parallel; most alerts analyzed in 2–3 minutes, complex multi-service incidents in 5–6 (per vendor)
  • Runs inside the customer's own VPC with read-only permissions, SOC 2 Type 2 certified, air-gapped option — built to pass security review
  • Works with the existing stack (Datadog, New Relic, Prometheus, Sentry, AWS/GCP/Azure, Kubernetes, Slack, Microsoft Teams) — no re-instrumentation or migration
  • Free tier is genuinely usable: 30 investigations a month, all 16+ agents included, no credit card required

❌ Cons

  • Free tier hard-caps at 30 investigations, then further investigations pause until the monthly reset
  • No mid-tier paid plan — the jump is from $0 straight to custom-priced Enterprise
  • Remediation is suggested fixes, not autonomous fixes — a human still applies the fix
  • Young vendor (listed October 2025) with a small public review base; MTTR and downtime reduction claims are vendor-reported, not independently verified

Frequently asked questions

Is Sherlocks.ai free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 30 investigations a month with all 16+ AI agents, Slack and Microsoft Teams integration, and self-serve onboarding — no credit card required. It hard-caps at 30 investigations, then pauses until the monthly reset.

What is Sherlocks.ai best for?

US on-call SRE and platform teams who want incidents investigated the moment an alert fires — the agents correlate logs, metrics, traces and runbooks and post a root cause with evidence in Slack, so the engineer starts from the answer instead of from scratch.

What should I watch out for with Sherlocks.ai?

There is no mid-tier paid plan, so growing past 30 investigations a month means jumping straight to custom Enterprise pricing. The agents suggest fixes rather than applying them, and the vendor's MTTR and downtime-reduction claims are self-reported.

Verdict

Best for: US on-call SRE and platform teams who want incidents investigated the moment an alert fires, with a root cause and evidence delivered in Slack — and regulated teams that need in-VPC, read-only, air-gapped deployment.
