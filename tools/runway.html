Runway Review 2026

Text-to-video with Gen-4.5, plus editing, restyling and third-party models in one workspace.

Overview

Producing short, cinematic video used to require a shoot, an editor, and days of post-production. Runway turns a text prompt or a still image into a polished 2–10 second clip in minutes, and lets creators edit, restyle, and extend existing footage in the same browser workspace — so small teams can ship ads, social clips, and film concepts without a studio.

Official site: https://runway.com

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Flagship Gen-4.5 model with strong motion consistency, realistic physics, and character stability across clips
  • Full creative suite in one place: text/image-to-video, video editing (Aleph), performance transfer (Act-Two), plus image and audio generation
  • Also hosts third-party models (Veo, Kling, Seedance) under one credit system
  • Free plan needs no card; paid plans remove watermarks and add 4K upscaling

Frequently asked questions

Is Runway free?

Yes — Runway has a free plan (Free, $0 / month). The entry paid plan is Standard at $15 / month ($12 billed annually).

What is Runway best for?

Best for: small creative teams shipping short social and ad clips fast. Standard at $15/mo is a good trial of the full model set; heavy video teams should budget for Pro or Max credits.
