Cassidy AI Review 2026

Cassidy lets non-technical teams build AI agents grounded in their own knowledge base, automating document-heavy work like underwriting triage and RFP responses.

What it is

In document-heavy businesses like insurance, professional services, and manufacturing, critical work is manual, slow, and buried in dense files. Cassidy lets non-technical teams build AI agents and workflows grounded in their own knowledge base, so high-stakes processes — underwriting triage, RFP responses, RFQ handling — run automatically, with guardrails and audit trails.

Official site: https://www.cassidy.ai/

✅ Pros

  • No-code agent and workflow builder — business teams ship automations in hours without engineering
  • Knowledge Base grounds every agent in company documents, with document verification to flag stale content
  • Strong enterprise compliance: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, CASA; no training on customer data
  • Free Starter tier (3 seats, 10,000 credits/mo) plus a 14-day free trial to evaluate

❌ Cons

  • No public prices above the free tier — Business and Enterprise are quote-only, so budgeting needs a sales call
  • Conflicting price history: Starter was $79/mo in mid-2026 and billing docs still show that figure, while the current pricing page lists it as free — confirm before buying
  • Credit metering is opaque — premium models burn roughly 5× the credits of standard ones and no top-up price is published
  • Key integrations (Zendesk, ServiceNow, Jira) plus SSO, audit logs, and retention tooling are paid-only; Starter can’t reach a helpdesk queue
