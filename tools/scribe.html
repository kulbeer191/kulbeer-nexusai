Scribe Review 2026

An AI process-documentation tool — capture clicks and screen as you work and Scribe automatically turns them into step-by-step guides with screenshots and AI-written instructions, which teams can edit, redact, brand and share.

Overview

US ops, IT and training teams lose days writing how-to guides and SOPs — the knowledge of how work actually gets done lives in people's heads, new hires wait weeks to ramp, and 'how do I do this?' tickets never stop. Scribe captures clicks and screen as you work and automatically turns them into step-by-step guides with screenshots and AI-written instructions, which teams can edit, redact, brand and share — and in 2026 it positions itself as 'workflow context for teams & AI agents,' feeding captured workflows to AI agents too.

Official site: https://scribe.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Record once — Scribe writes the guide with steps, screenshots and AI titles
  • Autocapture AI turns work the team already did into Scribes automatically
  • Edit, redact, brand and share guides; export to PDF/HTML/Markdown/Word
  • MCP server and enterprise search API feed captured workflows to AI agents
  • SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, CCPA, GDPR, FERPA; auto-redaction of PII/PHI on Enterprise

✅ Pros

  • Zero-effort documentation — record once, Scribe writes the guide (steps, screenshots, AI titles/descriptions); Autocapture AI turns work the team already did into Scribes automatically
  • Real free plan — Basic $0 with browser capture, quick customization, sharing by link/embed, up to 10 guides
  • Enterprise-grade: SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, CCPA, GDPR, FERPA; auto-redaction of PII/PHI on Enterprise; trusted by 94% of the Fortune 500 (per homepage), 7M+ users

Frequently asked questions

Is Scribe free?

Yes. The Basic plan costs $0 with browser capture, quick customization, sharing by link and embed, and up to 10 guides. Desktop capture needs Pro, starting at $25 a seat a month billed yearly (Pro Personal).

What is Scribe best for?

US ops, IT and training teams that need SOPs and onboarding guides without writing them by hand — turn repeated 'how do I' processes into branded SOPs once, produce client-facing process documentation at scale, and feed captured workflows to AI agents via the MCP server.

What should I watch out for with Scribe?

Pro Team's 5-seat minimum pushes the real team entry cost to about $65 a month billed yearly even for a 2-3 person team. Pricing moved twice in a month in 2026 ($23 to $13 annual), so confirm at checkout — and paid fees are non-refundable.

Verdict

Best for: US ops, IT and training teams that need SOPs and onboarding guides without writing them by hand — with the warning that the 5-seat Pro Team minimum pushes the real team entry cost to about $65 a month, and pricing moved twice in a month in 2026.
