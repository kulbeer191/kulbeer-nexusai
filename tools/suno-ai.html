Suno AI Review 2026

Type a prompt, get a full song with vocals — AI music generation for creators, from jingles and intros to complete tracks.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Original music used to mean hiring a composer or licensing stock tracks. Suno generates complete songs — lyrics, melody, vocals and arrangement — from a text prompt in under a minute, so a YouTuber, podcaster or indie game dev can have a custom soundtrack without a studio budget.

Official site: https://suno.com

✅ Pros

  • Full songs with vocals from a single prompt, including lyrics and arrangement
  • Commercial use rights on paid plans, for songs you generate while subscribed
  • Latest v6 models sound close to studio quality for most genres
  • Cheap entry — Pro is $10/mo and covers about 500 generations

❌ Cons

  • Downloads are capped separately from credits: 20 songs a month on Pro, 60 on Premier
  • Free plan has no song downloads and no commercial rights
  • Ongoing label litigation (Universal and Sony filed a second suit in Sept 2026)
  • You get commercial use rights, not outright copyright ownership of outputs

Pricing

Prices change — always check the official site before buying. Prices below are the widely published plans at time of writing. Prices exclude taxes.

PlanPriceBest for
Free$0 / month50 credits a day; v6-mini model; no downloads, no commercial rights
Pro$10 / month ($8 annual)2,500 credits a month; 20 song downloads a month; commercial rights; v6 and v6-wild
Premier$30 / month ($24 annual)10,000 credits a month; 60 downloads a month; Suno Studio

Verdict

Best for: creators who need original music fast — intros, jingles, background tracks and full songs. Pro ($10/mo) is the practical starting point: it unlocks the full models, downloads and commercial rights. Keep an eye on the litigation if you build a business on it.
