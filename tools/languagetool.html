LanguageTool Review 2026

A multilingual grammar, spelling, punctuation and style checker with an AI paraphraser, covering 30+ languages — with a genuinely free tier and a personal style guide that keeps wording consistent across a team.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US teams — especially non-native English speakers — need emails, docs, support replies and academic writing polished in consistent voice, often in more than one language. LanguageTool is a multilingual grammar, spelling, punctuation and style checker with an AI paraphraser, covering 30+ languages (including US, UK, Canadian and Australian English variants), with a genuinely free tier and a personal style guide that keeps wording consistent across a team.

Official site: https://languagetool.org/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Grammar, spelling, punctuation and style checks in 30+ languages
  • AI paraphraser with unlimited paraphrasing on Premium
  • Personal style guide and team dictionary on Premium/Team
  • Browser add-ons plus Google Docs, Microsoft Word and desktop apps
  • Open-source core teams can self-host

✅ Pros

  • Real free plan: the web editor checks spelling, grammar and simple punctuation with no login required
  • 30+ languages with automatic detection and six English variants — the strongest multilingual grammar checker in the directory
  • Open-source core that privacy-minded teams can self-host, unlike Grammarly or QuillBot
  • Browser add-ons (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) plus Google Docs, Microsoft Word and macOS/Windows apps
  • Premium adds 20,000+ extra checks (EN/DE/FR/ES/NL/PL/PT), unlimited AI paraphrasing, and a personal style guide / team dictionary

❌ Cons

  • Since March 2026 the browser extension is Premium-only — free users are limited to the web editor, losing the everywhere-you-type experience
  • Free plan is tight: 2,000 characters per text field and only 3 AI paraphrases per day
  • Prices are region-dependent and load via script on the official page — US and EU prices differ materially, so the checkout amount must be confirmed
  • It is a checker/paraphraser, not a draft-from-scratch AI writer — narrower scope than Jasper, Copy.ai or ChatGPT

Frequently asked questions

Is LanguageTool free?

Yes. The Basic plan costs $0 — the web editor checks spelling, grammar and simple punctuation with no login required. Paid plans start at Premium, $24.90 a month billed monthly or $69.90 a year ($5.83 a month).

What is LanguageTool best for?

Multilingual US teams and non-native writers who want reliable grammar and style checking with a genuinely free tier — a content team standardizing on a shared style guide across Google Docs and Word, non-native speakers polishing emails and support replies, or a research team checking theses and papers.

What should I watch out for with LanguageTool?

Since March 2026 the browser extension is Premium-only, so the free plan only works inside the web editor. Prices are also region-dependent (US and EU prices differ), so confirm the checkout amount on the official site.

Verdict

Best for: Multilingual US teams and non-native writers who want reliable grammar and style checking with a genuinely free tier — with the warning that since March 2026 the browser extension is Premium-only, so the free plan only works inside the web editor.
