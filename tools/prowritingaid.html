ProWritingAid Review 2026

A deep editing and style coach for authors and long-form writers — 25+ manuscript reports, unlimited rephrasing and AI-powered Sparks edits inside Word, Google Docs and Scrivener.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Grammar checkers catch typos but miss the problems that make long writing weak — repeated words, sticky sentences, pacing, cliches and flat style. ProWritingAid analyzes full manuscripts with 25+ in-depth reports and chapter critiques, so US authors, editors and content teams can line-edit a book, report or long article to a professional standard before it reaches readers.

Official site: https://prowritingaid.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • 25+ manuscript analysis reports covering style, pacing and repetition
  • Unlimited rephrasing and Sparks AI edits
  • Integrations with Word, Google Docs, Scrivener and Gmail
  • Daily Chapter Critiques with author comparison
  • Custom style guide and snippets for consistent voice

✅ Pros

  • 25+ in-depth writing reports — style, pacing, repetition, cliches, transitions and sentence variety
  • Unlimited rephrases and AI-powered Sparks edits on paid plans
  • Works inside Word, Google Docs, Scrivener, Gmail and browsers, plus desktop apps
  • Chapter Critiques and author comparison help fiction writers improve draft by draft
  • One-time lifetime license option — no endless subscription if you write for years

❌ Cons

  • Free plan only analyzes 500 words at a time, with 10 rephrases and 2 report runs a day
  • Monthly billing is steep — $30 a month versus $10 a month on the yearly plan
  • English only, and it is built for long-form writing rather than quick marketing copy
  • Refunds run only 3 days on yearly and lifetime plans, and monthly plans cannot be refunded

Frequently asked questions

Is ProWritingAid free?

Yes, there is a free plan at $0, but it only analyzes 500 words at a time and allows 10 rephrases and 2 report runs a day — full manuscripts need Premium.

What is ProWritingAid best for?

Novelists, nonfiction authors and long-form writers who want chapter-level line editing, style reports and critiques inside Word, Google Docs or Scrivener.

What should I watch out for with ProWritingAid?

Monthly billing costs three times the yearly rate ($30 vs $10 a month), refunds run only 3 days on yearly and lifetime plans, and the tool is English-only and built for long-form writing, not quick marketing copy.

Verdict

Best for: novelists, nonfiction authors and long-form writers who want chapter-level line editing, style reports and critiques inside Word, Google Docs or Scrivener.
