Synthesia Review 2026

Turn a typed script into a polished presenter-led video with a lifelike AI avatar in 140+ languages — no camera, actor, or editing skills required.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Corporate teams need professional presenter-led videos — training, onboarding, product updates, internal comms — but filming is slow, expensive, and impossible to update. Synthesia turns a typed script into a polished talking-head video with a lifelike AI avatar in 140+ languages, no camera, actor, or editing skills required, and a finished video can be regenerated in minutes when the script changes.

Official site: https://www.synthesia.io/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • No filming needed — type a script, pick from 125–240+ stock avatars or clone yourself as a personal avatar, and get a finished video in minutes
  • 1-click translation into 140+ languages with voice cloning and lip-sync, so one training video serves a global workforce
  • Built for enterprise L&D: SCORM export to any LMS, interactive videos/quizzes, branded video pages, and live team collaboration
  • New Syren AI video agent builds creative videos from a plain-language description, plus a free Basic plan to try it

❌ Cons

  • Credit-based pricing is hard to budget — a single minute of Syren video can burn 1,000–2,000 credits, so heavy users hit the cap fast
  • Avatars can still feel stiff or uncanny in emotional or conversational scenes; it is not a replacement for real presenters in high-trust content
  • Free Basic plan videos carry a watermark and cannot be downloaded (share link only)
  • Prices vary by visitor region — the official page showed Starter at $19/mo from the US but other sources saw $29/mo from elsewhere, so readers must confirm at checkout; all videos also pass through content moderation, which can delay publishing
