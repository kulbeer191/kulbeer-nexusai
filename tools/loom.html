Loom Review 2026

Async video messaging for teams — record your screen, camera or both in a few clicks and share it as a link with a transcript, comments and tasks, so the recipient watches at 2x when it suits them and the meeting never has to happen.

Overview

US teams lose hours to live meetings that could have been a short video — status updates, bug reports, design feedback and onboarding all wait for a calendar slot, and the context dies in a chat thread. Loom lets anyone record their screen, camera or both in a few clicks and share it as a link with a transcript, comments and tasks — the recipient watches at 2x when it suits them, and the meeting never has to happen. Loom AI then turns the recording into titles, summaries, chapters and action items automatically.

Official site: https://www.loom.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Record screen, camera or both in a few clicks and share as a link
  • Transcript, comments and tasks on every video; watch at 2x
  • Loom AI — auto titles, summaries, chapters, tasks, filler-word removal, edit by transcript
  • Native Jira, Confluence and Slack integrations, plus GitHub, Gmail, Figma embeds
  • Viewers and commenters are free — only recorders need paid seats

✅ Pros

  • The default async-video tool for US teams — 25M+ users, 200,000+ customers, ~5M videos recorded a month (Atlassian acquisition announcement, Oct 2023); 'send a Loom' is workplace vocabulary at companies like Airbnb, Dropbox, Zendesk, Uber and HubSpot
  • Real free plan — Starter $0 with 25 videos per person, 5-minute screen recordings, unlimited meeting length, transcriptions in 50+ languages and up to 50 workspace members
  • Loom AI is genuinely useful, not a checkbox — auto titles, summaries, chapters, tasks, filler-word and silence removal, edit-by-transcript, auto meeting notes and recap emails, video-to-text automation
  • Deep Atlassian integration — owned by Atlassian since Nov 2023 ($975M acquisition); native Jira, Confluence and Slack integrations, plus GitHub, Gmail, Figma embeds and more

❌ Cons

  • AI costs extra — the Business plan ($15/mo annual) has NO Loom AI at all; the AI suite is a $6/user/mo step-up to Business + AI ($20/mo annual), so the real AI price is $20, not $15
  • Billing is confusing after the Atlassian takeover — annual subscriptions bought on loom.com are charged by distinct paid users, while atlassian.com annuals are billed by user tier; older guides still quote pre-takeover rules and prices
  • Free plan is tight for real use — 25 videos per person and a 5-minute cap on screen recordings (meetings unlimited) plus 720p only; any team recording daily hits the cap fast
  • Per-seat pricing scales with every recorder — viewers are free, but everyone who records needs a paid seat, so a 20-person team of recorders pays $300-$400/mo before any discount

Frequently asked questions

Is Loom free?

Yes. The Starter plan costs $0 with 25 videos per person, 5-minute screen recordings, unlimited meeting length, transcriptions in 50+ languages and up to 50 workspace members. Paid plans start at Business, $15 a user a month billed annually — but Business has NO Loom AI; the AI suite needs Business + AI at $20 a user a month annual.

What is Loom best for?

US teams that want to replace status meetings with short async videos — sales teams sending personalized video pitches instead of booking intro calls, engineering teams dropping a bug repro or code walkthrough into a Jira ticket, and remote teams recording weekly updates and onboarding walkthroughs once for new hires to watch on demand.

What should I watch out for with Loom?

The real AI price is Business + AI at $20 a month annual, not the $15 Business tier. Billing rules differ between loom.com and atlassian.com annuals, and per-seat pricing scales with every recorder — a 20-person team of recorders pays $300-$400 a month before any discount.

Verdict

Best for: US teams that want to replace status meetings with short async videos — with the warning that the real AI price is Business + AI at $20 a month annual, not the $15 Business tier, and billing rules differ between loom.com and atlassian.com.
