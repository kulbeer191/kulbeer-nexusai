Zapier Agents Review 2026

No-code AI teammates that watch your apps and do the routine work themselves.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US teams lose hours every day to repetitive work spread across the apps they already pay for — qualifying leads, prepping for meetings, triaging support tickets. Zapier Agents lets a non-technical team build an AI teammate in plain language that watches those apps, pulls live data, and takes the actions itself, so routine work runs without a human in the loop.

Official site: https://zapier.com/agents

Use cases for US teams: Sales ops: an agent monitors inbound form fills, enriches each lead, scores it, and alerts the rep in Slack the moment a high-value prospect arrives. Support: an agent triages Zendesk tickets, auto-answers common questions, and escalates complex ones with full context.

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Works across 9,000+ apps your team already uses — no new stack to learn
  • No-code setup: describe the teammate in plain language; Zapier Copilot helps build it
  • Live data sources plus web browsing, so agents act on current information, not stale snapshots
  • Free tier (400 activities/month) lets a team test a real agent before paying

❌ Cons

  • Usage is metered in "activities" — a busy daily agent can burn through the 400 free activities fast
  • Agents are billed as a separate add-on from Zapier's Zap plans, so teams manage two bills
  • Product is in transition: Zapier announced Oct 6, 2026 that Agents is becoming "AI by Zapier" — confirm current status before buying
  • Still in beta per its own FAQ — support is limited and results can vary run to run
