Replit Review 2026

Browser IDE where an AI Agent plans, builds and publishes full-stack apps from a prompt.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Turning an idea into a working app usually means installing toolchains, configuring databases, and setting up hosting before writing a line of code. Replit removes that setup entirely: its AI Agent plans, builds, tests, and publishes full-stack apps in the browser, with authentication, database, and hosting built in — so founders and beginners can ship a live app from a plain-English prompt.

Official site: https://replit.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Zero setup — browser IDE, Agent, database, hosting, and publishing in one place
  • Free Mode lets you brainstorm and build without token costs
  • Built-in services (Auth, Database, Hosting, Monitoring) and 100+ integrations (OpenAI, Stripe, Google Workspace)
  • Best fit for non-developers and founders who want a live app fast

Is Replit free?
