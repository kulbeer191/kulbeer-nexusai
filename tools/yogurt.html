Yogurt Review 2026

A 100% free, open-source meeting notetaker that records, transcribes and summarizes calls entirely on your own computer with local AI — no bot joins the call, nothing is uploaded, and there is no subscription at all.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Paid meeting notetakers (Granola, Otter, Fireflies) send every call to the cloud and charge a monthly subscription, and many join the call as an awkward bot. Yogurt records the microphone and call audio on your own computer, transcribes and summarizes it with local AI models, and never uploads anything — so meeting notes stay private, cost nothing, and no bot ever joins the call.

Official site: https://yogurt.so

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Local transcription and summarization with on-device AI
  • No bot joins the call — records local audio from any call app
  • Transcribes in 99 languages (Whisper large-v3-turbo)
  • "Ask" across all meetings with answers linked to source meetings
  • Local MCP server for Claude Desktop/Code; upload existing recordings (MP3/M4A/WAV)

✅ Pros

  • 100% free, open source (MIT), no account, no subscription, no trial — $0 forever
  • Fully local and private: audio, transcripts, notes and AI processing never leave the computer; no telemetry; safe for confidential client, legal, therapy and board calls
  • No bot joins the call — works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and any other call app by recording local audio
  • Transcribes in 99 languages (Whisper large-v3-turbo on capable machines), including multi-language mode when speakers switch languages mid-meeting
  • "Ask" across one meeting, a folder, or all meetings at once ('what did we decide about pricing?') with answers linked to the source meetings
  • Local read-only MCP server lets Claude Desktop/Code read and search meeting notes without copy-pasting transcripts

❌ Cons

  • Very new and very early — launched 10–11 Nov 2026, currently v0.3.x; a days-old project with no long-term track record
  • Heavy local requirements: at least 8 GB RAM; first-run setup downloads ~5 GB of AI models, up to 14 GB or more afterwards; smaller machines get a smaller Whisper model and make more transcription mistakes
  • Speaker names and calendar integration work on Mac only; Windows installer is unsigned
  • No cloud sync — notes live on one computer only; no team sharing, no mobile app (desktop Mac/Windows/Linux only)
  • You must tell people you are recording (no bot announces it) — consent laws vary by US state

Frequently asked questions

Is Yogurt free?

Yes — completely. Yogurt costs $0 forever: the entire product is free and open source under the MIT license, with no paid tiers, no trial and no account required.

What is Yogurt best for?

A US consultant or lawyer recording client calls without a bot joining and without client audio ever touching a vendor's cloud, or a privacy-conscious founder running meeting notes entirely on-device.

What should I watch out for with Yogurt?

It launched 10–11 November 2026 and is v0.3.x, so it is very early — and it needs at least 8 GB of RAM with a ~5 GB model download at setup, with no cloud sync or team sharing.
