Overview

Teams' work is scattered across 100+ apps — tasks in one tool, docs in another, chat somewhere else — and context gets lost every time people switch. ClickUp is one workspace that connects tasks, docs, chat, dashboards and conversations to Brain², an AI layer that sees the whole workspace, finds answers, and delegates work to Super Agents that run 24/7. Instead of hunting for context, teams ask one place and get work done.

Official site: https://clickup.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • One workspace replaces 100+ products: tasks, docs, chat, dashboards, goals, time tracking and sprints in a single place
  • Brain² AI works with full workspace context and every frontier model (GPT, Claude Opus, Gemini), with deep search, auto-memory and Super Agents that delegate tasks around the clock
  • Free Forever plan is genuinely usable — unlimited tasks, subtasks and members, no credit card required
  • Enterprise-grade trust: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA; used by 85% of the Fortune 500
