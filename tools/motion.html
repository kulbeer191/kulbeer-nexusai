Motion Review 2026

AI auto-scheduling that time-blocks tasks, re-plans your day and warns of deadline risk.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Traditional to-do lists and calendars don't plan the day — tasks pile up, deadlines slip, and one rescheduled meeting wrecks the plan. Motion is an AI-powered all-in-one work platform that automatically prioritizes tasks, time-blocks them onto the calendar, and re-plans the day dozens of times as priorities change, combining tasks, projects, docs, calendar, and a meeting notetaker in one place.

Official site: https://www.usemotion.com/

✅ Pros

  • AI auto-scheduling that builds and rebuilds your day from tasks, deadlines, and priorities
  • Deadline-risk warnings days or weeks in advance, plus project ETA predictions
  • All-in-one: task planner, project manager, AI docs, calendar, and meeting notetaker with action items that become tasks
  • Meeting booking pages and automatic follow-up emails reduce scheduling busywork

❌ Cons

  • No free plan — paid only, with a free trial that requires a card and auto-converts unless cancelled
  • Solo users pay more than team seats (Pro AI $29/month annual for individuals vs $19/seat for teams)
  • AI usage is metered by monthly credits (7,500 on Pro AI, 15,000 on Business AI), with overage charges
  • Purchases are non-refundable per Motion's terms, so the trial must be used carefully
