Fyxer Review 2026

An AI executive assistant layered onto Gmail or Outlook — it sorts and labels the inbox, drafts replies in your own voice, records meetings with notes and follow-ups, and keeps a shared follow-up list so nothing promised to a client slips through.

Overview

For client-facing professionals, the inbox is the job — triaging email, drafting replies, tracking follow-ups and writing meeting notes eats hours every day. Fyxer layers an AI executive assistant onto Gmail or Outlook: it sorts and labels the inbox, drafts replies in the user's own voice, records meetings with notes and follow-ups, and keeps a shared follow-up list so nothing promised to a client slips through. Built on 500,000+ hours of human EA expertise from Fyxer People (founded 2016); trusted by 25,000+ companies and 100,000+ users.

Official site: https://www.fyxer.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Reply drafts in your own tone, with human approval before anything sends
  • Automatic inbox labeling and sorting (To respond / FYI / Marketing)
  • Video meeting recording with notes, action items and follow-ups
  • Shared Spaces and follow-up lists with owners for client-facing teams
  • SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR; data never used to train third-party models

✅ Pros

  • Drafts replies in your own tone and learns from corrections — and every draft waits for approval; Fyxer will never send email on your behalf
  • Real free plan ($0, 400 credits a month) with audio meeting notes, daily briefing and chat — plus a 14-day Team trial on every sign-up
  • End-to-end follow-up system: shared Spaces, follow-up lists with owners, team reporting on Team — built for client-facing teams (sales, recruiting, consultancy, real estate)
  • Strong privacy posture: SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR; data never used to train third-party models; data stored in EU/UK/US; HIPAA available on Enterprise

❌ Cons

  • Expensive — Pro starts at $49 per seat per month ($37 annual); a 10-person team pays $4,440 a year on Pro annual
  • Credit-metered usage on top of the seat price — chat costs 20 credits per message, meeting recording 1 credit per minute; heavy users can hit the 3,000-credit Pro cap
  • It will not send email or book anything without approval — a deliberate safety choice, but it caps how much work it can fully take off your plate versus a truly autonomous assistant. The Free plan also excludes the two headline features: no inbox sorting, no reply drafts

Frequently asked questions

Is Fyxer free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with 400 credits a month, audio meeting notes, a daily briefing and chat — but it excludes the two headline features (no inbox sorting, no reply drafts). Every sign-up also gets a 14-day free trial of Team.

What is Fyxer best for?

Email-heavy, client-facing US professionals (founders, recruiters, consultants, sales) who want EA-grade inbox help without hiring an EA — sales teams flagging every follow-up and drafting the reply, recruiters with auto-sorted inboxes, founders with a daily briefing and meeting recaps.

What should I watch out for with Fyxer?

The price: $37-$49 per seat per month plus a credit meter makes it one of the pricier productivity tools in the directory — a 10-person team pays $4,440 a year on Pro annual. And it will never send email or book anything without your approval.

Verdict

Best for: email-heavy, client-facing US professionals (founders, recruiters, consultants, sales) who want EA-grade inbox help without hiring an EA — with the warning that $37-$49 per seat per month plus a credit meter makes it one of the pricier productivity tools in the directory.
