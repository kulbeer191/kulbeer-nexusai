Magnific Review 2026

One subscription for 30+ leading AI models, pro editing tools and a 250M-asset stock library — generate, edit, upscale and publish on-brand visuals without leaving the platform.

Overview

US marketing and design teams juggle a dozen separate subscriptions — one image model here, a stock library there, an upscaler somewhere else — and burn hours switching tools. Magnific puts 30+ leading AI models, pro editing tools, and a 250M-asset stock library under one subscription with one shared credit pool, so a team can generate, edit, upscale, and publish on-brand visuals without leaving the platform. Magnific was formerly Freepik — the platform rebranded on 28 April 2026.

Official site: https://www.magnific.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • One subscription unlocks 30+ models (Flux, Nano Banana 2/Pro, Ideogram, GPT Image, Kling, Seedance) plus image, video, and audio tools — no need to pick a single vendor
  • 250M+ premium stock photos, vectors, PSDs, and templates included on paid plans, with a commercial license and no attribution required
  • Relax mode (Plus/Pro) gives unlimited generations on 30+ image models, so heavy creators stop watching a credit meter
  • “We never train on your data,” plus GDPR, ISO/IEC 27001, and SOC 2 compliance; Pro adds a Merchandise License for selling physical products
