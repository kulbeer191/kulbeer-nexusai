Junie Review 2026

JetBrains’ autonomous coding agent that plans, writes, tests, and refines code inside your IDE or terminal, running on any model you choose.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Development teams lose hours to boilerplate, debugging, and multi-file refactors that a human must babysit step by step. Junie is JetBrains’ autonomous coding agent: you describe the task in plain English and it plans, writes, tests, and refines the code inside your IDE or terminal — choosing the best model for each step, including models you bring yourself. It turns multi-hour coding tasks into supervised agent runs, so engineers review finished work instead of typing it.

Official site: https://www.jetbrains.com/junie/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Works inside the JetBrains IDEs teams already use, with IDE-native test verification — it runs the test suite and iterates on failures itself
  • Model freedom: 10+ frontier models (Claude Opus/Sonnet, GPT, Gemini, Grok) plus BYOK, including locally running models — no vendor lock-in
  • Free entry: Junie Lite is a free model in every JetBrains account with no trial or card, and the free Junie CLI with BYOK bypasses JetBrains credits entirely
  • Strong benchmark performance: top performer on SWE-Rebench (61.8% solve rate per third-party reviews)

❌ Cons

  • The IDE plugin requires a paid JetBrains IDE edition (IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate ~$199/yr) — a hidden cost on top of AI credits
  • Credit-metered billing: agentic tasks are token-heavy, so the 10-credit AI Pro quota can run out after just a few days of real use
  • Unused monthly credits expire every 30 days and do not roll over; top-up credits expire after 12 months

Frequently asked questions

Is Junie free?

Yes — Junie has a free plan (AI Free, $0). The entry paid plan is AI Pro (individual) at $10 / month ($8.33 billed annually).

What is Junie best for?

Best for: JetBrains users who want an autonomous agent that plans, tests and refines code with model freedom — including their own keys. Start free with Junie Lite or the CLI with BYOK; budget the AI Pro or Ultimate subscription plus a paid IDE edition if you want the full plugin.
