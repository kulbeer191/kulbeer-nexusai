Read AI vs Fathom (2026)

Two AI meeting notetakers go head to head — Read AI, the platform-agnostic assistant with searchable meeting memory and a personal coach, vs Fathom, the category's most generous free plan with unlimited recordings. Which one should US teams pick in 2026?

Read AI — Pros

  • Platform-agnostic — works across Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet, plus in-person capture via mobile/desktop apps; no install required to start
  • Genuine free plan — $0, 5 meeting transcripts a month, no credit card required, with unlimited enterprise search and the personal meeting coach included
  • Beyond notes: Ask Read AI search across meetings, email and messages with citations; action items routed to the tools the team already uses; personalized meeting coach (talking speed, filler words, bias, charisma)

Read AI — Cons

  • Audio/video playback is paywalled — only on Enterprise ($22.50/user a month annual); Fathom records video on its free plan, so Read AI is the expensive way to rewatch a meeting
  • Free plan is thin — 5 transcripts a month with a 1-hour meeting cap; any team that meets daily needs Pro
  • A bot joins every meeting — some clients/participants find a visible notetaker intrusive, and host approval is required (can block ad-hoc calls)

Fathom — Pros

  • The most generous free plan in the category: unlimited recordings, transcriptions, and storage, free forever
  • Accurate AI summaries, action items, and 'Ask Fathom' search across all past calls, plus clips and playlists
  • Deep integrations: summaries sync into Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, and Asana; CRM field sync on Business; public API + MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT

Fathom — Cons

  • Advanced AI summaries are capped on the free plan (5 per month) and AI action items, follow-up emails, and unlimited Ask Fathom require Premium
  • CRM sync is capped at 3 users per email domain on Free, Premium, and Team — full CRM field sync needs Business
  • Bot-free recording is Mac-first and still in beta; Windows bot-free is only a limited Microsoft Teams alpha

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Read AI or Fathom?

Both have a free $0 plan, but Fathom's is far more generous — unlimited recordings and transcriptions forever, versus Read AI's 5 transcripts a month. Paid entry is close: Read AI Pro is $15 a user a month billed annually, Fathom Premium is $20 a user a month ($16 annual). Read AI is cheaper per seat; Fathom gives you more for free.

Which should I choose, Read AI or Fathom?

Pick Read AI if your team meets across Zoom, Teams and Meet and wants searchable meeting memory across meetings, email and messages, plus a personal meeting coach. Pick Fathom if you want unlimited free recordings with video and the most generous free plan in the category.

What should I watch out for?

Read AI paywalls video playback behind Enterprise ($22.50 a user a month annual) and caps free at 5 meetings a month, and a visible bot joins every meeting. Fathom caps advanced AI summaries at 5 a month on free and caps CRM sync at 3 users per email domain below Business.

Verdict

Pick Read AI if you want searchable meeting memory across Zoom, Teams and Meet plus a personal coach. Pick Fathom if you want unlimited free recordings with video and the most generous free plan in the category.
