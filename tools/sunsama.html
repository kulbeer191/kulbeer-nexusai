Sunsama Review 2026

Guided daily planner that timeboxes your tasks and calendar into one realistic day.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Knowledge workers juggle tasks scattered across Asana, Jira, Gmail, and Slack, overcommit every morning, and end the day unsure what actually got done. Sunsama is a guided daily planner that pulls tasks and calendar events into one realistic day plan, timeboxes the work, and closes the day with a shutdown ritual — so US teams protect focus time and finish on time instead of working late.

Official site: https://www.sunsama.com/

Use cases for US teams: Ops and product teams whose work is scattered across Asana, Jira, and Slack: one daily plan with timeboxed focus blocks instead of context-switching all day. Founders, managers, and solopreneurs who overcommit: the guided morning plan and evening shutdown keep workloads realistic and protect work-life balance.

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • The guided planning ritual plus timeboxing puts tasks and calendar in one realistic daily plan, instead of an endless to-do list
  • Pulls tasks from 20+ tools (Asana, Jira, Trello, Todoist, Notion, Gmail, Slack, Teams) and unifies Google, Outlook, and Apple calendars in one view
  • Focus mode, Pomodoro timer, auto time tracking, and a daily shutdown ritual help teams end the day on time
  • AI assistant "Sunny" plus MCP/Zapier access, and SOC 2 compliance with IT-approval support for workplace rollouts

❌ Cons

  • No auto-scheduling: unlike Motion, it will not automatically reschedule tasks when plans change; the ritual requires daily manual effort
  • A single Pro plan with no cheaper tier, and Enterprise (SSO/SCIM, audit logs) is contact-sales only
