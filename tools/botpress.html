Botpress Review 2026

A visual AI agent builder with an open-source engine that lets US teams ship customer-facing agents across webchat, WhatsApp, Slack and Teams — billed per conversation instead of per seat, with one-click switching between GPT-4o, Claude and Gemini.

Overview

US teams need customer-facing AI agents that answer from their own knowledge, hand off to a human cleanly, and run on webchat and WhatsApp without per-seat pricing. Botpress gives them a visual builder (Studio) plus an Autonomous Engine that reasons over GPT-4o, Claude or Gemini, deploys to every major chat channel, and bills per conversation instead of per seat — with an open-source engine for teams that want data to stay in-house.

Official site: https://botpress.com

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Visual agent builder (Studio) plus Autonomous Engine over GPT-4o, Claude and Gemini
  • Deploy to webchat, WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram, Messenger and Microsoft Teams
  • Per-conversation pricing with auto-recharge packs
  • Whitelabel webchat and Help Center with a custom domain
  • Open-source engine with a self-host path

✅ Pros

  • Genuine free tier: $0, 3 AI agents, 3 seats and a Help Center — no credit card required
  • One-click model switching across GPT-4o, Claude and Gemini on the same agent, with no rebuild
  • Open-source engine with a self-host path, so data can stay in-house
  • 50+ integrations and deployment to webchat, WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram, Messenger and Microsoft Teams
  • Transparent conversation-based pricing with auto-recharge packs ($65/$50 per 100 conversations) — no surprise cut-offs

❌ Cons

  • Free tier is hard-capped at 100 conversations with no top-ups or overages — testing only, not a live channel
  • Plus price nearly doubled in the May 2026 repricing ($89 to $150 a month billed annually)
  • AI usage is billed separately through AI Spend on top of the subscription (Plus includes only $25)
  • Auto-recharge for conversation packs cannot be turned off; Team at $750 a month is a steep jump from Plus

Frequently asked questions

Is Botpress free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 3 AI agents, 3 seats and a Help Center — but it is hard-capped at 100 conversations with no top-ups, so it is for testing, not a live channel. Paid plans start at Plus, $150 a month billed annually ($189 billed monthly).

What is Botpress best for?

US teams that want to build and run customer-facing AI agents across chat channels with full control over models and flows — a support team deflecting repetitive tickets with an agent grounded in its Help Center, a sales team running a WhatsApp lead-qualification agent, or a product team shipping a white-labeled in-app assistant.

What should I watch out for with Botpress?

Plus costs nearly twice what it did before the May 2026 repricing, and AI usage is billed separately through AI Spend on top of the subscription. Also, the free tier's 100 conversations may be a one-time allowance — the pricing page does not say they reset monthly.

Verdict

Best for: US teams that want to build and run customer-facing AI agents across chat channels with full control over models and flows — with the warning that the free tier's 100 conversations are hard-capped and Plus costs nearly twice what it did before the May 2026 repricing.
