Akiflow Review 2026

Pull tasks from Gmail, Slack, Notion, and Jira into one inbox and drag them onto a single calendar — with an AI assistant that schedules and reschedules for you.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Akiflow pulls tasks from Gmail, Slack, Notion, Asana, Jira, and more into one Universal Inbox and lets busy professionals drag them onto a single calendar that syncs two ways with Google, Outlook, and iCloud — so founders, operators, and CXOs plan each day by time-blocking instead of juggling five inboxes, with an on-demand AI executive assistant (Aki) that schedules, reschedules, and nudges when the day overloads.

Official site: https://akiflow.com/

✅ Pros

  • One app for tasks + calendar: Universal Inbox, drag-and-drop time blocking, two-way Google/Outlook/iCloud sync, and booking links
  • Aki AI assistant + MCP Connector included in Pro — Claude/ChatGPT can read the schedule and manage tasks; Aki reschedules conflicts proactively
  • Single simple price: one feature set, no tiers or locked features; 44% saving on annual billing; free 1:1 onboarding call
  • Keyboard-first, fast capture (quick capture, shortcuts, focus mode, daily rituals) built for power users; offline mode and dark mode

❌ Cons

  • Expensive for a solo task manager ($34/mo on monthly billing) with no free plan — only a 7-day trial that requires a card and auto-converts
  • Completing a task in Akiflow does not always mark it complete in the source app (two-way sync gaps reported in reviews)
  • The AI Meeting Assistant (recording, transcripts, summaries, action items) is a separate $19/mo add-on, not included in Pro
  • Team pricing is by quote only (no published per-seat rate), and the company holds no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification
