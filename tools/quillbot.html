QuillBot Review 2026

All-in-one AI writing suite — paraphraser, grammar, plagiarism and AI detection — wherever you type.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Students, professionals, and content creators write in dozens of apps every day, and awkward phrasing, grammar slips, and accidental plagiarism quietly damage credibility. QuillBot is an all-in-one AI writing suite — paraphraser, grammar checker, plagiarism checker, AI detector, AI humanizer, summarizer, and AI chat — that works wherever you type through browser extensions, desktop, and mobile apps. It rewrites text in your voice across 9 paraphrasing modes and 23 languages, so everyday writing comes out clear, original, and professional without opening a separate app.

Official site: https://quillbot.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Genuinely useful free tier: paraphrase up to 125 words, 2 modes, basic grammar fixes, and limited AI Detector access
  • Full writing suite in one subscription — paraphrasing, grammar, plagiarism checking, AI detection, humanizing, summarizing, citations, and translation into 40+ languages
  • Works everywhere: Chrome/Edge extensions, macOS app, Word add-in, Android/iOS keyboards and apps
  • Massive scale and trust: 35+ million writers worldwide, 180+ countries, 4.5/5 Chrome extension rating, 4.7/5 overall

❌ Cons

  • It polishes and rewrites existing text — it is not a long-form content generator for drafting articles from scratch
  • Free tier is tightly capped (125-word paraphrase limit, 2 modes, limited humanizer and AI Detector usage)
  • Premium's headline price ($8.33/month) requires annual billing; the month-to-month rate was not shown on the pricing page
  • AI Detector and Humanizer results are probabilistic and should not be treated as proof of authorship
