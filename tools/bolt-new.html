Bolt.new Review 2026

Describe an app in plain language and get a working full-stack app running in the browser.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Non-technical founders and product teams have ideas but no fast way to turn them into working software — hiring developers or stitching together hosting, databases, and deployment is slow and expensive. Bolt.new lets anyone describe an app in plain language and get a working full-stack web or mobile app running in the browser within minutes, with hosting and backend included.

Official site: https://bolt.new/

Use cases for US teams: Startup founders validating an MVP — prompt-to-prototype in an afternoon and share a live link with users and investors before hiring engineers. Product and marketing teams shipping landing pages and internal tools without waiting on engineering sprint capacity.

✅ Pros

  • Full-stack in the browser — a real Node.js environment via WebContainers; no local setup, instant preview, one-click deploy, with hosting, databases, and auth built in
  • Genuinely fast prototyping — chat-to-working-app for websites, web apps, and mobile apps (Expo), plus GitHub and Figma imports and direct code editing
  • Generous free tier — 1M tokens/month with no card required, so US startups can validate an idea before paying
  • Automatic model routing and self-correction — Bolt picks the right model per task and tests/refactors to reduce errors

❌ Cons

  • Token metering is hard to budget — Bolt re-reads project files on every message, so costs rise as projects grow
  • JavaScript-only backends; limited control for teams needing other stacks or deep custom infrastructure
  • Subscriptions are non-refundable, and consumed AI usage stays non-refundable even if the output disappoints

Pricing

Prices verified directly on bolt.new/pricing. The $18/mo yearly Pro figure and $27/member/mo yearly Teams figure are corroborated from multiple Nov 2026 reads of the same page — confirm on the official page before buying.

Verdict

Best for founders validating an MVP in an afternoon. Free (1M tokens/mo) is enough to test a real build; Pro at $25/mo is the value tier — watch token burn as projects grow.
