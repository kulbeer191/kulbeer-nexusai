Which one should you pick?

v0 by Vercel and Lovable both turn a plain-language prompt into a working web app, but they are built for different buyers. v0 is Vercel's AI assistant, strongest for React/Next.js teams already deploying on Vercel. Lovable is an AI full-stack engineer for non-technical founders, handling hosting, auth, payments and integrations end to end. This comparison uses pricing and features from both official sites to help US startups pick.

Verdict

Pick Lovable if you are a non-technical founder who wants a full product — hosting, auth and payments included — without touching code. Pick v0 if you are a React/Next.js team already on Vercel and want generated UI that drops into your existing workflow. For most US startups shipping an MVP from scratch, Lovable's Pro at $25/mo is the simpler buy; for Vercel teams, v0 Plus at $30/user/mo fits the stack you already run.
