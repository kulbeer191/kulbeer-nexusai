Overview

Professional writers — essayists, journalists, academics, and newsletter writers — need a word processor where AI helps revise and polish long-form prose inside the document itself. Lex pairs a clean, distraction-free editor with inline AI feedback, per-action model choice, and version control, so editing happens in the draft instead of in a separate chatbot window.

Official site: https://lex.page/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI feedback inline in the document — Ask Lex chat, line-level Checks (brevity, grammar, readability), and in-line AI comments
  • Choose frontier models per action (GPT-4.1/4o, Claude Sonnet and Opus on Pro) instead of being locked to one vendor
  • Version control with Rewind — scrub through and restore earlier versions like a video
  • Live collaboration, knowledge bases and style guides for teams; loved by over 300,000 academics (per lex.page homepage)

Is Lex free?

Yes — Lex has a free plan ($0): full editor and collaboration with limited AI usage (~30 AI checks/month, lower-tier models). No time limit. The entry paid plan is Pro at $18 / month.
