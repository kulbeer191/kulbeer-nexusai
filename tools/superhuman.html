Superhuman Review 2026

Keyboard-first AI email client with auto-drafts, Split Inbox and AI docs.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Knowledge workers lose 4+ hours a week to email triage, drafting, and scheduling back-and-forth. Superhuman is a keyboard-first, AI-powered email client for Gmail and Outlook that auto-drafts replies, surfaces what matters with Split Inbox, and schedules without double-booking — and now bundles AI docs and an in-app assistant (Go) under one login.

Official site: https://www.superhuman.com/

✅ Pros

  • Fastest-in-class, keyboard-driven email workflow with Auto Drafts and Ask AI that draft in your voice
  • Split Inbox and auto-labels surface important mail and filter noise automatically
  • One suite covers email, collaborative AI docs, writing help, and an AI assistant with Daily Briefs
  • Business tier adds CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive) from inside the inbox

❌ Cons

  • Premium pricing — no free tier for the Mail app itself, so teams pay from day one
  • Works only with Gmail / Google Workspace and Outlook / Office 365
  • Keyboard-first workflow has a learning curve for mouse-heavy users
  • The Grammarly → Superhuman rebrand and four-product suite can confuse buyers about what is included in each plan

Pricing

Superhuman Mail bought standalone: Starter $30/member/month monthly ($25 billed annually); Business $40 monthly ($33 billed annually). Prices change — always check the official site before buying. Prices below are the widely published plans at time of writing.

Best for: Best for Gmail/Outlook power users who live in email and want speed above all. Try the free Go tier first, then move to Business only if you need Mail and CRM in the inbox.
