Overview

US knowledge workers write everywhere — Gmail, Google Docs, Notion, LinkedIn, Slack — but AI help usually lives in a separate tab, so every draft means copy-paste and lost context. Clico is a Chromium extension (plus Web and Mac desktop app) that puts ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 30+ other models into every text box: select text, hit a shortcut, and it drafts, rewrites, summarizes or searches in place, with no API key to set up — and the same workspace now covers chat, image, video, music, websites and browser games under one credit balance.

Official site: https://tryclico.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI in every text box — Chrome extension for Gmail, Google Docs, Notion, LinkedIn, Slack, Canva
  • 34 chat models mixable in one workspace with Auto model selection, no API keys
  • 22 image and 27 video models included on paid plans
  • Draft posts/replies, continue writing in your own style, summarize pages, dictate by voice
  • Web, Chrome Extension and Desktop for Mac under one credit balance

✅ Pros

  • AI that lives where you type — Chrome extension works in Gmail, Google Docs, Notion, LinkedIn, Slack, Canva and every text box; draft posts/replies, continue writing in your own style, summarize pages, dictate by voice, no tab-switching or copy-paste
  • One subscription, 34 chat models — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Kimi and more, mixable in one workspace with Auto model selection; no API keys to manage; 22 image and 27 video models included on paid plans
  • Real free plan — $0 forever, no card required, 100 credits a month, 8 chat models + selected image models, across Web, Chrome Extension and Desktop for Mac
  • Strongest affiliate terms in the directory so far — 50% of each referred sale for one year (via Dub), and every referred customer gets 20% off their first purchase

❌ Cons

  • It has pivoted beyond writing — the homepage now leads with product videos, short dramas, images, websites and browser games; the writing-extension story that made it distinct is now one surface among many, so the review must present it honestly as a creative studio with a writing extension, not a pure writing tool
  • Credit-metered everything — one shared balance for chat, images, video and websites; video generation burns credits fast, unused credits do not roll over, and the free 100 credits a month is thin for daily use
  • Free-plan limits moved recently — sources from Sept-Oct 2026 list 200 free credits a month; the official pricing page now lists 100 a month. Buyers should check their account balance
  • No team features — single-user plans only, no workspaces, shared billing or admin controls; it is a personal tool, not a team rollout

Frequently asked questions

Is Clico free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever, no card required, with 100 credits a month, 8 chat models and selected image models across Web, Chrome Extension and Desktop for Mac. Note the free allowance just dropped from 200 to 100 credits a month. Paid plans start at Pro, $19.90 a month ($16 billed annually).

What is Clico best for?

US knowledge workers who want AI in every text box with 34 models under one subscription — a sales/marketing rep drafting LinkedIn posts, email replies and Slack messages in place, a content creator turning a page into a summary or first draft with one shortcut, or a student writing essays across Google Docs with model choice and no API keys.

What should I watch out for with Clico?

It has pivoted into a broader creative studio, so the writing extension is now one surface among many. Credits are shared across everything and do not roll over, video generation burns them fast, and there are no team features — single-user plans only.

Verdict

Best for: US knowledge workers who want AI in every text box with 34 models under one subscription — with the warning that it has pivoted into a broader creative studio, credits are shared across everything and don't roll over, and the free allowance just dropped from 200 to 100 credits a month.
