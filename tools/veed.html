Overview

US marketing teams need captioned, on-brand video for every channel, but desktop editors are slow to learn and freelancers are expensive. VEED is a browser-based AI video editor that generates, cuts, subtitles, translates and dubs video in one workflow, so a small team can ship feed-ready video the same day without installing anything.

Official site: https://www.veed.io/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Full video editor in the browser — no install
  • Auto-subtitles in 50+ languages, translation and AI dubbing
  • Eye Contact AI, Clean Audio, background removal, AI avatars, AI b-roll
  • Templates and brand kits for consistent marketing output
  • iOS app on paid plans

✅ Pros

  • Full editor in the browser — no install, works on any machine; iOS app on paid plans
  • Auto-subtitles in 50+ languages, plus translation and AI dubbing (minute-based)

Frequently asked questions

Is VEED free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and lets you test the editor — but exports are watermarked 720p with a 10-minute limit, so it is not usable for client work. Paid plans start at Creator, $12.25 a seat a month billed yearly ($25 billed monthly).

What is VEED best for?

US marketing teams and creators who want browser-based video editing with auto-subtitles, translation and AI cleanup in one seat — a marketing team turning webinars into captioned social cuts the same day, an L&D team localizing training videos, or creators publishing same-day UGC ads.

What should I watch out for with VEED?

Per-seat pricing multiplies fast for teams, and AI features run on monthly credits that don't carry over. Pricing figures also differ across sources — the tier names changed in 2026 — so confirm the current price on the official site before buying.

Verdict

Best for: US marketing teams and creators who want browser-based video editing with auto-subtitles, translation and AI cleanup in one seat — with the warning that per-seat pricing multiplies fast for teams and AI features run on monthly credits that don't carry over.
