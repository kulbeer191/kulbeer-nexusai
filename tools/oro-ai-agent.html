Oro AI Agent Review 2026

Autonomous Sales, Support, Marketing and Operations agents that answer, qualify and follow up across Instagram, Messenger, TikTok and more from one shared memory — a 24/7 agent team for US small businesses, starting free.

Overview

US small businesses lose sales in their DMs — customers message on Instagram, Messenger and TikTok at all hours and expect instant replies, but a small team cannot staff every channel 24/7. Oro AI Agent deploys autonomous Sales, Support, Marketing and Operations agents that answer, qualify, follow up and report across every channel from one shared memory — no code and no extra hires.

Official site: https://oroshin.site

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Autonomous Sales, Support, Marketing and Operations agents
  • One shared memory and persona across Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, Telegram and Google Business Profile
  • Agents run on schedules and react to events without human prompting
  • 300 free credits every day on the Free plan, no card required
  • 200+ integrations including Stripe, Notion and GitHub

✅ Pros

  • Genuine free plan — 300 credits every day, all channels included, no card — rare for a truly autonomous agent
  • Covers the channels US small businesses actually live on (Facebook Messenger, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, Google Business Profile) with one shared memory and persona, so a customer can switch channels mid-conversation without losing the thread
  • Truly autonomous and no-code: agents run on schedules and react to events on their own — auto-posting, following up quiet leads, verifying payment slips, sending the weekly report — while the owner only approves what matters
  • Cheap paid tiers (from $4.99 a month) with no per-seat and no per-channel charges

❌ Cons

  • Very young, small company (founded 2024, based in Bangkok) — thinner track record, support scale and longevity than US incumbents
  • Management app is iPhone/iPad only (App Store); no Android app is mentioned on the official site
  • Credit-metered on every plan — heavy messaging volume burns through the daily/monthly credits fast, and the free 300 a day is shared across all agents

Frequently asked questions

Is Oro AI Agent free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 300 credits every day across all channels, with no card required. Paid plans start at $4.99 a month (Starter) and add monthly credits on top of the daily 300.

What is Oro AI Agent best for?

US small businesses and DTC brands that live in their DMs — the Sales Agent qualifies buyers and follows up quiet prospects on Instagram and Facebook after hours, the Support Agent handles booking and order-status questions, and the Marketing Agent schedules posts and runs comment-reply campaigns.

What should I watch out for with Oro AI Agent?

It is a very young company (founded 2024, based in Bangkok) with a thinner track record than US incumbents, the management app is iPhone/iPad only, and every plan is credit-metered — heavy messaging volume burns credits fast.

Verdict

Best for: US small businesses and DTC brands that live in their DMs and want a 24/7 agent team for the price of a coffee — with the warning that the company is young and every plan is credit-metered.
