Midjourney Review 2026

High-quality image generation for brand, marketing and concept art. Best-in-class style control for creative teams.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Stock photos look generic and custom shoots are slow and expensive. Midjourney turns a text prompt into distinctive, on-brand imagery in minutes — with the strongest style control of any image generator — so marketing and creative teams can produce campaign art, concept work and product visuals without a shoot.

Official site: https://www.midjourney.com

✅ Pros

  • Best-in-class image quality and style control
  • Relax mode gives unlimited images on Standard and above
  • Now includes video generation (SD on Basic, HD from Standard)
  • Commercial use included on all paid plans

❌ Cons

  • No free plan or trial — $10/mo minimum to try it
  • Discord-based workflow can feel clunky for new users
  • Companies over $1M revenue must be on Pro or Mega
  • Video is limited compared with dedicated video tools

Pricing

Prices change — always check the official site before buying. Prices below are the widely published plans at time of writing. Prices exclude taxes.

Verdict

Best for: creative and marketing teams that need distinctive, on-brand imagery fast. Standard at $30/mo is the sweet spot — unlimited Relax images change the economics.
