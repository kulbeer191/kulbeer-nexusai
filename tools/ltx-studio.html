LTX Studio Review 2026

A script-to-screen AI video studio — script, storyboard, shot-by-shot generation and timeline editing in one browser workspace, with characters, objects and locations staying consistent across every shot.

Overview

US film, agency and brand teams waste days turning a script into shootable video. LTX Studio takes a script or idea through storyboard, shot-by-shot AI generation and timeline editing in one browser workspace, with characters, objects and locations staying consistent across every shot — a production workflow, not a one-clip generator. Built on the open-weight LTX-2.5 model (October 2026, up to 4K, video + audio in a single pass); spun out of Lightricks as a separate unit in June 2026.

Official site: https://ltx.io/studio

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Script to storyboard to generated shots to timeline edit in one workspace
  • Reusable Elements keep characters, objects and locations consistent across shots
  • LTX-2.5 open-weight model (up to 4K, video + audio in one pass) plus partner models on Standard/Pro
  • Keyframed camera motion, audio-to-video, video-to-video control, SDR-to-HDR enhancement
  • AI Storyboards, Pitch Decks and Flows node-based automation

✅ Pros

  • True production workflow — script to storyboard to generated shots to timeline edit, plus pitch decks and AI storyboards
  • Reusable 'Elements' keep the same character, object or location consistent across every shot
  • Built on the open-weight LTX-2.5 model (up to 4K, video + audio in a single pass); Standard/Pro add partner models — Kling 3.0 Pro, Seedance 2.0, Veo 3.1, FLUX.2 Pro, Nano Banana 2, ChatGPT Images 2.0
  • Director-grade controls: keyframed camera motion, audio-to-video, video-to-video control, SDR-to-HDR enhancement, Flows node-based automation

❌ Cons

  • The credit system is opaque — the pricing page never states how many credits a second of video costs, so the real cost per clip is unknowable before generating
  • Free and $15 Lite are personal-use only (no business, marketing or paid work) — commercial rights start at Standard ($35 a month)
  • Desktop browser only; unused credits expire each cycle; mixed user reviews (G2 4.2/5 vs Trustpilot 1.6)

Frequently asked questions

Is LTX Studio free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 800 one-time credits for LTX-2.5 video, image generation, camera controls and Flows — but it is personal-use only. Commercial work needs Standard ($35 a month) or higher.

What is LTX Studio best for?

US agencies storyboarding and producing AI commercials with consistent brand characters across shots, indie filmmakers and faceless YouTube channels producing multi-shot short films from a script, and brand studios creating multi-language video versions and client pitch decks.

What should I watch out for with LTX Studio?

The credit system never states how many credits a second of video costs, so the real cost per clip is unknowable before generating. Free and Lite are personal-use only, and unused credits expire each cycle.

Verdict

Best for: US agencies, filmmakers and brand teams that want a script-to-screen production studio rather than a clip generator — with the warning that the credit meter never tells you what a second of video costs, and anything commercial needs Standard ($35 a month) or higher.
