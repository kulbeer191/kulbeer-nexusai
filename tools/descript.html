Descript Review 2026

Edit audio and video by editing a transcript — the all-in-one studio for podcasts, YouTube and team video.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Editing speech is slow because you scrub through waveforms. Descript transcribes your recording and lets you cut, rearrange and clean it up like a document — delete a sentence and the audio is gone — then adds Studio Sound, filler-word removal, screen recording and publishing, so one app covers record to release.

Official site: https://descript.com

✅ Pros

  • Transcript-based editing is genuinely faster than timeline editing for speech
  • Studio Sound, filler-word removal and Underlord AI tools clean up recordings automatically
  • Recording, editing, publishing and hosting in one place
  • 4K export on Creator and Business, with team seats

❌ Cons

  • Pricing is metered twice — media hours and AI credits — so the real cost is hard to size
  • Hobbyist cannot buy top-ups when its 10 media hours run out
  • Per-person pricing adds up fast for teams
  • Heavy projects can feel slow on long timelines

Verdict
