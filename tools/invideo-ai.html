Overview

Small US businesses and marketers need a steady stream of finished videos for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and YouTube, but hiring editors or learning Premiere is slow and expensive. InVideo AI turns a prompt, script, or blog post into a finished video — scenes, visuals, voiceover, captions, and music — then lets teams polish it in a full multiplayer timeline editor with AI agents doing the tedious work.

Official site: https://invideo.io/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • True prompt-to-finished-video: script writing, storyboarding, scene visuals, voiceover, captions, and music in one pass, plus a full timeline editor (multiplayer, color/audio/3D tools)
  • Access to strong current models in one credit system: Seedance 2.5 (1080p), Seedance 2.0 (4K), Nano Banana Pro images, GPT Image 2.5, plus custom AI agents (cinematographer, music designer)
  • Per-seat pricing with free guest seats (10 on Plus, 20 on Max) and real-time collaboration — built for small teams, not solo only
  • Trusted by teams at Google, NVIDIA, Salesforce, Meta, Netflix, and HubSpot; SOC 2 and GDPR compliant
