Reclaim AI Review 2026

AI calendar that auto-defends focus time, reschedules conflicts, and syncs Google and Outlook calendars.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

US knowledge workers lose their week to meeting sprawl — calendars fill with back-to-back calls, focus work gets squeezed into the margins, and tasks slip. Reclaim is an AI calendar that sits on top of Google Calendar or Outlook and automatically defends focus time, schedules tasks and habits, and reshuffles meetings when conflicts appear, so the week reflects priorities instead of whoever booked first.

Official site: https://reclaim.ai

✅ Pros

  • Auto-defends focus time, habits, lunch, and buffers around meetings, and auto-reschedules conflicts as the week shifts
  • Scheduling Links + calendar sync across multiple Google and Outlook calendars, so personal commitments stay protected on the work schedule
  • AI assistant (LLM chat) plus specialized AI Agents (Focus Time, Habits, Buffer Time, Smart Meetings) that continuously optimize the schedule
  • Team features on paid tiers: Team OOO Calendar, delegated access for EAs, focus-time OKRs and executive reports

❌ Cons

  • Free Lite tier is narrow: 1-week scheduling range, 1 calendar sync, 1 scheduling link, limited integrations
  • Only Google Calendar and Outlook are supported — no native Apple Calendar
  • Prices rose in late 2025 (Starter $8 → $10, Business $12 → $15 annual) and Enterprise requires a 100-seat minimum
