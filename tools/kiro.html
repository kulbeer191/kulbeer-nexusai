Overview

Vibe-coding tools write code first and explain later, leaving teams with unplanned, unreviewable work. Kiro is AWS’s agentic AI engineering system that turns a plain prompt into a written spec — requirements, design and tasks — before it writes any code, so teams get structured, reviewable work instead. It plans, builds, reviews and verifies real engineering work across an IDE, CLI, web app, mobile app and persistent cloud agents (Kiro Crew), so long tasks keep running in sandboxes while developers are away.

Official site: https://kiro.dev/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Spec-driven development — every feature starts as requirements, design and tasks you review before code is written
  • Works everywhere from one subscription — IDE, CLI, web, mobile app and Kiro Crew persistent agents, plus CI/CD automation
  • Frontier model choice with an Auto router — Claude (Opus 5, Sonnet 5/4.6/4.5, Haiku 4.5), OpenAI GPT-5.6 and open-weight models (DeepSeek 3.2, Qwen3 Coder Next, MiniMax, GLM-5)
  • Genuine perpetual free tier — 50 credits/month, no card — and team credit pooling ($20/seat/month, shared pool)

❌ Cons

  • Credit pricing is hard to predict — different models burn credits at different multipliers (a task on Sonnet 4.6 costs 1.3× the Auto router; top models up to ~6×), so a $20 Pro plan’s real capacity varies a lot
  • No annual billing discount — monthly prices only, billed on the 1st; unused monthly credits do not roll over
  • Free and individual-tier prompts may be used to train models unless you pay for Enterprise (reported by third-party reviewers, not stated on the pricing page)
