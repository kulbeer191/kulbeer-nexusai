Overview

Type is an AI-native document editor built for long-form writing. Instead of pasting text back and forth into a chatbot, writers draft, rewrite and revise whole documents up to 150,000 words inside one editor, with the AI remembering characters, story details and world notes across the manuscript. It targets the gap between generic chatbots (which lose context on long documents) and plain word processors (which offer no AI help at all).

Official site: https://type.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Long-form editor for documents up to 150,000 words
  • Notes memory that keeps characters, story and world details consistent
  • Word and PDF import/export with images, headings and tables preserved
  • Privacy-first: no AI training on user data
  • Free plan with credits that never expire; 30-day money-back guarantee on the first paid plan

✅ Pros

  • Built for genuinely long documents — works on manuscripts up to 150,000 words per document, with import/export of Word docs and PDFs (images, headings and tables preserved)
  • 'Notes' memory — the editor remembers characters, story and world details and factors them into every AI suggestion, so long-form work stays consistent
  • Privacy-first — Type states it does not train AI models on user data; all uploads and documents stay private
  • Real free plan — $0 with a fixed credit allowance that never expires, and every feature is tryable before paying; 30-day money-back guarantee on the first paid subscription

❌ Cons

  • Credit-metered AI — every AI feature burns credits, monthly credits reset and do not roll over, and heavy AI users can hit the cap mid-month (extra credits are a separate purchase)
  • Narrowly positioned for long-form creative writing (books, novels, screenplays) — overkill and poorly suited to quick marketing copy or short business writing
  • No team/enterprise tier on the public pricing page — priced per individual, so a US content team has no shared workspace or admin controls to buy into
  • Pricing just moved — the annual prices on the live page differ from every third-party tracker checked 10-20 Nov 2026, so buyers must confirm at checkout

Frequently asked questions

Is Type free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with a limited fixed credit allowance that never expires, and every feature is tryable before paying. Paid plans start at Basic, $12 a month ($6 a month billed annually).

What is Type best for?

Anyone doing serious long-form writing that needs deep, context-aware editing — authors and screenwriters drafting full manuscripts with an AI that remembers plot and character details, and content teams drafting long-form reports, white papers or guides without losing context across 50+ page documents.

What should I watch out for with Type?

The credit meter: every AI feature burns credits, monthly credits reset and do not roll over, and heavy AI use can get expensive beyond the monthly allowance. Prices also just changed (annual $8/$16/$64 became $6/$12/$48 around 21-22 Nov 2026), so confirm at checkout.

Verdict

Best for: anyone doing serious long-form writing that needs deep, context-aware editing — with the warning that the credit meter makes heavy AI use expensive beyond the monthly allowance.
