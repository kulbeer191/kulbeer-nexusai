Granola Review 2026

AI notepad that captures meetings from your device — no bot joins the call.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US professionals spend their days in back-to-back meetings and lose the thread between calls — notes get skipped, action items are forgotten, and follow-ups slip. Granola is an AI notepad that captures meetings from your own device, with no bot joining the call, and turns them into clear notes, action items, and follow-ups the moment the meeting ends, with a searchable memory of every conversation.

Official site: https://www.granola.ai/

Use cases for US teams: Sales and customer-success teams: capture client calls without a bot in the room and get action items and follow-up emails ready instantly. Founders and executives in back-to-back meetings: pre-meeting briefs plus a searchable memory of every conversation, so nothing from last week's call is lost.

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • No bot joins your calls — capture runs on your own device, so there is no awkward "recording bot" participant to admit or announce
  • Unlimited meeting notes on the free Basic plan, with action items and follow-up emails ready the moment a meeting ends
  • Pre-meeting briefs from calendar sync plus Granola Chat and MCP/API, so notes flow into Claude, ChatGPT, Notion, HubSpot, Slack, and Zapier
  • Works beyond video calls — in-person meetings and coffee catch-ups via the mobile apps; SOC 2 Type II certified

❌ Cons

  • The free Basic plan only shows the last 30 days of notes — full history is paywalled behind Business
  • No audio playback: audio is deleted after transcription, so you cannot re-listen to a call
  • It cannot capture a meeting you do not attend — there is no bot/recorder option for calls you miss
  • Anonymized data is used for model training unless you opt out in Settings (off by default only on Enterprise)
