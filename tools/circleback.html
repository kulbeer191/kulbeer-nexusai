Circleback Review 2026

An AI meeting notetaker that records every meeting, writes meticulous notes with speaker-labeled transcripts, auto-assigns action items, and pushes the outcomes into the tools the team already uses — with a bot-free desktop app option and a genuinely generous free plan.

Overview

Teams lose hours every week to meeting admin — someone has to take notes, chase action items, and remember what was decided across dozens of calls. Circleback records every meeting, writes meticulous notes with speaker-labeled transcripts, auto-assigns action items, and then pushes the outcomes into the tools the team already uses, so nobody has to be the note-taker and nothing agreed in a call falls through the cracks.

Official site: https://circleback.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI meeting notes with speaker-labeled transcripts and auto-assigned action items
  • Bot-free desktop app that captures shared screens into the notes
  • Automations to Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Slack and webhooks
  • Ask Circleback — search across every meeting ever captured
  • Imports past meetings from Fathom, Fireflies, Granola and Read AI

✅ Pros

  • Generous real free plan — unlimited meetings and unlimited members, with no minute or meeting caps (only history and automations are limited)
  • Bot-free option: the desktop app for macOS/Windows records without a bot joining the call, and it captures what's shared on screen (slides, dashboards, docs) into the notes
  • Automations are the differentiator — paid plans push notes and action items to Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Slack, and webhooks automatically, plus an API, MCP, and CLI
  • Ask Circleback searches across every meeting ever captured, turning past calls into a searchable 'company brain'
  • Works everywhere: Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, WebEx, Slack huddles, in-person meetings via mobile/Apple Watch apps, 100+ languages, and it imports past meetings from Fathom, Fireflies, Granola, and Read AI. No customer data used to train models

❌ Cons

  • Free plan keeps only 30 days of meeting/recording history and limited automations — the CRM and workflow automations that justify the tool need Pro
  • Per-seat pricing adds up fast for large teams (Business $25/user/mo); the value is in the automations, so teams that only want transcripts overpay
  • Bot-free recording requires installing the desktop app; the bot mode announces a participant in the call
  • Recording storage is capped on paid plans (1 year on Pro, 2 years on Business; unlimited only on Enterprise)
  • No built-in video conferencing — it always rides on another meeting platform

Frequently asked questions

Is Circleback free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with unlimited meetings and unlimited members, no minute or meeting caps — only history (30 days) and automations are limited. Paid plans start at Pro, $15 a user a month billed annually.

What is Circleback best for?

Sales teams whose call notes and action items flow straight into HubSpot or Salesforce after every call; founders and ops leads building a searchable record of every meeting; and remote/async teams where people skip meetings they don't need and catch up from the notes (on Business, teammates who only read notes are free unlimited viewers, not paid seats).

What should I watch out for with Circleback?

The free plan keeps only 30 days of meeting and recording history and limited automations — the CRM and workflow automations that justify the tool need Pro. And per-seat pricing adds up fast for large teams, so teams that only want transcripts overpay.

Verdict

Best for: Teams that want meeting notes to land in their CRM automatically — with the warning that the automations that justify the tool need Pro and per-seat pricing adds up fast.
