Bardeen Review 2026

Overview

US sales, GTM and ops teams lose hours every week to repetitive browser work — sourcing leads, copying data between tabs, enriching CRM records, chasing follow-ups. Bardeen runs as a Chrome extension with an AI agent layer: describe the outcome in plain language and it builds the automation, scraping, enriching, qualifying and moving data across 100+ apps with no code. It turns manual web research and data entry into a repeatable, credit-metered workflow.

Official site: https://www.bardeen.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • No-code AI agent builder with 1,000+ pre-built automations
  • Agentic web scraper with AI enrichment and qualification
  • 100 free credits every month, forever; utilities and CSV import/export free
  • 100+ app integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Sheets, Airtable and Notion
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CASA Tier 2 & 3 certified

✅ Pros

  • No-code AI agent builder plus 1,000+ pre-built automations — describe the outcome and Bardeen assembles the steps
  • Agentic web scraper with AI enrichment and qualification built in — sources leads, verifies emails, qualifies with AI, exports to Google Sheets or a CRM
  • Genuine free allowance — 100 credits free every month, forever, and imports/exports plus utilities cost nothing
  • Enterprise-grade security — SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CASA Tier 2 & 3 certified; Deel's Sales and Ops teams are the featured customer

❌ Cons

  • Credit-metered pricing is easy to misjudge — enrichment costs 3 credits per row, so enriching a few thousand records burns through Premium's 1,000 credits fast
  • Chrome-extension only — little value for teams that do not live in the browser; no native desktop app
  • The Premium tier's details are thin on the official pricing page itself — confirm at checkout

Frequently asked questions

Is Bardeen free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with 100 credits every month, forever — scraper, web-search and AI-tool rows cost 1 credit each, enrichment rows cost 3 credits each, and utilities plus CSV import/export are free. Unused credits expire at the end of the billing period.

What is Bardeen best for?

US sales, GTM and ops teams drowning in browser-based research and data entry — build a targeted lead list, scrape and enrich it, verify emails, qualify with AI and push it to Salesforce, HubSpot or Google Sheets, all without code.

What should I watch out for with Bardeen?

The credit math: enrichment at 3 credits per row means real monthly volume can push costs well past the $10 headline price. It is Chrome-extension only, and the Premium tier's details are thin on the official pricing page — confirm at checkout.

Verdict

Best for: US sales, GTM and ops teams drowning in browser-based research and data entry — with the warning that the credit math is easy to misjudge: enrichment at 3 credits per row means real volume can push costs well past the $10 headline price.
