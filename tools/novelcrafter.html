Overview

Novelists juggling a manuscript in one app, a story bible in another, and AI chats in a third lose continuity — the AI forgets characters, contradicts lore, and every draft starts from zero context. Novelcrafter is a novel-writing workspace built around the Codex, a structured story bible of characters, places, and lore that automatically feeds the relevant context into every AI generation, so the assistant actually knows the writer's world. Its AI is bring-your-own-key, so authors choose the model and keep control of their data.

Official site: https://novelcrafter.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Codex story bible — characters, places and lore auto-fed into every generation
  • Bring-your-own-key AI across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, OpenRouter
  • Local models via Ollama/LM Studio
  • Workshop Chat for structured, context-aware feedback
  • Collaboration for co-authors, proofreaders and teams

✅ Pros

  • The Codex is the standout: it auto-detects mentions, tracks how characters/places change across the story, and can be shared across books in a series
  • Bring-your-own-key AI — OpenAI (GPT-5), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Meta (Llama), Mistral, OpenRouter (300+ models), plus local models via Ollama/LM Studio. No vendor lock-in
  • Honest pricing: the pricing page states 'We don't do sales' — no fake countdowns or inflated prices, and a 21-day full-feature trial with no credit card
  • Collaboration built in (co-authors, proofreaders, teams) on the Specialist plan
  • Privacy policy states the company does not use authors' novels or creative content to train AI models

❌ Cons

  • No built-in AI: from the $8/mo Hobbyist plan up you connect your own API key and pay the model provider separately on top of the subscription
  • No free plan — only the 21-day trial (verified on the official pricing page FAQ: 'Is there a free plan? No')
  • Bring-your-own-key setup is technical (API keys, model choice, token costs) and will put off non-technical writers
  • No unified team billing — each collaborator needs their own paid account and subscription
  • Small independent team with no venture funding, so long-term support depends on the subscription base

Frequently asked questions

Is Novelcrafter free?

No. The official pricing page FAQ says 'Is there a free plan? No' — only a 21-day full-feature trial with no credit card. Paid plans start at Scribe, $4 a month (corroborated — see pricing note).

What is Novelcrafter best for?

An indie author or co-author pair keeping a multi-book series consistent through a shared Codex instead of scattered notes and wikis; a game studio's narrative team managing a lore bible and drafting AI-assisted scenes using its own Claude/OpenAI keys, keeping IP off third-party training; or a writing coach or classroom using Workshop Chat for structured, context-aware feedback.

What should I watch out for with Novelcrafter?

There is no built-in AI — from the $8/mo Hobbyist plan up you connect your own API key and pay the model provider separately on top of the subscription. And bring-your-own-key setup is technical (API keys, model choice, token costs) and will put off non-technical writers.

Verdict

Best for: Novelists who want an AI that actually knows their world through the Codex — with the warning that there is no free plan and no built-in AI: you pay your model provider on top.
