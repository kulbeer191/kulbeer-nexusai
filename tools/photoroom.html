Photoroom Review 2026

An AI visual editor built for e-commerce — upload a product photo and it generates marketplace-ready listing images with background removal, AI backgrounds, fashion models and batch processing across whole catalogs, plus video ads on higher plans.

Overview

US e-commerce sellers and DTC brands need marketplace-ready product photos at volume — clean cutouts, studio-style backgrounds, model shots, consistent brand visuals — without a photo shoot or a designer for every SKU. Photoroom is an AI visual editor built for e-commerce: upload a product photo and it generates listing images (and, on higher plans, video ads) with background removal, AI backgrounds, fashion models and batch processing across whole catalogs. Founded 2019 (Paris), launched 2020; background remover trusted by 80M+ users; SOC 2 Type 2, GDPR compliant.

Official site: https://www.photoroom.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI product photography with background removal and AI backgrounds
  • AI Fashion Models and batch processing across whole catalogs
  • Unlimited core tools on paid plans (no credit cost, fair-use limits apply)
  • Video Ad Maker — product photos into video ads for Meta, TikTok, YouTube
  • Shopify integration on Max and above; SOC 2 Type 2, GDPR compliant

✅ Pros

  • Purpose-built for e-commerce product visuals — AI product photography, AI backgrounds, AI fashion models and batch processing across whole catalogs, with a Shopify integration on Max and above
  • Core tools are unlimited on paid plans with no credit cost — background remover, retouch, AI shadows (presets), AI Fill, AI Expand/Resize and Image Enhancer use no AI credits (fair-use limits apply)
  • Real free plan ($0) plus a 7-day trial on yearly Pro/Max; SOC 2 Type 2 audited, GDPR compliant, built on GCP/AWS/Cloudflare
  • One subscription covers listing images AND ad video — Video Ad Maker (product photos into video ads with actors, music and voiceover for Meta, TikTok, YouTube) is included on Max (up to 50 generations) and Ultra (up to 150)

❌ Cons

  • Free plan is personal-use only — any seller needs a paid plan, and the free plan's exact limits are not published on the pricing page
  • Credit-metered on top of the subscription — AI credits cap at 8,000 / 25,000 / 75,000 per month and video ads burn them fast; exports are also capped (1,000 / 3,000 / 10,000 per month)
  • No Video Generator on Pro — video unlocks only at Max ($34.99 a month), and Ultra is a steep $99 a month
  • Refunds only up to 1 week after purchase, and the API is a separate paid plan — app plans include no API allowance

Frequently asked questions

Is Photoroom free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with limited features, AI credits and exports (25 exports a week) — but it is personal, non-commercial use only. Any seller needs a paid plan, starting at Pro, $12.99 a month.

What is Photoroom best for?

US e-commerce sellers and DTC brands that need marketplace-ready product photos at volume — batch background removal, AI backgrounds and brand templates across hundreds of SKUs, model shots and lifestyle images without a photo shoot, and video ads for Meta, TikTok and YouTube on Max/Ultra.

What should I watch out for with Photoroom?

The free plan is personal-use only, video unlocks only at Max ($34.99 a month), and the subscription is credit-metered on top — AI credits cap at 8,000 / 25,000 / 75,000 a month and video ads burn them fast.

Verdict

Best for: US e-commerce sellers and DTC brands that need marketplace-ready product photos at volume — with the warning that the free plan is personal-use only, and video unlocks only at Max ($34.99 a month).
