Overview

US teams lose hours writing meeting notes and chasing follow-ups, while decisions and action items stay buried in call recordings and never reach the CRM or task list. MeetGeek's AI meeting agent joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls — or records bot-free via a Chrome extension or desktop recorder — transcribes in 100+ languages, summarizes each meeting with decisions and action items, and pushes them automatically into HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Slack and task tools, so nobody has to write minutes or copy-paste into the CRM again.

Official site: https://meetgeek.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI meeting agent for Zoom, Google Meet and Teams, plus bot-free recording
  • Transcription in 100+ languages with automatic speaker recognition
  • AI summaries with decisions and action items, pushed to CRM and task tools
  • CRM sync (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive) plus Zapier, Make and n8n
  • Voice Agents (new in 2026) can attend meetings and respond on your behalf

✅ Pros

  • Real free plan: 3 hours of transcription a month, AI summaries, AI Chat and search across meetings, API & MCP access
  • Bot-free recording option (Chrome extension / desktop recorder / mobile app) — no awkward bot joining the call
  • CRM sync (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive) plus Zapier, Make and n8n automations from Pro — follow-ups flow into tools without copy-paste
  • 100+ languages, automatic speaker recognition, meeting templates and automatic meeting-type detection
  • Strong compliance on every plan: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 9001, GDPR and CCPA, HIPAA with a BAA on annual plans, EU or US data storage, and your data is never used to train AI models

❌ Cons

  • Free plan is thin: 3 hours a month, transcripts deleted after 3 months, audio after 1 month, and no exports or integrations
  • Pro caps transcription at 20 hours a month — a heavy meeting load forces Business at $27 monthly
  • Per-meeting recording caps (2 hours on Basic/Pro, 3 hours on Business) — long workshops get cut off
  • The pricing page defaults to the monthly view ($15.99/$27); the yearly rates ($9.99/$17) only appear on the yearly toggle, so the headline price is easy to misread

Frequently asked questions

Is MeetGeek free?

Yes. The Basic plan costs $0 forever with 3 hours of transcription a month, AI summaries and bot-free recording. Paid plans start at Pro, $15.99 a user a month billed monthly ($9.99 billed yearly, corroborated).

What is MeetGeek best for?

Sales and customer-success teams that need meeting notes to land in the CRM by themselves — a sales team auto-logging call summaries into HubSpot or Salesforce, a recruiting team sharing structured interview notes, or a customer-success team turning QBRs into tracked action items.

What should I watch out for with MeetGeek?

The free plan's 3-hour cap and 3-month transcript retention make it a trial, not an archive. And the pricing page defaults to the monthly view — the $9.99 yearly rate only appears on the yearly toggle.

Verdict

Best for: Sales and customer-success teams that need meeting notes to land in the CRM by themselves — with the warning that the free plan's 3-hour cap and 3-month transcript retention make it a trial, not an archive.
