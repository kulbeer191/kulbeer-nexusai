Poe Review 2026

Quora's multi-model chat hub — one account and one subscription that puts GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, image, video and voice models in a single chat app, so a team can test the same prompt across models and only pay for what it actually uses.

Overview

Teams and individuals who need several frontier AI models end up paying $20 a month each to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google separately, and still cannot compare answers side by side. Poe is Quora's multi-model chat hub — one account and one subscription that puts GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, image, video, and voice models in a single chat app, so a team can test the same prompt across models and only pay for what it actually uses.

Official site: https://poe.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and hundreds of models in one chat app
  • Side-by-side model comparison and group chat
  • Image, video and voice generation from the same subscription
  • Build and publish your own bots; API access on paid plans
  • Entry point at $4.99 a month

✅ Pros

  • One subscription unlocks GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and hundreds of other models plus image/video/voice generation — no need for three separate $20/mo subscriptions
  • Side-by-side model comparison in one interface, with group chat for team discussion
  • Very cheap entry point at $4.99 a month, and a free tier to try before paying
  • Users can build and publish their own bots, and paid plans include API access that spends from the same point balance
  • Actively maintained roster — the homepage currently lists GPT-6.2-Astra, Claude-Opus-5.5, Claude-Fable-5.2, Gemini-4-Neon, and Gemini-4-Hydrogen

❌ Cons

  • The points economy is confusing and unpredictable — in October 2026 Poe moved popular text models to per-token pricing and reviewers report the same message costing 2–3x the points it used to
  • The free tier is tiny (about 300 points a day per third-party trackers), so it is a trial in practice, not a usable free plan
  • Heavy users of one frontier model usually pay less subscribing to that model directly than burning Poe points on it
  • No team/business plan — it is a consumer product with per-user subscriptions

Frequently asked questions

Is Poe free?

Yes, with a small daily allowance — about 300 points a day per third-party trackers, though the official page does not spell out the free allowance, so treat the figure as corroborated, not official. In practice the free tier is a trial, not a usable plan; paid plans start at $4.99 a month.

What is Poe best for?

A content or marketing team drafting the same blog or ad copy in GPT, Claude, and Gemini from one account to pick the best output before committing to a single vendor; a product team A/B testing a support bot against several models without buying three subscriptions; or a small agency giving staff one multi-model account instead of separate ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, and Gemini Advanced seats.

What should I watch out for with Poe?

The points economy is confusing and unpredictable — in October 2026 Poe moved popular text models to per-token pricing and reviewers report the same message costing 2–3x the points it used to. Heavy users of one frontier model usually pay less subscribing to that model directly.

Verdict

Best for: Teams that want every frontier model in one chat app for the price of a coffee — with the warning that the points economy got 2–3x more expensive for popular models in October 2026.
