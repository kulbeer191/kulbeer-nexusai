Morgen Review 2026

A calendar and task planner that merges every calendar and task list into one view, then its AI Planner drafts the day for you — time-blocking tasks around meetings, flagging work at risk of running late, and re-planning when the day changes — while you stay in control of the final plan.

Overview

Knowledge workers juggle work calendars, personal calendars, and task lists across half a dozen apps, so days get double-booked, deadlines slip, and planning eats the morning. Morgen merges every calendar and task list into one view and its AI Planner drafts the day for you — time-blocking tasks around meetings, flagging work at risk of running late, and re-planning when the day changes — while you stay in control of the final plan.

Official site: https://morgen.so

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI Planner that drafts the day with time-blocking around meetings
  • Every calendar and task list in one view — Google, Outlook, Apple, Notion, Todoist
  • Built-in scheduling links and team availability
  • Calendar automations: sync, travel time, buffer time
  • Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android and web

✅ Pros

  • One plan, nothing held back: every feature (Planner, calendar sets, scheduling links, calendar sync, automations) is in the single Pro tier
  • AI Planner is assistive, not autonomous — it previews recommendations inside week templates ('Frames') you design, and you adjust or confirm before anything is scheduled
  • Unlimited calendar and task integrations on every tier with no per-connection metering — Google, Outlook, Apple, Fastmail, Notion, Todoist, and more
  • Built-in scheduling links and team availability replace a separate Calendly-style tool; calendar automations cover sync, travel time, and buffer time
  • Runs everywhere: Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, and web. Data stored in Switzerland/EU, never sold

❌ Cons

  • No free plan — only a 14-day full-access trial (no credit card); when the trial ends the app locks until you subscribe
  • Monthly billing doubles the price ($30/mo vs $15/mo annual) — the headline rate is only available yearly
  • The AI plans but doesn't execute on its own; unlike Motion it won't fully auto-schedule your day without your confirmation
  • Odd pricing quirk: the Team plan ($10/seat/mo annual) is cheaper per person than the individual Pro plan ($15/mo)
  • Smaller ecosystem than the native Google/Outlook calendars it sits on top of, and some organizations block third-party calendar tools, so IT approval can be a hurdle

Frequently asked questions

Is Morgen free?

No. There is no free plan — only a 14-day full-access trial with no credit card required. When the trial ends the app locks until you subscribe. Paid plans start at Pro, $15 a user a month billed yearly ($30 billed monthly).

What is Morgen best for?

Consultants and freelancers juggling work and personal calendars — one view of every calendar and task source, with tasks dragged onto the calendar as time blocks so nothing gets double-booked; small teams that want shared availability and booking links without a separate scheduling tool; and focus workers whose AI Planner drafts each day around deadlines and re-prioritizes when meetings move.

What should I watch out for with Morgen?

Monthly billing doubles the price ($30/mo vs $15/mo annual) — the headline rate is only available yearly. And the AI plans but doesn't execute on its own; unlike Motion it won't fully auto-schedule your day without your confirmation.

Verdict

Best for: Knowledge workers who want one view of every calendar and task list with an AI Planner drafting the day — with the warning that there is no free plan and monthly billing doubles the price.
