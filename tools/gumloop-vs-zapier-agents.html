Gumloop — Pros

  • Flat $37/mo with unlimited seats — the whole team on one price, no per-seat math
  • Any of 35+ AI models plus a visual canvas and chat-first builder, with guardrails, spend caps, and SOC 2 for enterprise control

Gumloop — Cons

  • No free plan — cheapest entry is a 14-day trial, then $37/mo whether you use it or not
  • Credit metering plus the 8% orchestration fee makes the real monthly cost hard to forecast

Zapier Agents — Pros

  • 9,000+ apps and no-code plain-language setup — the easiest on-ramp for non-technical teams
  • A real free tier (400 activities/mo) lets a team test a live agent before paying

Zapier Agents — Cons

  • Metered activities burn fast, and Agents is billed separately from Zap plans — two bills to manage
  • Still in beta and mid-transition to “AI by Zapier” (announced Oct 6, 2026), so confirm current status before buying

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Gumloop or Zapier Agents?

Gumloop's entry paid plan is $37/mo flat (Pro); Zapier Agents's is $50/mo ($33.33 billed annually, Pro). Gumloop is cheaper at the entry tier.

Which should I choose, Gumloop or Zapier Agents?

Pick Zapier Agents if your team already lives in Zapier and wants the cheapest no-code start — the free tier and 9,000-app coverage are unmatched for getting a first agent running this week.

What should I watch out for with Gumloop?

Credit burn is hard to predict — a heavy run can drain 20k credits fast; credits don’t roll over

Verdict

Pick Zapier Agents if your team already lives in Zapier and wants the cheapest no-code start — the free tier and 9,000-app coverage are unmatched for getting a first agent running this week. Pick Gumloop if you want agent-first workflows where the AI reasons and picks its own tools, with any frontier model and one flat price for the whole team — just budget for credits, because 20,000 a month disappears fast on heavy enrichment or research runs.
