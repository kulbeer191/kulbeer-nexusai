Overview

US teams run the same manual work across dozens of disconnected SaaS apps — copying data between a CRM, spreadsheets, email, and Slack. Make is a visual no-code platform that connects 3,500+ apps into automated workflows ('scenarios') and AI agents, so routine ops, marketing, and finance work runs itself without engineers or code.

Official site: https://www.make.com

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Visual drag-and-drop builder connecting 3,500+ apps
  • AI agents and AI toolkit built in (Make AI Agents, Maia)
  • Build by prompt, drag-and-drop, or from Claude/ChatGPT
  • Own AI provider connection on every paid plan since Nov 2025
  • Enterprise governance: SSO, role-based access, audit logs

✅ Pros

  • Visual drag-and-drop builder connects 3,500+ apps with no code; build by prompt, drag-and-drop, or from Claude/ChatGPT
  • Free plan with 1,000 credits a month, no credit card and no time limit — real automations run on free
  • AI agents and AI toolkit built in (Make AI Agents, Maia); since Nov 2025 every paid plan can use its own AI provider connection
  • Enterprise-grade governance: GDPR, SOC 2 Type II and SOC 3, SSO, role-based access, audit logs

❌ Cons

  • Credit-based billing is confusing and prices have moved repeatedly in Oct–Nov 2026; AI steps consume variable credits, so costs scale with usage
  • No usable free tier beyond 1,000 credits a month; heavy users hit overage costs fast
  • Complex scenarios (routers, error handling, data mapping) have a real learning curve versus simpler Zapier-style zaps

Frequently asked questions

Is Make free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 1,000 credits a month, 2 active scenarios and a 15-minute interval, with no credit card and no time limit — real automations run on free, though heavy users hit overage costs fast.

What is Make best for?

US ops, marketing and finance teams automating work across disconnected SaaS apps — syncing CRM deals to Slack and a tracker, drafting and approving social posts from new blog content, or pulling invoice PDFs from email into QuickBooks or Xero.

What should I watch out for with Make?

Credit-based billing is confusing and prices have moved repeatedly in Oct–Nov 2026, so confirm at checkout. Complex scenarios with routers, error handling and data mapping have a real learning curve versus simpler Zapier-style zaps.

Verdict

Best for: US ops, marketing and finance teams automating work across disconnected SaaS apps — syncing CRM deals, drafting and approving social posts, or pulling invoices into QuickBooks or Xero — without engineers or code.
