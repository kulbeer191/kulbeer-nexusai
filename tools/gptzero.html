GPTZero Review 2026

Overview

Teachers, publishers, recruiters and editors need to know whether a piece of writing was produced by AI — and students and writers need a trusted way to prove their work is their own. GPTZero is the leading AI detector: paste or upload text and get sentence-level AI probability scores, plagiarism checks, grammar feedback and authorship verification, built for education first. 20M+ users, 3,500+ colleges, official AI detector partner of the American Federation of Teachers; first US patent on AI detection.

Official site: https://gptzero.me/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Sentence-level AI probability scores with plain-language explanations
  • Plagiarism check, grammar check, AI Vocabulary check and Writing Feedback
  • Detects ChatGPT, GPT-5/6, Gemini, Claude, Llama, DeepSeek
  • Authorship verification (Writing Replay) for students proving their own work
  • SOC 2 Type II, FERPA/GDPR/CCPA, HECVAT + VPAT documentation

✅ Pros

  • Category leader with the strongest US education footprint: 20M+ users, 3,500+ colleges, 100+ institutions, official AI detector partner of the American Federation of Teachers, trusted by 10M+ teachers and students
  • First US patent on AI detection (multi-class detection including mixed AI writing and humanized texts); retrained as new models launch; detects ChatGPT, GPT-5/6, Gemini, Claude, Llama, DeepSeek
  • Sentence-level explainability — every sentence checked hundreds of times, with the patterns behind each flag explained in plain language; the only detector de-biased for ESL writers (false-positive rate for ESL writing ~1%)
  • Real free plan ($0, 10,000 words a month, no expiry) plus a compliance posture built for schools: SOC 2 Type II, FERPA/GDPR/CCPA, HECVAT + VPAT documentation

❌ Cons

  • No detector is 100% accurate — GPTZero itself says results should not be used to punish or as a final verdict; independent real-world testing puts accuracy at 82-87% on edited/paraphrased content, versus the vendor's 99.9% headline claim
  • Pricing page is JavaScript-rendered — dollar figures cannot be read directly, and the tier lineup changed in late 2026 (the Essential tier appears to have been removed; confirm on the official site)
  • Free plan is thin: 10,000 words a month, 10,000 characters per scan, only 1 Advanced Scan and 5 Chrome-extension highlights
  • Paid value concentrates at Premium — Professional costs more per word than Premium ($24.99 for 500K words vs $12.99 for 300K), so the top tier is poor value for pure volume

Frequently asked questions

Is GPTZero free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with 10,000 words a month, a Basic AI Scan, 1 free Advanced Scan and 5 Chrome-extension highlights — a genuine free plan with no expiry. Paid plans start at Premium, $12.99 a month billed annually.

What is GPTZero best for?

US educators and publishers who need the most trusted AI detector with evidence they can show — teachers checking student essays with sentence-level evidence and downloadable reports, publishers verifying freelancer copy is human-written, and students proving authorship of their own work.

What should I watch out for with GPTZero?

No detector is 100% accurate — GPTZero itself says results should not be used to punish or as a final verdict, and independent testing puts accuracy at 82-87% on edited/paraphrased content versus the vendor's 99.9% headline claim. The pricing page hides its figures behind JavaScript, so confirm on the official site.

Verdict

Best for: US educators and publishers who need the most trusted AI detector with evidence they can show — with the warning that no detector is 100% accurate, so results should never be used as a final verdict.
