Taskade Review 2026

An AI-native workspace where one prompt becomes a live app — projects, custom AI agents, automations and team chat in one place, with flat workspace pricing instead of per-seat billing.

Overview

US teams juggle separate tools for docs, tasks, chat and automations, and per-seat pricing makes every new hire expensive. Taskade replaces that stack with one AI workspace: describe what you need in plain English and Genesis builds a working app, while AI agents handle follow-ups, summaries and routine work across projects.

Official site: https://www.taskade.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Genesis AI app builder — one prompt becomes a live app
  • Custom AI agents trained on your data
  • Unlimited automations and AI teams on Pro
  • Flat workspace pricing — $10/mo for up to 10 users
  • 100+ integrations, MCP and bring-your-own-key

✅ Pros

  • Flat workspace pricing — Pro is $10/mo for up to 10 users, not per seat, so growing teams pay far less than Notion or Asana
  • Genesis turns one prompt into a live, working app — dashboards, CRMs, client portals and trackers with no code
  • Unlimited AI agents, teams and automations on Pro, with agents trainable on your own data
  • 100+ integrations, MCP support and bring-your-own-key on Pro, so it plugs into Slack, Gmail, GitHub and more
  • Real-time collaboration, video chat and 1,000+ free app templates built in

❌ Cons

  • Free plan includes no monthly AI credits — AI work on Free depends on credits earned through referrals and social actions
  • AI runs on a credit system, and a long, complex build can burn several thousand credits, so heavy builders must watch usage
  • Advanced admin controls and SAML SSO need the Business plan, and custom domains start on Pro
  • SOC 2 Type II compliance is still in progress, which may block regulated teams

Frequently asked questions

Is Taskade free?

Yes — Taskade has a free-forever plan at $0 for up to 3 AI apps, AI agents and automations. But it includes no monthly AI credits, so AI work on Free depends on credits earned through referrals and social actions; the paid Starter plan ($6/mo) is the cheapest way to get a monthly AI allowance.

What is Taskade best for?

Small US teams and agencies that want projects, AI agents and simple internal apps in one workspace without per-seat pricing — Pro costs a flat $10/mo for up to 10 users, and Genesis can turn a plain-English brief into a working CRM, dashboard or client portal.

What should I watch out for with Taskade?

AI runs on credits, and a long build can use several thousand credits, so heavy builders should watch the monthly allowance (50,000 on Pro, 150,000 on Business); and the Free plan has no monthly AI credits at all, so serious AI use needs a paid plan.

Verdict

Best for: small US teams and agencies that want projects, AI agents and simple internal apps in one workspace without per-seat pricing — Pro costs a flat $10/mo for up to 10 users.
