Overview

HyperWrite is a browser-wide AI writing assistant from OthersideAI that meets people where they already write — Gmail, Google Docs and any text field in Chrome. Its TypeAhead autocomplete finishes sentences as you type, its chat assistant drafts and rewrites on demand, and its Personal Assistant agent can browse and fill forms, so busy US professionals get AI help without switching to a separate writing app.

Official site: https://hyperwriteai.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • TypeAhead autocomplete inside Gmail, Google Docs and any web text field
  • Scholar AI — cited answers backed by live research across millions of scholarly articles
  • Custom Personas that write in your own voice (3 on Premium, 10 on Ultra)
  • Hundreds of ready-made AI tools plus a Personal Assistant browser agent
  • Chrome extension that works across the whole browser

✅ Pros

  • Works everywhere in the browser — TypeAhead gives personalized, context-aware sentence completions inside Gmail, Docs and any web text field via the Chrome extension
  • Citations + real-time info — answers and drafts are backed by live research across millions of scholarly articles, with proper citations (Scholar AI)
  • Custom Personas — the AI can be trained to write in your own voice (3 on Premium, 10 on Ultra), so output sounds like you, not a generic bot
  • Hundreds of ready-made AI tools plus a Personal Assistant browser agent that can navigate sites and complete tasks, not just generate text

❌ Cons

  • Premium caps AI messages at 250 a month — heavy daily chat users will hit the limit, and HyperWrite does not define what counts as a 'message,' so budgeting is guesswork
  • No team plan — priced per individual only, with no shared workspace, admin controls or volume pricing for US teams
  • Privacy policy dates from August 2022 with no security certifications listed on the pricing/review pages — a harder sell for employers that require a security review
  • Ultra is a steep jump ($44.99 a month monthly) for what is essentially 'unlimited messages + more personas'

Frequently asked questions

Is HyperWrite free?

Yes. The free account costs $0 with limited monthly credits on the basic AI (the exact amount is not published). Premium starts at $19.99 a month ($16 a month billed annually) and caps AI messages at 250 a month.

What is HyperWrite best for?

People who write all day inside Chrome, Gmail and Docs and want AI help without leaving the page — sales and ops staff drafting email replies in Gmail, content marketers drafting cited, research-backed articles with Scholar AI, and solo founders who live in the browser.

What should I watch out for with HyperWrite?

Premium caps AI messages at 250 a month and HyperWrite does not define what counts as a 'message.' There is no team plan, and the privacy policy dates from August 2022 with no security certifications listed.

Verdict

Best for: people who write all day inside Chrome, Gmail and Docs and want AI help without leaving the page — with the warning that Premium caps AI messages at 250 a month and there is no team plan.
