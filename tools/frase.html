Frase Review 2026

AI agent that researches, writes and optimizes SEO content, tracks whether AI engines cite you, and fixes pages losing rank.

Overview

Content teams waste hours researching SERPs, writing drafts, and checking whether Google — or ChatGPT and Perplexity — actually cite them. Frase runs the whole loop in one platform: its AI agent researches, writes and optimizes SEO/GEO content, tracks AI visibility, watches for ranking decay, and publishes straight to the team’s CMS.

Official site: https://www.frase.io

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • One platform for the full loop — research, AI drafting, SEO/GEO scoring, publishing and monitoring
  • AI Visibility tracking shows whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini cite your content
  • Content Guard watches ranking decay and drafts the fix for your approval
  • Publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Wix or FraseCMS; MCP server on every tier

Frequently asked questions

Is Frase free?

No — Frase has no free plan. The entry paid plan is Starter at $39 / month billed annually ($49 monthly).

What is Frase best for?

Best for: SEO teams and agencies that want the full loop — research, drafting, optimization, AI visibility tracking and publishing — in one platform. There is no free plan, only a 7-day trial, and article caps are tight on Starter, so size the plan to your publishing volume.
