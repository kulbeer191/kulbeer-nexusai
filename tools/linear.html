Overview

Product and engineering teams lose hours to issue triage, backlog grooming, and status updates in slow, over-configured trackers. Linear is the fast, keyboard-first issue tracker where AI agents now do that busywork in the same workspace — triaging incoming bugs, drafting updates, and even writing code and opening pull requests.

Official site: https://linear.app/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Fastest issue tracker for product teams — opinionated, keyboard-first workflow (the “Linear Method”) with almost no configuration overhead
  • Linear Agent is included on every plan, even Free — it triages issues, summarizes projects, creates issues, and answers questions in-app, in comments, Slack, and Microsoft Teams
  • Third-party AI agents (Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Codex, Devin, and more) can be assigned issues like teammates — and agents do not count as billable seats
  • Genuinely usable free plan: unlimited members, 250 issues, and the full agent platform

❌ Cons

  • AI coding sessions and Loops are usage-based (prepaid AI credits), so costs vary and can surprise; purchased credits expire 12 months after purchase
  • Opinionated workflow — teams used to Jira or Notion’s flexibility may find it rigid; closed-source SaaS with no self-hosted option
  • The best AI features (Triage Intelligence, Code Intelligence, SAML, dashboards) are gated to Business/Enterprise, and Basic is capped at 5 teams
