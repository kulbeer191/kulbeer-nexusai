Overview

US support teams face rising ticket volumes and customer expectations for instant, accurate answers, while hiring more agents is slow and expensive. Fin is an autonomous AI customer agent that resolves real support work end-to-end — answering questions, updating accounts, processing refunds, troubleshooting issues, and completing multi-step workflows across chat, email, voice, WhatsApp, and Slack — trained on the company's own help content, policies, and procedures, with human handoff when needed.

Official site: https://www.intercom.com/fin

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Autonomous resolution across chat, email, voice, WhatsApp and Slack
  • Outcome-based pricing — $0.99 per resolved outcome
  • Works with Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot and Freshdesk helpdesks
  • Proprietary Apex models with simulations, regression tests and AI QA
  • SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, HIPAA; 99.8% uptime SLA

✅ Pros

  • Best published resolution performance in the category: 76% average resolution rate across 12,000+ customers (many over 85%), with about 2 million resolutions a week
  • Outcome-based pricing ($0.99 per resolved outcome) — you pay only when Fin actually delivers value, not per seat or per message
  • Works with any helpdesk, not just Intercom's — native with Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, and Freshdesk, and live in under an hour with no migration project
  • Runs on proprietary Apex models custom-trained on millions of customer-experience interactions, with a full testing suite (simulations, regression tests), observability, and AI QA before changes go live
  • Enterprise-grade trust: SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, HIPAA, 99.8% uptime SLA, SSO/SCIM, and US/EU/Australia data residency

❌ Cons

  • No free plan — only a 14-day free trial (no credit card required); every outcome after the trial is billed
  • Seat prices for the Essential, Advanced and Expert helpdesk plans are NOT published on the pricing page (calculator-gated) — this page lists no dollar figure for them
  • The per-outcome model can get expensive fast if deflection is low — at $0.99 per outcome with a 50-outcome monthly minimum, a team with poor knowledge content pays for attempts as well as successes
  • The Intercom to Fin rebrand (May 2026) means docs, contracts, and some URLs still carry both names, which can confuse buyers researching the product

Frequently asked questions

Is Fin by Intercom free?

No. There is no free plan — only a 14-day free trial with all features, no credit card required. After the trial, Fin costs $0.99 per resolved outcome (Fin Voice $1.99 per voice outcome), with a 50-outcome monthly minimum for standalone use.

What is Fin by Intercom best for?

US support teams that want an autonomous agent to resolve tickets end-to-end — a SaaS company letting Fin handle billing, refund and account-access tickets overnight, an e-commerce brand running order-status and returns across chat and email, or a B2B team running multi-step onboarding workflows with Procedures.

What should I watch out for with Fin by Intercom?

The per-outcome model can get expensive fast if deflection is low — at $0.99 per outcome with a 50-outcome monthly minimum, a team with poor knowledge content pays for attempts as well as successes. And helpdesk seat prices are not published at all, so the full bill needs a quote.

Verdict

Best for: US support teams that want an autonomous agent to resolve tickets end-to-end and pay only per outcome — with the warning that low deflection makes the per-outcome bill grow fast and helpdesk seat prices are not published.
