Sintra AI — Pros

  • One subscription covers 12 role-specialized AI employees (Soshie for social media, Seomi for SEO, Milli for sales, Cassie for support, Emmie for email, Dexter for data and more) — no need to buy separate tools per function
  • Brain AI shared memory: the helpers learn the brand voice and business context once and apply it across every role and task
  • Genuinely no-code setup — add brand context, connect tools, set rules; the employees work in chat on web and mobile, 24/7, in 100+ languages

Sintra AI — Cons

  • Hard 250-credit monthly cap shared across all 12 helpers — heavy users hit the wall and the helpers stop working until credits are topped up; credits do not roll over
  • Advertised prices are promotional (50-70% off); regular rates are $97/$59/$52 per month, so the real long-term cost may be much higher than the sale price
  • Trustpilot has flagged Sintra's profile for incentivized reviews — its 8,600+ reviews should be read with caution, not quoted as a trust signal

Lindy AI — Pros

  • No-code setup — connect tools in about 2 minutes, no API keys or IT ticket
  • Slack-native with 3,000+ integrations plus MCP support, and persistent workspace context
  • Approvals built in — anything with outside impact waits for your OK before it goes out

Lindy AI — Cons

  • Credit-based pricing makes costs unpredictable — bigger jobs burn 250-2,500 credits each
  • Per-seat billing — anyone who uses Lindy or @mentions it in Slack takes a billed seat
  • Official pricing pages showed different Team figures at time of writing, which is confusing for buyers

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Sintra AI or Lindy AI?

Sintra's 12-month plan is $15.60 a month ($187.20 a year upfront), while Lindy's Team plan is $49.99 a month — but Sintra has no free plan (only a 3-day trial) and a hard 250-credit monthly cap shared across all 12 helpers, while Lindy starts free with $50 in credits for 7 days. Sintra is cheaper per month; Lindy gives you more credits to work with.

Which should I choose, Sintra AI or Lindy AI?

Pick Sintra AI if you are a solo founder or lean team that wants one subscription covering a dozen business roles with a shared Brain AI memory. Pick Lindy AI if your team lives in Slack and wants agents with human approval gates, 3,000+ integrations and SOC 2 / HIPAA compliance.

What should I watch out for?

Sintra's advertised prices are promotional (50-70% off) — regular rates run $52-$97 a month — and its 250-credit cap stops all 12 helpers when hit. Lindy's credit-based pricing makes costs unpredictable (bigger jobs burn 250-2,500 credits each), and anyone who uses Lindy or @mentions it in Slack takes a billed seat.

Verdict

Pick Sintra AI if you want one subscription covering a dozen business roles for a solo founder or lean team. Pick Lindy AI if your team lives in Slack and wants agents with approval gates, 3,000+ integrations and enterprise compliance.
