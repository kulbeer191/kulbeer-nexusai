v0 by Vercel Review 2026

Prompt-to-live-app AI assistant from Vercel, with one-click deployment and GitHub sync.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Turning an idea into a working web app usually means days of scaffolding, UI work, and deployment setup before anything is visible. v0 is Vercel's collaborative AI assistant that generates working full-stack applications from a plain-language prompt in minutes, with visual design controls, GitHub sync, and one-click deployment to Vercel — so founders and frontend teams can ship a live product without building the boilerplate first.

Official site: https://v0.app/

✅ Pros

  • Prompt-to-live-app in minutes, with one-click deployment to Vercel and GitHub sync built in
  • Agentic by default — v0 plans, creates tasks, and connects to databases as it builds
  • Visual Design Mode and templates for fine-tuning every detail with live preview
  • Strong React/Next.js fit — generated UI drops directly into an existing Vercel workflow

❌ Cons

  • Credit/token-based pricing is hard to budget — costs vary by model (Mini/Pro/Max) and prompt size
  • The $20 Premium plan is being sunset and is closed to new signups, so the real entry price is $30/user/month
  • New v0 Plus teams on a Vercel Hobby account may also need a Vercel Pro subscription ($20/mo), an extra cost that is easy to miss
  • Best for frontend/React work — less suited to non-web or backend-heavy projects

Pricing

Premium ($20/month) is being sunset and is closed to new signups — do not buy expecting it. Plan prices are corroborated by multiple Oct–Nov 2026 reads of v0.app/pricing (plan cards did not render in our direct fetch) — confirm on the official page before buying.
