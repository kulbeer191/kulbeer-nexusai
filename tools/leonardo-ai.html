Leonardo.Ai Review 2026

Consistent characters, product shots and campaign art with custom-trained models.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Designers, game artists, and marketers need a large volume of consistent, high-quality visuals — characters, product shots, campaign art — but commissioning or shooting each one is slow and expensive. Leonardo.Ai combines its own image models (Lucid, Phoenix) with leading third-party models (FLUX, GPT-Image, Veo, Kling) in one workspace, and lets users train custom models on 10–20 reference images so every asset stays on-style.

Official site: https://leonardo.ai/

✅ Pros

  • Wide model choice in one subscription, including its own Lucid Origin/Realism and Phoenix models plus FLUX, GPT-Image, Veo, and Kling
  • Train custom models for consistent characters, products, and brand styles
  • Unlimited relaxed generation on selected models for Premium/Ultimate plans, so work continues after tokens run out
  • Paid subscribers retain full ownership and IP rights to images they generate

❌ Cons

  • Token system is confusing — costs vary by model, size, and quality, and free tokens expire daily
  • Free-tier generations are public, and Leonardo retains rights to free users' images (paid users own theirs outright)
  • Third-party models are excluded from relaxed/unlimited generation and always cost tokens
