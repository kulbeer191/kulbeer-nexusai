Google Antigravity Review 2026

Google's agent-first development platform — a desktop agent manager, a full agentic IDE, a terminal CLI and editor extensions in one system — that plans and executes whole multi-step coding tasks, not just autocompletion.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US development teams need an agent platform that plans and executes whole multi-step coding tasks — not just autocompletion — across the editor, terminal and browser. Antigravity is Google's agent-first development platform: a desktop agent-manager app, a full agentic IDE, a terminal CLI (agy) and extensions for VS Code, JetBrains, Zed and Xcode, all in one system. It launched in November 2025 and in June 2026 replaced Gemini CLI entirely — it is now Google's single flagship coding-agent surface, bundled into Gemini Enterprise.

Official site: https://antigravity.google/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Desktop agent-manager app running multiple agents in parallel
  • Full agentic IDE with artifacts and verification tests
  • Terminal-first CLI (agy) plus extensions for VS Code, JetBrains, Zed and Xcode
  • Multi-model: Gemini on every tier, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 on paid plans
  • Google Cloud billing, admin controls and pooled AI credits in Gemini Enterprise

✅ Pros

  • Genuine $0 free Individual tier — unlimited Tab completions and unlimited Command requests, with a basic weekly agent quota. No credit card required
  • Four surfaces in one platform: desktop app to run and supervise multiple agents in parallel (grouped into Projects, scheduled messages), a full IDE with artifacts and verification tests, a terminal-first CLI, and editor extensions
  • Multi-model, not locked to one lab: Gemini models on every tier, plus Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5 on paid Google AI Pro/Ultra plans
  • Google-scale backing and enterprise path: Google Cloud billing, admin controls, pooled AI credits in Gemini Enterprise, browser-in-the-loop agents, and scheduled agent tasks

❌ Cons

  • No standalone Antigravity subscription — paid capacity rides on Google AI plans ($19.99/$99.99/$199.99), so you buy a whole Google AI bundle, not just the coding agent
  • Free-tier quota is unpublished and weekly ('meaningful' allowance, no numbers) — heavy users hit the wall unpredictably
  • Fast-moving product with real churn: Claude Sonnet/Opus 4.6 and gpt-oss-120b are removed on November 30, 2026, and Gemini CLI was shut down in favor of Antigravity CLI in June 2026

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Antigravity free?

Yes. The Individual tier costs $0 and includes unlimited Tab completions, unlimited Command requests and a basic weekly agent quota, with Gemini models — no credit card required. The free agent quota is unpublished and weekly, so heavy users hit the wall unpredictably.

What is Google Antigravity best for?

US teams already in the Google ecosystem, and any developer who wants a genuinely free agent platform with a real IDE and CLI — full-stack product teams prototyping and shipping features with several agents running in parallel, and solo founders building MVPs at $0.

What should I watch out for with Google Antigravity?

There is no standalone subscription — paid value is bundled into Google AI plans ($19.99/$99.99/$199.99), not a coding-agent purchase on its own. Free-tier quotas are unpublished and weekly, and the product moves fast: some models are removed on November 30, 2026.

Verdict

Best for: US teams already in the Google ecosystem, and any developer who wants a genuinely free agent platform with a real IDE and CLI — with the warning that paid value is bundled into Google AI plans and free-tier quotas are unpublished.
