Fathom Review 2026

Fathom joins your Zoom, Meet, or Teams calls as an AI notetaker, recording and transcribing every meeting into summaries, action items, and searchable notes.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Fathom joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams calls as an AI notetaker, recording and transcribing every meeting and turning it into instant summaries, action items, and searchable notes — so US teams stop taking manual notes, never lose decisions or follow-ups, and can search across every conversation for what was promised and by whom. Note: Superhuman (formerly Grammarly) announced its acquisition of Fathom on 14 September 2026 — Fathom says nothing changes today for users, and the free plan and pricing are unchanged so far.

Official site: https://fathom.video/

✅ Pros

  • The most generous free plan in the category: unlimited recordings, transcriptions, and storage, free forever
  • Accurate AI summaries, action items, and "Ask Fathom" search across all past calls, plus clips and playlists
  • Deep integrations: summaries sync into Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, and Asana; CRM field sync on Business; public API + MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT
  • Bot-free capture option (Mac beta) plus SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance with a standard BAA on team plans

❌ Cons

  • Advanced AI summaries are capped on the free plan (5 per month) and AI action items, follow-up emails, and unlimited Ask Fathom require Premium
  • CRM sync is capped at 3 users per email domain on Free, Premium, and Team — full CRM field sync needs Business
  • Bot-free recording is Mac-first and still in beta; Windows bot-free is only a limited Microsoft Teams alpha
  • Refund terms conflict: the pricing page advertises a "90-day guarantee" while the Terms of Service say all fees are non-refundable

Pricing

Prices change — always check the official site before buying. Prices below are the widely published plans at time of writing. Prices exclude taxes. Free $0, Premium $20/mo, Team $19/mo, Business $34/mo, Enterprise $35/mo verified on fathom.ai/pricing. Acquired by Superhuman (formerly Grammarly) 14 Sept 2026 — free plan and pricing unchanged so far. Do NOT confuse Fathom with Fathom Analytics (a different product) — Fathom has no public self-serve affiliate program.

Verdict

Best for: sales and customer-success teams that want free, unlimited meeting recordings with AI summaries and CRM sync. Start free forever; Premium $20/mo unlocks unlimited AI summaries and Ask Fathom.
