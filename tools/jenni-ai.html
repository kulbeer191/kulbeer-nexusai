Jenni AI Review 2026

Overview

Students and researchers waste hours turning scattered sources into cited, well-structured papers. Jenni AI is an academic editor whose AI autocomplete writes with you, grounded in your own sources — with in-text citations in 10,000+ styles, chat over your PDF library, and reviews that catch weak arguments before submission.

Official site: https://jenni.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI autocomplete grounded in your uploaded sources, not generic text
  • One-click citations in 10,000+ styles, with unlimited citations on every plan
  • Chat with your PDF library, outline/thesis helpers and paper reviews
  • Exports to .docx, LaTeX and HTML for submission-ready papers

Frequently asked questions

Is Jenni AI free?

Yes — Jenni AI has a free plan (Free, $0). The entry paid plan is Plus at $12 / month billed annually ($30 monthly).

What is Jenni AI best for?

Best for: graduate students and research teams writing cited papers, theses and grant proposals from a shared PDF library. The free tier is genuinely usable; Plus at $12/mo billed annually is the sweet spot, while monthly billing costs 2.5x more.
