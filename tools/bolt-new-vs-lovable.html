Bolt.new — Pros

  • Full-stack in the browser — real Node.js environment, no local setup, one-click deploy
  • Generous free tier: 1M tokens/month, no card required
  • Automatic model routing and self-correction

Bolt.new — Cons

  • Token metering is hard to budget as projects grow
  • JavaScript-only backends
  • Subscriptions are non-refundable

Lovable — Pros

  • True end-to-end build — hosting, auth, payments, integrations in one place
  • Per-workspace pricing with unlimited members
  • Strong founder proof points (customers retiring $1M+ in SaaS contracts)

Lovable — Cons

  • Credit-based pricing — monthly spend is hard to predict
  • Business costs twice Pro for the same 100 credits
  • Monthly credits expire; AI apps still need human review for production
