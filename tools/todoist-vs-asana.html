Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Todoist or Asana?

Todoist’s entry paid plan is Pro at $5/mo ($60/year); Asana’s is Starter at $10.99/user/mo billed annually ($13.49 monthly). Todoist is cheaper — and its free plan has no user cap, while Asana’s free Personal plan is limited to 2 users.

Which should I choose, Todoist or Asana?

Pick Todoist if you are a solo professional or small team that wants the fastest way to capture and finish daily tasks. Pick Asana if you run cross-functional work with timelines, dependencies and portfolios — and need AI agents with governance.

What should I watch out for with Todoist?

It is a task list, not a full project manager — no Gantt charts, portfolios or workload views, and its AI is lighter than Asana’s agent features.

Verdict

Pick Todoist if your work is personal tasks and small-team triage — it is faster, cheaper ($5/mo) and simpler. Pick Asana if your work is cross-functional projects with timelines, portfolios and governance — just budget $10.99/user/mo for Starter and expect $24.99/mo for the AI features that matter most.
