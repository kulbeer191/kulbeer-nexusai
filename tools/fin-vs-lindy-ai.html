Fin by Intercom vs Lindy AI (2026)

Both are autonomous agents pitched at replacing first-line support and ops work, but with opposite pricing philosophies. Fin charges $0.99 per resolved outcome — you pay only when it actually delivers value — and plugs into any helpdesk, while Lindy is a no-code AI employee with 3,000+ integrations that works from inside Slack and charges per seat plus credits. Pick Fin for support resolution at scale; pick Lindy for no-code ops automation your whole team can build.

Fin by Intercom — Pros

  • Best published resolution performance in the category: 76% average resolution rate across 12,000+ customers (many over 85%), with about 2 million resolutions a week
  • Outcome-based pricing ($0.99 per resolved outcome) — you pay only when Fin actually delivers value, not per seat or per message
  • Works with any helpdesk, not just Intercom's — native with Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, and Freshdesk, and live in under an hour with no migration project
  • Runs on proprietary Apex models custom-trained on millions of customer-experience interactions, with a full testing suite (simulations, regression tests), observability, and AI QA before changes go live

Fin by Intercom — Cons

  • No free plan — only a 14-day free trial (no credit card required); every outcome after the trial is billed
  • Seat prices for the Essential, Advanced and Expert helpdesk plans are NOT published on the pricing page (calculator-gated) — this page lists no dollar figure for them
  • The per-outcome model can get expensive fast if deflection is low — at $0.99 per outcome with a 50-outcome monthly minimum, a team with poor knowledge content pays for attempts as well as successes

Lindy AI — Pros

  • No-code setup — connect tools in about 2 minutes, no API keys or IT ticket
  • Slack-native with 3,000+ integrations plus MCP support, and persistent workspace context
  • Approvals built in — anything with outside impact waits for your OK before it goes out
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and PIPEDA compliant; data is encrypted and never used to train models

Lindy AI — Cons

  • Credit-based pricing makes costs unpredictable — bigger jobs burn 250–2,500 credits each
  • Per-seat billing — anyone who uses Lindy or @mentions it in Slack takes a billed seat
  • Official pricing pages showed different Team figures at time of writing, which is confusing for buyers

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Fin or Lindy AI?

It depends on volume. Fin costs $0.99 per resolved outcome (50-outcome monthly minimum standalone), so a team resolving 500 tickets a month pays about $495. Lindy Team is $49.99 a month for 5,000 credits, but bigger jobs burn 250–2,500 credits each, so the real cost depends on job size — measure with your own workload.

Which is better for customer support, Fin or Lindy AI?

Fin. Its 76% average resolution rate, Apex models, and native fit with any helpdesk (Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, Freshdesk) are built for support resolution at scale. Lindy is the stronger pick for no-code ops automation — inbox triage, meeting notes, lead follow-ups — run from Slack.

Can I use Fin without Intercom's helpdesk?

Yes. Fin works standalone with another helpdesk at $0.99 per outcome with a 50-outcome monthly minimum — no seat costs, no setup, integration or platform fees. Lindy, by contrast, is Slack-native and needs your team working from Slack to get the most out of it.

Verdict

Pick Fin if your goal is resolving support tickets end-to-end at scale and paying only per outcome — with the warning that low deflection makes the bill grow fast. Pick Lindy if your goal is no-code ops automation your whole team can build from Slack — with the warning that credit costs are unpredictable and @mentions take a billed seat.
