Fireflies.ai Review 2026

AI teammate Fred records, transcribes and summarizes every meeting into searchable notes.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Meetings generate the decisions, but the notes, action items and follow-ups live in people's heads — and get forgotten the moment the call ends. Fireflies' AI teammate Fred records, transcribes and summarizes every Zoom, Google Meet and Teams call, turns the conversation into searchable notes, decisions and action items, and answers questions across all past meetings, email and apps — so nothing discussed is ever lost.

Official site: https://fireflies.ai/

✅ Pros

  • Records, transcribes and summarizes Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls in 100+ languages, with AskFred AI assistant searching across meetings, docs and apps
  • Free plan includes unlimited transcription; Pro adds video recording, downloads, email assistant and 8,000 minutes of storage per seat
  • 240+ integrations (Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, Asana) plus CRM sync and action items pushed into 15+ tools
  • Strong privacy: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 42001, GDPR, HIPAA-ready; Fireflies does not use meeting data to train AI models

❌ Cons

  • Free plan caps at 400 minutes of storage per team and limited AI summaries — heavy meeting teams hit the ceiling fast
  • The notetaker bot joining calls can feel intrusive to attendees (bot-free recording needs the Chrome extension/desktop app)
  • AI usage is metered by credits (20 on Free/Pro, 30 on Business, 50 on Enterprise) with rate limits that add complexity
  • Enterprise at $39/seat/month is annual-only and pricey for small teams

Pricing
