Dzine AI Review 2026

An all-in-one AI creative workspace — formerly Stylar — where a layered canvas, 20+ image models and a dozen video models share one credit balance, so creative teams stop juggling five subscriptions and keep the composition they arrange.

Overview

Creative teams juggle five subscriptions (one image generator, one editor, one upscaler, one video tool, one face-swap app) and lose composition control to one-click generators. Dzine puts GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0, Ideogram, Flux, Midjourney, plus Seedance, Kling, Veo 3.1 and Hailuo video models in one layered canvas where the layout you arrange is the layout you keep — with consistent characters, real-time team collaboration, and chain-of-title/copyright checks for production work.

Official site: https://www.dzine.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Layered canvas with 20+ image models and a dozen video models
  • Consistent characters with multiple views, outfits and expressions
  • Deep AI Editor: local edit, insert object, AI eraser, generative expand, face swap, lip sync, 8K upscaling
  • Real-time team collaboration and project spaces
  • Copyright and chain-of-title records for production work

✅ Pros

  • One credit balance across 20+ image models (GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0, Ideogram 4.5, Flux.3, Midjourney on Master+) and video models (Seedance 2.5, Kling, Veo 3.1, Hailuo, Wan)
  • Layered canvas — Image-to-Image merges your composition while keeping positions, sizes and orientation
  • Consistent characters: reusable characters with multiple views, outfits and expressions
  • Deep AI Editor — local edit, insert object, AI eraser, generative expand, face swap, lip sync, upscaling to 8K video
  • Real-time team collaboration, project spaces, and copyright/chain-of-title records for production workflows

❌ Cons

  • No recurring free plan — only a one-time 100-credit signup grant (valid 12 months); the old daily 32-credit refill was discontinued, and free generations are public and non-commercial
  • Credit math is hard to predict — per-image costs range from 8 credits (FLUX.2 Pro) to 50 credits (Nano Banana Pro), so the same balance yields wildly different output counts
  • No refunds under any circumstances, including unused subscription periods or accidental purchases (stated on the pricing page)
  • Most AI Editor tools are paid-only; the free grant covers only basic tries
  • Third-party reviews report throttled 'unlimited' image plans on Master/Master Pro (Trustpilot complaints, corroborated only)

Frequently asked questions

Is Dzine AI free?

Not as a recurring free plan. There is a one-time 100-credit signup grant (valid 12 months) — the old daily 32-credit refill was discontinued, and free generations are public and non-commercial. Paid plans start at Beginner, $8.99 a month.

What is Dzine AI best for?

E-commerce and marketing teams producing on-brand product visuals with consistent characters and backgrounds, design studios doing layered compositing and client revisions in one canvas instead of five subscriptions, and indie filmmakers storyboarding and generating consistent shots with the Pipeline node canvas and provenance records.

What should I watch out for with Dzine AI?

There are no refunds under any circumstances, including unused subscription periods or accidental purchases. And credit math is hard to predict — per-image costs range from 8 credits to 50 credits, so the same balance yields wildly different output counts.

Verdict

Best for: Creative teams that want one canvas and one credit balance across every major image and video model — with the warning that there is no recurring free plan and no refunds under any circumstances.
