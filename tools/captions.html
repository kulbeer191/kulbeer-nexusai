Overview

US solo creators and small social teams shoot talking-head video on their phones but lose hours to editing — cutting pauses, adding styled captions, dubbing, making clips and ads. Captions (by Mirage, the company renamed from Captions in Sept 2025) is a mobile-first AI video editor: upload footage and its AI Edit cuts scenes, adds B-roll, music and captions in 100+ languages in a couple of taps, plus AI Twins and digital actors that generate new footage without recording, a chat-based editor for changes, and one-tap Clips that turn long videos into post-ready shorts.

Official site: https://www.captions.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • One-tap full edit — AI Edit cuts scenes, overlays B-roll, adds music and captions
  • AI Twin and 200+ digital actors (Mirage Avatar X generates face, voice and motion)
  • Third-party video models (Google Veo 3, Kling, Sora 2) on one credit balance
  • One-tap Clips turn long videos into post-ready shorts
  • AI Translate + Lipdub, AI music, sounds, voiceover, chat-based editor

✅ Pros

  • True one-tap full edit — AI Edit cuts scenes, overlays B-roll, adds music, transitions and captions from a single style pick, with a chat-based editor for follow-up changes; no editing experience needed
  • AI Twin and digital actors — make a digital twin of yourself from one selfie or pick from 200+ library avatars (Mirage Avatar X, announced July 28, 2026, generates face, voice and motion in one pass), then change outfits, backgrounds and product inserts by prompt
  • Generation built in — the Max plan accesses third-party video models (Google Veo 3, Kling, Sora 2) plus Mirage's own models from one monthly credit balance, with AI Creator, AI Ads and AI Skits for one-tap generated footage
  • Real scale signals — 20M users (per homepage), 4.7 stars from ~38,000 App Store ratings (Sept 2026), $100M+ raised at a $500M valuation, $75M growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund (March 2026), $28.4M in-app revenue over the prior 12 months (per TechCrunch)

❌ Cons

  • Credit-metered on top of the subscription — Max buys only 500 credits a month and Frontier 1,400; heavy generation burns through fast, and only Frontier 4x can buy top-up credits
  • Pricing is iOS-centric — the official pricing page states 'All prices displayed in USD. Features and prices reflect iOS plans only,' so web/Android prices may differ; the $9.99 Basic tier vanished from the pricing page between June and August 2026 (still sold in-app per some sources), so the entry paid tier is unstable
  • Mobile-first means thinner as a pure editor — the company is drifting from editing toward generation after the Mirage rebrand; no desktop-grade timeline depth, and Mirage Studio (brand ad tool) was merged into the Captions web app in 2026
  • Free plan is thin — basic tools only (trim, transitions, some media library assets, 1 caption template) and no monthly AI credits; the 60-200 one-time credits figure comes from the help center, not the pricing page

Frequently asked questions

Is Captions free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with basic tools — trimming, transitions, some media library assets, captions in 100+ languages and 1 caption template — but no monthly AI credits (the help center lists 60-200 one-time credits). Paid plans start at Max, $24.99 a month.

What is Captions best for?

US solo creators and small social teams who want phone-first, one-tap AI editing with AI Twins and generated footage in the same app — YouTubers/TikTokers who film talking-head content on their phone, small social/ad teams generating UGC-style ads and skits at volume, and podcasters repurposing long recordings into captioned, dubbed clips.

What should I watch out for with Captions?

Credits cap at 500 a month on Max, and the prices on the page are iOS plans only — web/Android prices may differ. The $9.99 Basic tier vanished from the pricing page in 2026, and the company is drifting from editing toward generation after the Mirage rebrand.

Verdict

Best for: US solo creators and small social teams who want phone-first, one-tap AI editing with AI Twins and generated footage in the same app — with the warning that credits cap at 500 a month on Max, and the prices on the page are iOS plans only.
