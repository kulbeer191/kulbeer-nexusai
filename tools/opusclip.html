OpusClip Review 2026

OpusClip turns long podcasts, interviews, and webinars into viral-ready vertical clips with captions and a Virality Score — used by 20M+ creators and businesses.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US creators and marketing teams sit on hours of long-form video, but cutting it into Shorts, Reels, and TikToks by hand takes days. OpusClip’s AI finds the strongest moments, reframes them to vertical, adds animated captions, and schedules the posts — so one recording becomes a week of short-form content in minutes.

Official site: https://opus.pro/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI clipping with Virality Score and ClipAnything model
  • AI reframe with moving-object tracking to any aspect ratio
  • Animated captions and multi-language transcription
  • Auto-posting and social scheduler for Shorts, TikTok, and Reels
  • AI B-roll, video dubbing, and export to Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve (Pro)

✅ Pros

  • ClipAnything finds viral moments in any genre — podcasts, vlogs, gaming, sports — not just talking-head video
  • Virality Score ranks every clip so you post the strongest moments first
  • Animated captions in 20+ languages with 99% transcription accuracy
  • One-click auto-posting and scheduling to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels
  • Free-forever plan with 60 credits a month, plus a 7-day Pro trial with no card required

❌ Cons

  • Credits are spent on minutes of source video imported, not on clips produced — long uploads burn credits fast
  • Free plan clips carry a watermark, have no editor, and stop being exportable after 3 days
  • Starter is 9:16 vertical only — 1:1 and 16:9 need the Pro plan
  • In-app upgrade prices have differed from the public pricing page, so the checkout price can surprise you

Frequently asked questions

Is OpusClip free?

Yes. OpusClip has a free-forever plan with 60 credits a month (one credit processes one minute of source video), up to 1080p clips with a watermark. New users also get a 7-day free trial of the Pro plan with no card required — but the trial is not a free plan, and free clips stop being exportable after 3 days.

What is OpusClip best for?

Best for US creators, podcasters, and marketing teams turning long videos — podcasts, interviews, webinars, streams — into a steady stream of captioned vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels, ranked by Virality Score.

What should I watch out for with OpusClip?

Credits are spent on the minutes of source video you import, not the clips you keep, so a long upload can burn a month of credits in one job. Free clips are watermarked, cannot be edited, and expire after 3 days, and the price shown inside the app has differed from the public pricing page.

Verdict
