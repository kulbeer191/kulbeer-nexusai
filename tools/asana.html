Overview

Work is scattered across tools, inboxes and spreadsheets, status lives in people’s heads, and chasing Friday updates eats the week. Asana is the work-management platform trusted by 85% of the Fortune 100 that connects goals, projects, tasks and dependencies in one shared Work Graph — and now adds AI Teammates, pre-built AI agents with shared memory, scoped permissions and audit trails, plus AI Studio and Asana Dash, so humans and agents run critical workflows together on the same plan.

Official site: https://asana.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Full work-management depth — Timeline and Gantt views, portfolios, goals, resource management, approvals, proofing and intake forms, far beyond a to-do list
  • AI Teammates with shared memory and governance (agent identities, scoped permissions, audit trails, cost constraints) that learn from completed work
  • AI Studio lets teams build no-code AI workflows and agents, with 50K–200K AI credits included depending on plan
  • Enterprise-grade — 100+ integrations, SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning on Enterprise, and adoption by 85% of the Fortune 100

✅ Pros

  • Enterprise-grade — 100+ integrations, SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning on Enterprise, and adoption by 85% of the Fortune 100

❌ Cons

  • AI usage is metered and tightly capped — Starter includes only 5 AI Teammate requests per user per month (up to 50 per account), so heavy AI use costs extra
  • Steep jump from the free Personal plan (2 users only) to Starter at $10.99/user/month, and the feature surface can overwhelm small teams
  • The AI features that matter most — AI Meetings, resource management, goals — are locked behind the $24.99 Advanced tier, and Enterprise pricing is sales-only

Frequently asked questions

Is Asana free?

Yes — Asana has a free Personal plan ($0) for up to 2 users. The entry paid plan is Starter at $10.99 / user / month billed annually ($13.49 monthly).

Best for: cross-functional teams that need real work management — timelines, portfolios, goals and governance — with AI agents running alongside humans. Start free on Personal (2 users); Starter $10.99/mo adds AI Teammates and Timeline views.
