Manus AI Review 2026

Autonomous agent that plans and executes multi-step work and delivers finished reports, slides and websites.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Most AI chatbots only answer questions and leave the work to you. Manus is a general-purpose autonomous agent — you describe a goal in plain language, and it plans and executes the work itself inside a cloud virtual machine, browsing the web, running software, and writing code. Instead of a chat reply, it delivers a finished artifact such as a research report, slide deck, website, or video.

Official site: https://manus.im/

✅ Pros

  • True end-to-end execution — plans and completes multi-step tasks without step-by-step prompting
  • Delivers finished artifacts (reports, slides, websites, videos), not just text answers
  • Runs in a cloud virtual machine, so work continues without your computer staying on
  • Wide Research, Slack integration, and automations in the 2.0 release

❌ Cons

  • Credit costs are unpredictable — Manus does not publish how many credits a task consumes before it runs
  • Can loop or make factual errors on complex or unusual tasks, so outputs need human review
  • Credit-based pricing is complex, which makes monthly cost hard to forecast
