Taskthread Review 2026

One shared kanban board for you and all your AI assistants over MCP — Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and Cursor plan, claim and hand off work on the same board, and an AI day plan lays the day out hour by hour.

Overview

Every AI chat starts from zero and no assistant knows what another one did, so work planned in Claude gets lost when ChatGPT picks it up. Taskthread gives a person and all their AI assistants one shared kanban board over MCP: assistants plan, claim, hand off and sign their work on the same board, and an AI day plan lays the day out hour by hour. It also folds in a meeting notetaker whose action items become tasks in the same app.

Official site: https://taskthread.denegra.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Shared kanban board over MCP for all AI assistants
  • Handoff notes between assistants
  • Task claiming to prevent duplicate work
  • AI Day Plan hour by hour around the existing calendar
  • Meeting notetaker with action items; web, iPhone and Android apps in sync

✅ Pros

  • One board shared by Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or any MCP assistant — each one signs its work under its own name
  • Handoff notes: an assistant leaves a note where it stopped, so the next chat — or a different assistant — continues from that note, not from scratch
  • Task claiming prevents two AIs (or an AI and a human) from working on the same task at once
  • Assistants cannot delete anything, and board access is scoped per assistant and revocable at any time
  • AI Day Plan lays the day out hour by hour around the existing calendar; meeting recordings become proposed action items you approve
  • Web + iPhone + Android apps, all in sync; permanent free plan (not a trial)

❌ Cons

  • Extremely new — launched ~19–20 Nov 2026 (Show HN + Product Hunt); no track record, reviews or adoption case studies yet
  • MCP only works with assistants that support MCP connectors; agents without MCP support cannot use it
  • Meeting recording is capped even on Pro (5 hours/month, up to 3 hours each) — heavy meeting users will hit the ceiling
  • AI features send text/audio/image to Microsoft Azure OpenAI (no training on content, but it is still a cloud round-trip, not local)
  • No desktop app and no admin/SSO controls mentioned — it is a small indie product, not an enterprise platform

Frequently asked questions

Is Taskthread free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever — up to 5 boards, unlimited tasks, calendar and day plan, and 3 recorded meetings a month. Paid plans start at Pro, $4.99 a month ($39.99 a year), with a 7-day free trial.

What is Taskthread best for?

A US startup founder running launch work across Claude, ChatGPT and Codex on one board, or an ops team turning every meeting's recording into approved action items instead of re-typing notes.

What should I watch out for with Taskthread?

It launched around 19–20 November 2026, so there is no track record yet — and MCP only works with assistants that support MCP connectors.

Verdict

Best for: People coordinating work across several AI assistants on one shared board — with the warning that it launched around 19–20 November 2026, so there is no track record yet.
