Overview

US social and marketing teams need publish-ready images and short video fast, without a separate design or editing stack. Grok Imagine turns a text prompt or a reference photo into images and 6-15 second clips with native synchronized audio, inside the same Grok/X workflow where their audience already lives — and it can publish straight to X, a distribution shortcut no rival video tool offers. Imagine Image 2.0 (August 2026) has strong instruction-following, readable text inside images, up to 5 reference images, Magic Wand edits and background removal.

Official site: https://grok.com/imagine

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Image 2.0 generation with up to 5 reference images and Magic Wand edits
  • Short video with native synchronized audio (6-15 second clips)
  • First-party publishing straight to X
  • Product-shot, headshot and icon presets with background removal
  • Agent Mode stitches 6-second clips into longer films

✅ Pros

  • One subscription covers images AND short video with native audio — no second tool needed for social creative
  • Imagine Image 2.0 has strong instruction-following, readable text inside images, up to 5 reference images, Magic Wand edits, background removal and product-shot/headshot/icon presets
  • First-party publishing into X (600M+ monthly users) in the same session — unmatched distribution for social teams
  • Imagine Video 1.5 ranks #1 on the public Arena image-to-video leaderboard; Agent Mode stitches 6-second clips into longer films

❌ Cons

  • Video generation only unlocks at Premium ($30 a month) — the $8 Plus plan makes images only
  • Clips cap at ~15 seconds, so it is strictly a short-form tool, not for longer films
  • Pricing has been rebundled repeatedly (SuperGrok became The X Subscription in October 2026) and no plan publishes an image or clip count — usage is an opaque 'pool multiple' of Plus; sign-in is required and the free tier is tightly capped

Frequently asked questions

Is Grok Imagine free?

Yes, with limits. The Free tier costs $0 and includes limited daily image generation — a capped free tier, not a trial. Video generation only unlocks at Premium ($30 a month), and no plan publishes an image or clip count.

What is Grok Imagine best for?

US social and marketing teams that live on X and need fast images plus short, audio-native video in one subscription — social creative, memes and reactive posts published directly to X, plus product shots, headshots and icons without a designer.

What should I watch out for with Grok Imagine?

Video only unlocks at Premium ($30 a month) — the $8 Plus plan makes images only. Clips cap at about 15 seconds, xAI publishes no image or clip count for any plan, and pricing has been rebundled repeatedly (SuperGrok became The X Subscription in October 2026).

Verdict

Best for: US social and marketing teams that live on X and need fast images plus short, audio-native video in one subscription — with the warning that video only unlocks at Premium ($30 a month) and xAI publishes no image or clip count for any plan.
