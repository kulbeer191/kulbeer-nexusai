Pick Lindy AI if you want an AI employee working in Slack and email this week — its ready-made agents and 300+ integrations are the fastest path to automating sales, support, or ops. Pick Dust if you want agents built on your company’s full context with your choice of model and enterprise-grade governance — just budget in euros and watch the credit meter, because the flat per-seat price is only the starting point.
