Overview

Most AI tools can retrieve a message from Slack or a record from a CRM, but they can’t act on the full picture. Dust connects a company’s systems, documents, and tools so custom AI agents can answer with complete context and take real action across teams — with the governance and permissions enterprise IT requires. It turns scattered company knowledge into agents that work across departments, with human review built into shared workflows.

Official site: https://dust.tt/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • 20+ frontier and open-source models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, DeepSeek) — pick the best model per workflow
  • 70+ connectors (Slack, Notion, GitHub, Google Drive, Salesforce, Zendesk) plus MCP servers for any tool
  • Enterprise-grade governance: dual-layer permission model, SSO (Okta, Entra ID, JumpCloud), SCIM, audit logs, US & EU data residency
  • Genuine free tier — 500 credits lifetime, no card needed to start

❌ Cons

  • Credit-based pricing is hard to forecast; heavy agent use burns through the 8,000-credit Pro allowance fast
  • Building good agents takes real setup — connectors, skills, permissions — it’s a platform, not a plug-and-play employee
  • Business plan caps at 100 people and (per its comparison table) 3 connectors and 5 Spaces; real scale needs Enterprise
