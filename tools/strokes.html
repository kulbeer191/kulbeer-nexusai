Overview

Writers who already use AI are stuck copying text between a chatbot and their editor, or handing their files to a cloud app to get AI features. Strokes is a desktop writing app for macOS and Windows that puts an AI assistant directly inside the document: select any passage, describe the change, and GPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok proposes it as a diff you accept or reject. Your documents stay as files on your own computer, the editor works fully offline, and Strokes never trains AI on your writing.

Official site: https://strokes.ink/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • True offline-first design — documents are local files in folders; you write, edit and export with no internet connection (only AI features need one), and the app never trains models on your content
  • In-document AI with diffs, not silent rewrites — the assistant proposes changes you accept or reject, and you pick the model per request (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok)
  • Generous free plan — all writing features, publishing/sharing, cloud backups, Cloud AI and local offline AI, image generation and Deep mode at $0 with no credit card required
  • Broad import/export — Word (.docx), Markdown, plain text and Notion imports; one-click export to PDF, Word, EPUB, Markdown and HTML, plus publishing a document as a public read-only web page

❌ Cons

  • Desktop-only (macOS and Windows) — no web app and no mobile version, so you cannot pick up a draft on your phone or a borrowed computer
  • Very young product — launched October 2026 by a small US startup (1–9 employees), so long-term support, roadmap and ecosystem are unproven
  • The paid tiers mostly buy more AI usage and storage, not new writing features — Plus ($9) and Pro ($29) differ from Free mainly in cloud backup size (5GB / 50GB) and Cloud AI allowance (5x / 15x), which is thin value for light users
