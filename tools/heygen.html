HeyGen Review 2026

Lifelike AI avatars that turn a script into a presenter video in 175+ languages.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Business video — training, onboarding, marketing, and sales outreach — is slow and expensive to produce because every update means re-shooting with a camera, a presenter, and an editor. HeyGen replaces the shoot with lifelike AI avatars: type a script (or import a PPT/PDF), pick an avatar and voice, and get a polished presenter video in minutes, translated into 175+ languages with lip-sync — so teams can publish and update video at the speed of a document edit.

Official site: https://www.heygen.com/

✅ Pros

  • Most lifelike avatars in the category — G2 #1 Most realistic avatars, 4.8/5 from 1,000+ reviews; Avatar V keeps character consistency across angles and expressions
  • Video translation into 175+ languages with tone preservation and lip-sync, plus voice cloning
  • End-to-end workflow: text-to-video Video Agent, image/audio-to-video, PPT/PDF-to-video, and a text-based editor in one platform
  • Trust & safety built in: verified identity for every custom avatar, moderation, annual penetration testing; Forbes AI 50 company

❌ Cons

  • Credit-based pricing — premium features (Avatar IV, lip-sync translation) burn premium credits fast, so Creator's 600 credits go quicker than the headline suggests
  • Free plan is only 1–3 short watermarked videos per month (1-minute max, 720p) with no download — a trial, not a workflow
  • HeyGen states credit costs can vary by account creation date and region, so the price shown in-app is the one that counts
  • Steep jump from Creator ($29/mo) to Business ($149/mo) for teams that need SSO and longer videos
