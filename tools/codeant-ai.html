Overview

Engineering teams drown in manual pull-request reviews while security bugs slip into production, and they usually need three separate tools — a reviewer, a SAST scanner, and a secrets and IaC checker. CodeAnt AI combines AI pull-request review with SAST, secrets detection, and IaC scanning in a single CI/CD pass, with one-click auto-fixes, DORA metrics, and quality gates, so insecure or low-quality code is caught before merge instead of in a separate scan hours later.

Frequently asked questions

Is CodeAnt AI free?

No. CodeAnt AI has no permanent free plan — only a 14-day free trial with 100 PR reviews and all premium features, no credit card required. After the trial it is paid only, starting with AI Code Review Premium at $24 per user per month billed annually.

What is CodeAnt AI best for?

US SaaS teams that want one PR pass to catch security and quality issues before merge — replacing separate review and SAST tools — and startups preparing for SOC 2 that can use its audit reports and CI/CD quality gates as compliance evidence.

What should I watch out for with CodeAnt AI?

Pricing is per-module and has shifted recently, so confirm the current figures on the official pricing page before buying. It is also a young vendor (founded 2023, about 50 customers) next to established rivals like CodeRabbit and SonarQube.
