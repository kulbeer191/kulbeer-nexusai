Lindy AI Review 2026

No-code AI employee for inbox triage, meeting notes, lead follow-ups and CRM updates in Slack and email.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Small teams drown in recurring operational work — inbox triage, meeting notes, lead follow-ups, invoice chasing, and CRM updates. Lindy is a no-code AI employee that lives in Slack, email, text, and your calendar, connects to 3,000+ apps, and takes whole jobs off your plate. You hand it work the way you would a new hire, and it comes back with the work done.

Official site: https://www.lindy.ai/

✅ Pros

  • No-code setup — connect tools in about 2 minutes, no API keys or IT ticket
  • Slack-native with 3,000+ integrations plus MCP support, and persistent workspace context
  • Approvals built in — anything with outside impact waits for your OK before it goes out
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and PIPEDA compliant; data is encrypted and never used to train models

❌ Cons

  • Credit-based pricing makes costs unpredictable — bigger jobs burn 250–2,500 credits each
  • Per-seat billing — anyone who uses Lindy or @mentions it in Slack takes a billed seat
  • Official pricing pages showed different Team figures at time of writing, which is confusing for buyers

Pricing

Lindy's homepage showed Team at $29.99/month (3,000 credits) while lindy.ai/pricing showed $49.99/month (5,000 credits) on the same day. The pricing-page figure is used here — check the official site before buying.
