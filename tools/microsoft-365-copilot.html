Microsoft 365 Copilot Review 2026

AI directly inside Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams and OneNote — grounded in your company's own work data (Work IQ), so staff draft, analyze and catch up without leaving the tools they already use.

Overview

US teams live in Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams but lose hours drafting documents, summarizing meetings, and hunting for information across emails, files, and chats. Copilot puts AI directly inside those apps, grounded in the company's own work data (Work IQ), so staff draft, analyze, and catch up without leaving the tools they already use.

Official site: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • AI inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams and OneNote
  • Work IQ grounding in emails, files and meetings
  • Pre-built agents (Researcher, Analyst, Facilitator) plus Agent Builder
  • Copilot Studio for custom work agents
  • Enterprise-grade security on the Microsoft 365 foundation

✅ Pros

  • Works inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, and OneNote — no new app to learn; grounded in your emails, files, and meetings via Work IQ
  • Pre-built agents (Researcher, Analyst, Facilitator) plus Agent Builder and Copilot Studio for custom work agents
  • Enterprise-grade security and compliance built on the Microsoft 365 foundation; work and personal accounts stay separate
  • Copilot Chat is included at no extra cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans (web-grounded AI chat)

❌ Cons

  • $30 per user per month is an add-on on top of a qualifying Microsoft 365 base plan — the true cost is base plan plus add-on, and there is no free tier for the full experience
  • Value is weak for teams that live in Google Workspace or Slack instead of Microsoft 365
  • Pricing has been restructured repeatedly in 2026, and agent usage is metered, so bills can grow beyond the headline seat price

Verified Pricing

Prices change — always check the official site before buying. Prices below are the widely published plans at time of writing. Prices exclude taxes. Frequently asked questions

Is Microsoft 365 Copilot free?

Partly. Copilot Chat is included at no extra cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans. But the full Microsoft 365 Copilot experience is a $30-per-user-per-month add-on (paid yearly) on top of a qualifying Microsoft 365 base plan — there is no free tier for the full experience.

What is Microsoft 365 Copilot best for?

US teams that already live in Microsoft 365: sales teams drafting follow-ups and summarizing meetings in Outlook and Teams, finance teams preparing budget forecasts in Excel with the Analyst agent, and legal teams drafting and reviewing contracts in Word.

What should I watch out for with Microsoft 365 Copilot?

The true cost is the base plan plus the $30 add-on, and agent usage beyond the license is metered via Copilot Credits, so bills can grow past the headline seat price. Pricing has been restructured repeatedly in 2026 — confirm at checkout.

Verdict

Best for: US teams that already live in Microsoft 365 — sales drafting follow-ups in Outlook and Teams, finance forecasting in Excel, legal drafting contracts in Word — with enterprise-grade security built in.
