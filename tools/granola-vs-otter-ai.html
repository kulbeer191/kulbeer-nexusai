Granola — Pros

  • No bot joins your calls — capture runs on your own device
  • Unlimited meeting notes on the free Basic plan
  • Pre-meeting briefs plus Granola Chat and MCP/API

Granola — Cons

  • Free Basic only shows the last 30 days of notes
  • No audio playback — audio is deleted after transcription
  • Cannot capture a meeting you do not attend

Otter.ai — Pros

  • Lowest paid entry at $8.33/user/mo billed annually
  • Live transcription with real-time captions
  • 300 free minutes/month on the Basic plan

Otter.ai — Cons

  • A bot joins your meetings to record
  • Free plan caps at 300 minutes/month
  • Business tier starts at $19.99/user/mo billed annually
