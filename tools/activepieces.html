Activepieces Review 2026

An open-source automation platform with AI agents, tables and MCP servers in one workspace at a flat $20 a month — one run costs 1 credit however many steps it has, with one-command self-hosting as an escape hatch.

Overview

Small US teams drown in copy-paste work across hundreds of apps but cannot justify per-task automation pricing or enterprise suites. Activepieces gives them open-source flows, AI agents, tables and MCP servers in one workspace at a flat $20 a month — with one-command self-hosting as an escape hatch if they ever want out.

Official site: https://www.activepieces.com

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Open-source flows, AI agents, tables and MCP servers in one workspace
  • Flat 1-credit-per-run pricing however many steps a flow has
  • 770+ integrations, every piece also an MCP server
  • Bring-your-own AI keys on Plus — every AI step costs 1 credit
  • One-command Docker self-hosting (MIT license)

✅ Pros

  • Free forever, no card: 1,000 credits a month, unlimited flows, up to 5 users, Flows/Agents/Tables, MCPs and API access included
  • Flat pricing — one run costs 1 credit however many steps it has, so long multi-step flows stay cheap
  • MIT open source with one-command Docker self-hosting (25,700+ GitHub stars)
  • 770+ integrations, and every piece doubles as an MCP server Claude or Cursor can use
  • Bring-your-own AI keys on Plus drops every AI step to 1 credit and moves model cost to your own provider bill

❌ Cons

  • AI steps cost 2–20 extra credits on vendor keys, so heavy AI use burns credits fast without BYO keys
  • Free-plan credit reset wording is inconsistent on the official page (FAQ says a fresh 1,000 credits every month; the comparison table implies a daily reset)
  • SOC 2 status is disputed: the pricing-page FAQ now says SOC 2 Type 2 audited, but October 2026 reviews said the program was still in progress
  • Summer 2026 brought serious self-hosted security flaws (since reported patched) — self-hosters must keep installs updated; no phone/voice channel

Frequently asked questions

Is Activepieces free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever with no credit card — 1,000 credits a month, unlimited flows, up to 5 users. Paid plans start at Plus, a flat $20 a month ($16 billed yearly, corroborated-only).

What is Activepieces best for?

Small US teams that want open-source automation plus AI agents at a flat price — an ops team automating lead routing from web forms into Slack, a small agency running client automations with BYO keys at a flat rate, or a dev team self-hosting for data residency.

What should I watch out for with Activepieces?

AI steps cost 2–20 extra credits on vendor keys, so heavy AI use burns credits fast without bring-your-own keys. And the free plan's credit reset is worded inconsistently on the official page — the FAQ says monthly, the table implies daily.

Verdict

Best for: Small US teams that want open-source automation plus AI agents at a flat price, with self-hosting as an escape hatch — with the warning that AI-step credit costs are hard to predict on vendor keys, so bring your own keys or budget for overage.
