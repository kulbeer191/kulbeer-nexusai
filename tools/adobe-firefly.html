Adobe Firefly Review 2026

Commercially safe image, video and audio generation for marketing teams, inside Adobe's ecosystem.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Marketing and design teams need on-brand images, video clips, and audio fast, but most AI generators raise licensing questions and force creators to juggle separate apps. Firefly is a single studio for image, video, and audio generation and editing whose own models are trained on licensed and public-domain content, so the output is safe for commercial use — with partner models from Google, OpenAI, Luma, and Runway available in the same workspace.

Official site: https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly.html

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Commercially safe Adobe models — trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public-domain content, with Content Credentials built in
  • All-in-one studio: generate images/video/audio, edit on a timeline, build mood boards, and translate content in 20+ languages
  • Access to top partner models (Google, OpenAI, Luma, Runway) without switching apps
  • Deep integration with Photoshop, Express, and the wider Creative Cloud ecosystem

❌ Cons

  • Video, audio, and partner-model work spends a monthly pool of generative credits, so heavy video use gets expensive
  • Requires an Adobe ID and sign-in even to try the free tier
  • Credit burn rates vary by model and resolution, making the real per-video cost opaque
