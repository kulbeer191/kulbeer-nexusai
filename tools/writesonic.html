Writesonic Review 2026

AI search visibility platform for GEO and AEO, with tracking, writing and outreach in one place.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Marketing teams can no longer win with Google SEO alone — buyers now get answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, and most brands are invisible there. Writesonic is an AI search visibility platform for GEO and AEO that tracks brand mentions, rank, and sentiment across AI search, then closes the gaps with prioritized actions and agent-led outreach. It pairs that tracking with an AI article writer, site audits, and content optimization, so one system covers both SEO and AI search instead of three separate tools.

Official site: https://writesonic.com/

✅ Pros

  • Tracks brand visibility, share of voice, citation share, and sentiment across 8 AI search platforms, then runs the fix — not just a dashboard
  • All-in-one: AI article writer, content optimization, site audits, and AI agents (SEO & Content agent, Outreach Agent) in one platform
  • Trusted at enterprise scale — homepage cites Amazon, Unilever, and NP Digital as customers; 4.8 rating across 4,133 reviews
  • Enterprise-grade compliance: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, SSO/SAML, hosted on Microsoft Azure

❌ Cons

  • Expensive for small teams — cheapest self-serve plan starts at $79/month billed annually, with no permanent free plan (7-day free trial only)
  • Repositioned from AI writer to AI visibility platform in 2026, so older reviews describe a different product and pricing model
  • Quota-based pricing (articles, tracked prompts, audits) makes costs hard to compare with flat-rate writing tools
  • Action Center's full fix-and-execute workflow is only complete on Enterprise; Growth gets only a trial (5 on-page + 5 off-page actions/month)
