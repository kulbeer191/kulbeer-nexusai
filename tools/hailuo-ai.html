Hailuo AI Review 2026

MiniMax’s flagship H3 video generator that turns a text prompt or a single photo into a realistic 4–15 second clip with native sound — plus a huge library of one-click templates that need no prompting at all.

Overview

Social and marketing teams need short, realistic video clips fast — but professional video tools are slow, expensive and demand prompt-engineering skill. Hailuo AI, built on MiniMax’s flagship H3 model, turns a text prompt or a single photo into a 4–15 second clip with native sound, believable physics and realistic people, with a huge library of one-click templates (K-pop music videos, skydive shots, product transformations) that need no prompting at all. It is built for creators who need scroll-stopping vertical video in minutes, not hours.

Official site: https://hailuoai.video/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • MiniMax H3 flagship model (live since 31 July 2026) — 4–15 second clips at up to 2K with native stereo sound, dialogue, effects and ambience in the same pass
  • Best-in-class realism for people and physics among 2026 roundups, at a low per-video cost (about $0.20–$0.38 per 6-second 768p clip on Standard)
  • Enormous one-click template library for photo-to-video — no prompt writing required
  • Failed generations and content that fails moderation are automatically refunded their credits, per MiniMax’s payment policy
