Overview

Small US businesses need a full team — marketing, sales, support, SEO, data — but cannot afford five hires or five subscriptions. Sintra AI bundles 12 named, role-specialized AI employees behind one subscription and one shared Brain AI memory, so a solo founder or lean team can delegate day-to-day business work in plain chat and keep operating around the clock.

Official site: https://sintra.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • 12 role-specialized AI employees under one subscription
  • Brain AI shared memory across every role and task
  • No-code setup; works in chat on web and mobile, 24/7, in 100+ languages
  • 14-day money-back guarantee on all plans
  • 600,000+ accounts and 100M+ prompts processed in 2025 (per vendor)

✅ Pros

  • One subscription covers 12 role-specialized AI employees (Soshie for social media, Seomi for SEO, Milli for sales, Cassie for support, Emmie for email, Dexter for data and more) — no need to buy separate tools per function
  • Brain AI shared memory: the helpers learn the brand voice and business context once and apply it across every role and task
  • Genuinely no-code setup — add brand context, connect tools, set rules; the employees work in chat on web and mobile, 24/7, in 100+ languages
  • Low entry price on the annual plan with a 14-day money-back guarantee on all plans

❌ Cons

  • Hard 250-credit monthly cap shared across all 12 helpers — heavy users hit the wall and the helpers stop working until credits are topped up; credits do not roll over
  • Advertised prices are promotional (50-70% off); regular rates are $97/$59/$52 per month, so the real long-term cost may be much higher than the sale price
  • Trustpilot has flagged Sintra's profile for incentivized reviews — its 8,600+ reviews should be read with caution, not quoted as a trust signal

Frequently asked questions

Is Sintra AI free?

No. Sintra AI has no free plan — only a 3-day free trial with 50 credits and no card required. Paid plans start at $15.60 a month on the 12-month plan ($187.20 a year upfront). Confirm the current trial terms on the official site before signing up.

What is Sintra AI best for?

Solo US founders and lean small teams that want one subscription covering a dozen business roles — Soshie drafts and schedules social posts, Seomi writes SEO blog posts, Emmie runs email win-back flows, Cassie drafts customer replies, and Dexter handles revenue reporting, all from one shared Brain AI memory.

What should I watch out for with Sintra AI?

The shared 250-credit monthly cap is the real limit — heavy use stops all 12 helpers until you top up, and credits do not roll over. Advertised prices are promotional (50-70% off), so the long-term cost is closer to the $52-$97 regular rates.

Verdict

Best for: solo US founders and lean small teams that want one subscription covering a dozen business roles — with the warning that the shared 250-credit monthly cap is the real limit on how much work the team can do.
