Gumloop Review 2026

No-code AI agents that work across 400+ apps, with unlimited seats and guardrails your IT team controls.

Overview

Gumloop is a no-code AI agent platform that lets non-technical US teams build agents that work across their CRM, inbox, docs and 400+ connected apps — researching leads, enriching records and drafting outreach without writing code. Agents run on triggers, schedules or chat, with guardrails, approvals and spend controls so IT can let the whole company automate safely.

Official site: https://gumloop.com

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Unlimited seats, teams and agents on the $37 Pro plan — no per-user billing
  • 35+ models to choose from, plus bring-your-own API keys
  • No-code builder: anyone who understands the task can build the agent
  • Plain-English guardrails, approvals and spend caps on every run

❌ Cons

  • Unused credits do not roll over, and agents stop at the 20,000-credit cap unless pay-as-you-go is enabled
