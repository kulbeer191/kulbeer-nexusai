Krea AI Review 2026

One browser workspace with 150+ image, video and 3D models — real-time generation, upscaling to 22K and LoRA training.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Creative teams juggle a dozen separate subscriptions to reach the best image and video models. Krea AI puts 150+ models — its own Krea 1, 2 and 3 plus Seedance, Veo, Kling, Sora, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image and Flux — into one browser workspace, so US design and marketing teams can generate, edit, upscale and train custom styles with a single subscription and one balance of compute units.

Official site: https://www.krea.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • 150+ image, video, audio and 3D models in one browser workspace
  • Real-time generation canvas that redraws as you type, plus upscaling to 22K
  • LoRA training for custom brand styles, with a commercial license on paid plans
  • Public REST API and MCP endpoint for automation

✅ Pros

  • Prices and promo discounts change often; renewals bill at the non-promo rate
  • Sign-up (Google, Apple or email) required even for the free tier
