Recraft Review 2026

Generate production-ready images and true editable SVG vectors from prompts on an infinite canvas, with reusable brand styles that keep every asset consistent.

Overview

Designers and brand teams need production-ready visuals — logos, icons, illustrations, product photos, mockups — that stay on-brand and editable, not one-off raster images they cannot change. Recraft generates images and true editable SVG vectors from prompts on an infinite canvas, with reusable custom styles and brand palettes, so a team can produce a whole consistent asset set instead of regenerating from scratch every time.

Official site: https://www.recraft.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Native editable SVG vector generation — real paths and anchor points, scalable to any size, a genuine differentiator for logos and icons
  • Consistent styles without training — drop in reference images and Recraft turns them into a reusable, editable brand style
  • Strong prompt understanding (V4.1) plus mockups, background removal, vectorization, and inpainting in one canvas
  • Paid plans unlock third-party frontier models (Nano Banana, Seedream, GPT Image, Flux) and video generation (Kling, Veo, Seedance), plus an API and MCP for pipelines

❌ Cons

  • Free-plan images are public, owned by Recraft, and carry no commercial rights — useless for real client or brand work
  • Generating requires an account (email OTP, Google, Discord, or Apple sign-up; no password option) and users must be 18+
  • Credit costs vary by model and action with no single flat per-image price, so costs are hard to predict before generating
  • Refunds are discretionary and only considered if fewer than 30 credits were used since the last payment; prices exclude VAT/taxes
