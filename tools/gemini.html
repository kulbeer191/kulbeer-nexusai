Gemini Review 2026

Google’s flagship AI inside Gmail, Docs and Drive — drafts, rewrites, summaries and research without leaving Google Workspace, with source-grounded Deep Research and multimodal generation.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US teams who live in Gmail, Docs, and Drive need an AI assistant that drafts, rewrites, summarizes, and researches without leaving Google Workspace. Gemini puts Google’s flagship models directly inside those apps, with source-grounded research (Deep Research, Gemini Notebook) and multimodal generation, so writing and research happen where the work already lives.

Official site: https://gemini.google.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Deep Google Workspace integration — Gemini works inside Gmail, Docs, Vids, and Chrome, plus 5 TB storage on Pro
  • Generous free tier — Deep Research, image generation, Canvas, and Gemini Live included at $0
  • 1M-token context window on Pro for long documents and large file uploads (up to 1,500 pages)
  • Strong multimodal range — video generation (Flow/Veo), Nano Banana image editing, and Gemini Notebook for source-grounded reports
