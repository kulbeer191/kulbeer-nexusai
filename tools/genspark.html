Genspark Review 2026

Super Agent that takes a whole job and returns finished reports, decks, spreadsheets and pages.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Knowledge workers describe a job once and still end up assembling the answer themselves — copying chat output into slides, sheets, and docs. Genspark's Super Agent takes the whole job, plans the steps, browses the web, and routes each step to the best of 30+ AI models and 80+ built-in tools, then hands back finished files: a report, a deck, a spreadsheet, or a working page. It turns a single prompt into a deliverable instead of a conversation.

Official site: https://www.genspark.ai/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Super Agent plans multi-step jobs and delivers finished files (reports, decks, spreadsheets, pages, images, video), not just chat answers
  • Mixture-of-Agents design routes each step to the best model/tool, with a reflection pass to combine the strongest parts
  • All-in-one workspace: AI Chat, Super Agent, AI Slides, AI Sheets, AI Docs, Genspark Code, and Media Studio, plus SecondBrain memory and GenTeam multi-agent workspaces
  • Free plan needs no credit card (100 credits/day) and paid plans include commercial use of generated content
