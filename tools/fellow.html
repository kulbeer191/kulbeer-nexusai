Fellow Review 2026

AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and summarizes Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls into action items.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US teams run their week in meetings but lose the outcomes — decisions and action items scatter across chat threads, and nobody remembers who promised what. Fellow is an AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and summarizes Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls into shared notes with action items, agendas, and searchable recaps, so every meeting ends with clear ownership.

Official site: https://fellow.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Records, transcribes, and summarizes Zoom, Meet, and Teams with a bot, botless via Mac/Windows apps, or in-person capture — with AI summaries and auto-identified action items
  • Ask Fellow AI agent plus MCP connections to ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot, so meeting intelligence flows into prep, follow-ups, and CRMs
  • Collaborative agendas, recurring 1:1s, and org-wide meeting templates keep meetings structured before they start
  • Security-first: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, configurable retention including zero-day, and a strict no-AI-training-on-meeting-data policy

❌ Cons

  • Team caps at 10 AI notes/recordings per user per month, so heavy meeting users must jump to Business ($23/mo monthly)
  • Seat caps per tier (Free 5 seats, Team 20, Business 50) and Enterprise starts at a 10-user minimum

Frequently asked questions

Is Fellow free?

Yes — Fellow has a free plan (Free, $0). The entry paid plan is Solo at $19 / month billed annually ($29 monthly).

What is Fellow best for?

Best for: managers and sales teams that want every meeting to end with clear ownership — agendas, AI notes and action items tracked week to week, with summaries that sync to Salesforce and HubSpot. The free plan’s 5 lifetime AI notes run out fast, so most teams land on Team or Business.
