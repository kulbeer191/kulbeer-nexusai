Which one should you pick?

Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai both record, transcribe and summarize Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls — but they price very differently. Fireflies gives unlimited transcription even on its free plan and is cheaper at the Business tier, with far more storage per seat. Otter's free plan is more generous on AI chat queries and its Pro tier is the cheapest paid entry. This comparison uses both official pricing pages to help US teams pick.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Fireflies.ai or Otter.ai?

Fireflies.ai's entry paid plan is Pro $10/seat/month billed annually; Otter.ai's is Pro $8.33/user/month billed annually. Otter.ai is cheaper at the entry tier.

Which should I choose, Fireflies.ai or Otter.ai?

Pick Fireflies.ai if you record a lot — unlimited transcription on the free plan and 8,000 minutes of storage per seat on Pro beat Otter's 1,200 minutes/month.

What should I watch out for with Fireflies.ai?

Free plan caps at 400 minutes of storage per team

Verdict

Pick Fireflies.ai if you record a lot — unlimited transcription on the free plan and 8,000 minutes of storage per seat on Pro beat Otter's 1,200 minutes/month. Pick Otter.ai if you want the lowest paid entry ($8.33/user/mo annual vs $10/seat/mo) and strong AI chat across past meetings. For heavy meeting teams, Fireflies wins on volume; for light teams, Otter wins on price.
