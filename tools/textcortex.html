TextCortex Review 2026

Overview

Enterprise teams waste hours rewriting the same answers and drafting from scratch because generic chatbots don't know the company's own documents, policies or voice. TextCortex is a model-agnostic enterprise AI platform where teams connect internal knowledge, build no-code AI agents grounded in that knowledge, and deploy them where people already work (Slack, Teams, browser), so drafting, research and internal Q&A all draw on approved company material instead of public-model guesswork.

Official site: https://textcortex.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Model-agnostic Model Hub — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral and open-weight models
  • Knowledge bases with retrieval (RAG) and sourced answers
  • No-code agent builder deployed in Slack, Teams, SharePoint and Outlook
  • EU hosting, GDPR compliance, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 (vendor claims)
  • Browser extension across 30,000+ apps and sites, 25+ languages

✅ Pros

  • Model-agnostic Model Hub — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral and open-weight models in one place, no lock-in to a single provider
  • Knowledge bases with retrieval (RAG) — agents answer from the team's own files and connected sources, with sourced answers
  • EU hosting + GDPR compliance, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified (vendor claims), and customer data is never used for model training — strong for regulated teams
  • No-code agent builder with deployment where teams work — Slack, Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, plus a browser extension across 30,000+ apps and sites; 25+ languages

❌ Cons

  • Premium is $29.99 per user per month with prices EXCLUDING VAT, and usage runs on a token wallet (worth 50% of the licence fee) — heavy users pay extra usage on top of the subscription
  • The product pivoted hard to enterprise agents in 2026 — solo writers and small teams get less attention, and the old cheap writing tiers are gone
  • The Free plan's model usage is limited, so it works more as a guided trial than a usable free tier
  • Enterprise pricing is quote-only — no self-serve team tier between Premium and Enterprise

Frequently asked questions

Is TextCortex free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes knowledge bases, 50 MB file storage, custom agents and deep research with no credit card required — but model usage is limited, so it works more as a guided trial than a usable free tier.

What is TextCortex best for?

Enterprise support and ops teams deploying an internal policy assistant grounded in HR and IT documents, sales teams drafting proposals from approved sales-enablement knowledge bases, and global marketing teams producing multilingual content with EU-hosted, GDPR-compliant AI.

What should I watch out for with TextCortex?

Premium is $29.99 per user per month excluding VAT, and heavy users pay extra token-wallet usage on top of the subscription. The 2026 pivot to enterprise agents means solo writers and small teams get less attention, and there is no self-serve tier between Premium and Enterprise.

Verdict

Best for: enterprise support, ops, sales and global marketing teams that want AI agents grounded in their own documents, policies and voice — deployed in Slack, Teams and the browser.
