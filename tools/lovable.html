Lovable Review 2026

AI full-stack engineer that builds production-grade software from plain language.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Non-technical founders and small teams have product ideas but no engineering capacity, so ideas sit in documents for months. Lovable is an AI full-stack engineer — describe what you want in plain language and it builds production-grade software in real time, handling hosting, authentication, payments, and integrations end to end, so a team can ship a working product in a weekend instead of hiring a dev team.

Official site: https://lovable.dev/

✅ Pros

  • True end-to-end build — hosting, authentication, payments, and integrations handled in one place
  • Genuinely usable free plan (5 daily build credits) for trying real builds before paying
  • Plans are per workspace with unlimited members — cost scales with usage, never headcount
  • Strong founder proof points — customers report retiring $1M+ in SaaS contracts with Lovable-built tools

❌ Cons

  • Credit-based pricing — build costs vary by task complexity, so monthly spend is hard to predict
  • Business costs twice Pro for the same 100 credits — you pay for SSO and governance, not more building
  • Credits are not refundable and monthly credits expire (2 months after issue)
  • AI-generated apps still need human review for security, edge cases, and production hardening
