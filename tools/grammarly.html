Grammarly Review 2026

Writing polish that works everywhere you type, with tone, rewrites and AI prompts.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

What it is

Professionals, students, and teams write in dozens of apps every day, and typos, unclear phrasing, and the wrong tone quietly damage credibility. Grammarly works inside every text box — email, docs, browser, and chat — catching grammar and spelling mistakes, adjusting tone, and rewriting full sentences while keeping the writer's voice. It is the polish layer that makes everyday writing clear and professional without opening a separate app.

Official site: https://www.grammarly.com/

✅ Pros

  • Works everywhere you type — browser, Google Docs, email, desktop, and mobile
  • Genuinely useful free tier: spelling/grammar checks, tone display, and 100 AI prompts/month
  • Pro adds full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustment, on-brand rules, and 2,000 AI prompts/month
  • Strong privacy stance: Grammarly states it does not sell or monetize user content or train models on it; now bundled with Superhuman Go agents

❌ Cons

  • It polishes writing — it is not a content generator for drafting long articles from scratch
  • The headline Pro price ($12/month) requires annual billing; month-to-month is $30/month
  • AI prompts are capped (100/month free, 2,000/month Pro); Enterprise pricing is not published
