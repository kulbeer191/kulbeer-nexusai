Ideogram Review 2026

AI image generation with the most accurate readable text in the picture — posters, logos and ads that come out spelled right.

Overview

Most AI image tools garble text, forcing designers to re-typeset every headline in Photoshop. Ideogram renders accurate, legible text inside the image — headlines, logos, posters and packaging copy come out spelled right — so US marketing and design teams can ship publish-ready visuals with readable type straight from the prompt.

Official site: https://ideogram.ai

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Best-in-class legible text rendering in images — posters, logos and packaging come out spelled right
  • Ideogram 4.0/4.5 models with character consistency, Precise Edit, background removal and Topaz upscaling
  • Private generation, unlimited slow credits and batch generation on paid plans
  • Open-weight Ideogram 4.0 model plus a developer API and MCP support

❌ Cons

  • Free tier is public-only (no private generation) and needs Google/Apple sign-in for weekly credits
  • Unused monthly credits do not roll over
  • Partner models (GPT Image, Nano Banana) are billed separately, not included in plan credits
