Overview

Founders and small teams juggle a calendar, a to-do app, and a notes app that never agree with each other. Routine unifies tasks, calendar, notes, docs, and contacts into one timeline, with an AI assistant, voice commands, time blocking, and AI meeting notes — so the day plans itself around what actually matters.

Official site: https://routine.co/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • One app replaces calendar + tasks + notes + docs: a single timeline view with natural-language input, voice commands, and a universal inbox
  • Strong free plan with real AI — AI assistant and voice commands plus 250 AI credits — and full offline mode
  • AI agents, AI automations, AI meeting notes, and MCP support on the Professional plan; time blocking is built in, not bolted on
  • Cheap entry: $5/month Personal on yearly billing undercuts most rivals
