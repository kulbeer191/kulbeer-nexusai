Overview

US engineering teams are drowning in ticket backlogs and large refactors that autocomplete tools cannot finish. Factory's autonomous coding agents, called Droids, take a whole ticket — plan, write code, run tests, review and open a pull request — across the desktop app, CLI, SDK, web, Slack, Linear, Jira and GitHub, running many tasks in parallel under company-set autonomy controls. Founded 2023 in San Francisco; $200M raised in September 2026 at a $5B valuation; customers include Nvidia, Blackstone, Adobe, T-Mobile, DoorDash and HP.

Official site: https://factory.com/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Autonomous Droids that plan, build, validate, review and open pull requests
  • Model-agnostic across Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI and open-weight models, plus bring-your-own-keys
  • Desktop app, CLI, SDK, web, and cloud/local background agents running in parallel
  • Droid Computers — persistent cloud machines that keep packages, repos and state
  • Admin-set autonomy levels, SSO, SAML/SCIM, zero data retention, audit logs, on-premise deployment

✅ Pros

  • Truly model-agnostic: runs Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI and open-weight models under one rate-limit system, plus bring-your-own-keys — never locked to one lab's model or price
  • Agents work everywhere and in parallel: desktop app, CLI, SDK, web, and cloud/local background agents, with missions, review workflows, and Droid Computers (persistent cloud machines that keep packages, repos and state between sessions)
  • Enterprise-grade governance: admin-set autonomy levels, SSO, SAML/SCIM, zero data retention, audit logs, on-premise deployment, and data residency
  • Built for the whole ticket, not the keystroke: Droids plan, build, validate and review, with usage/readiness dashboards that tie agent adoption to measurable engineering output

❌ Cons

  • No verified free plan — paid plans start at $20 a month. The pricing page offers a bring-your-own-inference option, but third-party reads disagree on whether a $0 tier exists, and no $0 price was visible on the official page
  • Pricing and metering have changed repeatedly during 2026 (monthly token allowances replaced by rolling 5-hour/7-day/30-day rate limits), and per-model multipliers (0.04x-12x) mean the same $20 plan buys vastly different amounts of work depending on the model
  • Steep jump from Pro ($20) to Plus ($100) — about 5x the usage for 5x the price, with no volume discount; the Teams plan caps at 10 seats with no SSO listed, so growing teams are pushed to custom Business pricing fast

Frequently asked questions

Is Factory AI free?

No — there is no verified free plan. Paid plans start at Pro, $20 a month. When standard usage runs out, individual plans fall back to Droid Core (open-weight models) with separate limits, but that is a fallback, not a free plan.

What is Factory AI best for?

US engineering orgs clearing ticket backlogs — Droids pick up whole Linear/Jira tickets in parallel and return reviewed pull requests — platform teams running large-scale refactors, and regulated enterprises that need model choice plus zero data retention and on-premise deployment.

What should I watch out for with Factory AI?

Pricing and metering have changed repeatedly in 2026, and per-model multipliers (0.04x-12x) mean the same $20 plan buys vastly different amounts of work depending on the model. The jump from Pro ($20) to Plus ($100) is steep, and the Teams plan caps at 10 seats.

Verdict

Best for: US engineering teams that want autonomous agents with real model choice and enterprise governance — with the warning that pricing and metering have changed repeatedly in 2026 and the $20 to $100 jump is steep.
