Gemini — Pros

  • Deep Google Workspace integration — Gemini works inside Gmail, Docs, Vids and Chrome, plus 5 TB storage on Pro
  • Generous free tier — Deep Research, image generation, Canvas and Gemini Live included at $0

Gemini — Cons

  • Best models and limits are paywalled — the free tier was moved to Flash-Lite only on Oct 9, 2026, with a 32K context window
  • Pricing restructured repeatedly in 2026 (new Plus tier, Ultra cut from $249.99 to $99.99)

ChatGPT — Pros

  • Broadest range of any general assistant: GPT-5.6 reasoning, deep research, Codex coding, custom GPTs
  • Huge ecosystem of apps, connectors and integrations

ChatGPT — Cons

  • Can be confidently wrong — always verify facts and code
  • Free and Go plans have usage limits, and Go may show ads; Plus has no annual discount

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Gemini or ChatGPT?

Both have a free $0 plan. Gemini’s entry paid plan is Plus at $4.99/mo; ChatGPT’s is Go at $8/mo (Plus $20/mo). Gemini is cheaper at the entry paid tier — and its Pro plan at $19.99/mo undercuts ChatGPT Plus at $20/mo while adding 5 TB storage and YouTube Premium Lite.

Which should I choose, Gemini or ChatGPT?

Pick Gemini if your team already lives in Gmail, Docs and Drive — the assistant is inside the apps where the work happens, with Deep Research grounded in your sources. Pick ChatGPT if you want the broadest general assistant — GPT-5.6 reasoning, deep research, Codex coding and custom GPTs — with the biggest ecosystem of apps and connectors.

What should I watch out for with Gemini?

Best models and limits are paywalled — the free tier moved to Flash-Lite only on 9 Oct 2026 with a 32K context window, and pricing was restructured repeatedly in 2026, so confirm current limits on the official site.

Verdict

Pick Gemini if your team lives in Google Workspace — it is inside Gmail, Docs and Drive, with Deep Research and 5 TB storage on Pro at $19.99/mo. Pick ChatGPT if you want the broadest general assistant — GPT-5.6 reasoning, deep research, Codex coding and custom GPTs — with the biggest ecosystem; start free and move to Plus at $20/mo when you hit limits.
