Clipdrop Review 2026

A browser-based AI image utility kit from Jasper that bundles background removal, object cleanup, upscaling, relighting and text-to-image into one tab — so a non-designer finishes in seconds what used to take a designer an afternoon.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

US marketing and design teams waste hours in Photoshop on repetitive image chores — removing backgrounds, erasing objects, upscaling low-res assets, relighting product shots. Clipdrop bundles those single-purpose AI tools into one browser tab, so a non-designer can finish in seconds what used to take a designer an afternoon, with a free tier that covers everyday volume.

Official site: https://clipdrop.co/

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Background removal, cleanup, relight, uncrop and text-to-image in one browser tab
  • 16x AI upscaler for low-res assets
  • Free HD download for background removal
  • Desktop plugins for Photoshop plus a mobile app
  • API available for embedding (via the Jasper API)

✅ Pros

  • Genuinely useful free tier — 20 uses per day per tool, no credit card required
  • One-click background removal with strong hair/detail handling; free HD download for background removal
  • 16x AI upscaler, relight, uncrop, cleanup and text-to-image in one browser tab — no install
  • Desktop plugins for Photoshop plus a mobile app
  • Pro removes all daily caps (1,000 uses/24h per tool) and skips the processing queue

❌ Cons

  • Free outputs capped at standard resolution (~1024px) and 2x upscaling; high-res needs Pro
  • Login required even for free use
  • No annual discount — Pro is monthly-only at $15
  • Commercial-use and output-ownership terms are not spelled out on the pricing page/terms

Frequently asked questions

Is Clipdrop free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with 20 uses per day per tool and no credit card required. Paid plans start at Pro, $15 a month (monthly only — there is no annual discount).

What is Clipdrop best for?

US marketing and design teams that need fast, free, no-install image edits dozens of times a day — an e-commerce team stripping backgrounds from hundreds of product shots, a social team upscaling and relighting campaign images, or a content team generating quick hero images without a designer.

What should I watch out for with Clipdrop?

Free outputs are standard-resolution only (~1024px, 2x upscaling) — high-res needs Pro. And Pro has no annual discount, so the $15 a month is the price every month.

Verdict

Best for: US marketing and design teams that need fast, free, no-install image edits dozens of times a day — with the warning that free outputs are standard-resolution only and Pro has no annual discount.
