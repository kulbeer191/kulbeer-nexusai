Clearscope Review 2026

Clearscope runs the whole content lifecycle in one workspace: it discovers the questions buyers actually ask Google and AI, grades drafts live against top-ranking pages, and monitors published pages for decay.

Visit Official Site ↗   Feature your tool

Overview

Content teams usually stitch together a keyword tool, a prompt tracker, a writing tool, and a spreadsheet — and still cannot tell whether their pages rank or get cited in AI answers. Clearscope replaces that stack: it discovers the questions buyers ask Google and AI, tracks whether Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude mention your brand for each query, grades drafts live against the top-ranking pages, and flags decaying pages for refresh. For US content-led teams, it is one workspace for search visibility and AI-answer visibility.

Official site: https://www.clearscope.io

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Live content grading (F to A++) against top-ranking pages.
  • AI brand-visibility tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Google and Claude.
  • Topic discovery with search volume, searcher intent and content clustering.
  • Guided AI drafting by searcher intent with tone matching.
  • Content Inventory with Google Search Console integration.

✅ Pros

  • Live content grading (F to A++) against top-ranking pages, with NLP-driven term, heading and question recommendations.
  • AI brand-visibility tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Google and Claude, including query fan-out awareness.
  • Topic discovery with search volume, searcher intent and content clustering.
  • Guided AI drafting by searcher intent, with tone matching, outline control and a "Boost Content Grade" feature.
  • Content Inventory + Google Search Console integration flags decaying pages and striking-distance queries for refresh.
  • Works inside Google Docs, Microsoft Word and WordPress; unlimited users and unlimited projects on every plan.

❌ Cons

  • Highest entry price in its category — $129/mo to start.
  • No free plan, and the trial length is not stated on the official pricing page.
  • Tight monthly caps: only 20 AI Drafts a month even on the $399 Business plan.
  • Not an all-in-one SEO suite: no backlink analysis, no technical site audit, no API outside Enterprise, and no native plagiarism or AI-detection check.

Frequently asked questions

Is Clearscope free?

No — Clearscope has no free plan. Every plan shows a "Start for free" button, which is a free trial; the official pricing page does not state the trial length, so confirm it at signup.

What is Clearscope best for?

Content-led US teams and agencies that want one workspace to discover, write, grade, and monitor search and AI-answer visibility — provided they can justify the $129/month entry price and the 20-draft monthly cap.

What should I watch out for with Clearscope?

The monthly caps: only 20 AI Drafts a month even on Business, and creating a report consumes a credit that cannot be undone (refunds only via support on request). It also has no backlink analysis or technical site audit, so it does not replace a full SEO suite.

Verdict

Best for: Content-led US teams and agencies that want one workspace to discover, write, grade, and monitor search and AI-answer visibility — provided they can justify the $129/month entry price and the 20-draft monthly cap.
