Overview

ChatGPT is a conversational AI assistant. You describe the task in plain language — draft, summarise, explain, debug, plan — and it returns a draft you can edit. It is the reference point most US teams compare other assistants against.

Key Features

The capabilities that matter most for US teams:

  • Strong general writing and reasoning for everyday work
  • Large ecosystem: apps, integrations and plugins
  • Fast to start — free plan needs no card
  • Good coding help for common languages and frameworks
